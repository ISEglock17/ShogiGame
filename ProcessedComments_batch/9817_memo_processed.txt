I've got to strip away all the fluff - the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps, and any other irrelevant details. It's about getting down to the bare bones of the analysis and commentary.

My process will be straightforward: First, I'll meticulously go through each comment, applying the pre-defined rules to identify and remove all the extraneous information. It's a bit like archaeology, sifting through the debris to find the valuable artifacts. Then, with the essential parts remaining, I'll apply my knowledge to re-structure what's left into smooth, coherent sentences. I need the text to read naturally, as if a human analyst had written it. Finally, I'll format the cleaned-up commentary according to the specified output requirements, ensuring consistency and ease of use for whoever is going to use it afterward.
