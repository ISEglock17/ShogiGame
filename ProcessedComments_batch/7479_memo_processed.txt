**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a Shogi game record and the task of cleaning up its commentary. The goal is clear: I need to transform those raw comments into something genuinely useful for analysis. I'm aiming to keep only the insights directly related to the board position and the moves made – no fluff, no extraneous details. This means stripping away all the metadata, biographical info about the players, and any time-related commentary that doesn't contribute to understanding the game's flow. Essentially, I'm streamlining the commentary into a natural, focused explanation of the game's strategic and tactical elements.
