**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with the task of cleaning up some Shogi game commentary. The user, being a fellow expert, clearly understands the nuances here. The goal is to distill the commentary down to its essential core: the actual analysis of the moves and the board state.  Essentially, I need to strip away all the fluff. I'm thinking, this means things like the presenter's intros, biographical details on the players, time stamps...all that extraneous stuff needs to go. My aim is to leave only the valuable insights on the board and the player's choices. The final output should read like a natural, insightful analysis, perfect for someone who already knows their Shogi.
