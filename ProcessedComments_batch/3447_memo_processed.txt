**Streamlining Game Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of Shogi commentary. My goal is clear: strip away all the fluff and noise, leaving only the pure essence of the game's unfolding. I need to get rid of anything that doesn't directly contribute to the analysis of board positions and move explanations. This means anything that's metadata, player introductions, time stamps, or anything unrelated to the immediate tactical considerations is going straight out the window.

Essentially, I'm following a specific set of rules to selectively edit each comment. I'm focusing on preserving the core insights into the strategic thinking and tactical nuances. My goal is to craft the cleanest possible dataset for future analysis of the game's flow and decision-making.
