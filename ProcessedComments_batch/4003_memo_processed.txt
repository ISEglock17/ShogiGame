Essentially, I need to go through each comment and be ruthless in removing anything that doesn't directly relate to the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. That means all the meta-data, introductions, time stamps – *gone*. Those things have their place, but they're not helping me understand the game's unfolding strategic drama.

The real challenge will be creating a clear set of rules to make this process consistent and efficient. It can't be a purely subjective call; I need a system to reliably identify and eliminate those irrelevant elements, leaving behind only the concise, insightful explanations of what's happening on the board and why. I'm after the essence of the game's strategic thinking.
