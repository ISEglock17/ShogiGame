**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so here's the thing. I've got this data set, a bunch of shogi game records, and each move's got these comments attached. Now, the goal is to make these comments much more useful. Right now, they're cluttered with all sorts of things that aren't actually *about* the game itself. There's introductions, mentions of who's playing, the time it took, all this extra fluff. My job is to strip all that away. I need to get down to the core: the actual analysis of the board position and the *reasoning* behind each move. It's about distilling the essence of the game commentary into something clean and focused. It's all about extracting the valuable insights, the explanations of why a move was played, and leaving the extraneous details behind. I have to apply the rules to decide what stays and what goes, and then reshape what's left into natural, readable sentences. Basically, I want to create a dataset that's directly useful for understanding and learning from the games.
