**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw commentary into something useful for in-depth analysis of the game.  I'm basically stripping away all the fluff – you know, the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, any of that extraneous noise – and distilling it down to the core essence. I want the plain-English explanations of the board state and specific move choices. It's about preserving the *strategic* insights, not the pre-game hype or after-the-fact chronology.

The process I'm imagining is fairly algorithmic, I think. First, I'll need to define some concrete rules – a clear set of criteria for what gets the axe and what stays.  I'll review the provided text to figure out what those criteria might be.  Anything not directly related to the *why* and *how* of the moves has got to go. I'll need to develop that list of rules, and then I'm set!
