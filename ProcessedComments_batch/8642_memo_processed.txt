**Processing and Cleaning Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform the existing text into something concise and useful for my analysis. I want to strip out all the extraneous information – the meta commentary, the player introductions, time stamps... everything that isn't directly related to the actual board state and move explanations. I need to leave behind only the truly valuable stuff, the natural language explanations of what's happening on the board and why each move is significant.

I'll be tackling each comment, one at a time, and rigorously applying the provided rules to determine what needs to go and what needs to stay. It's a bit tedious, but the end result will be a much cleaner and more focused dataset for my research. Let's get started.
