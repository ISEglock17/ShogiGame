**Focusing the Analysis: Streamlining Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm looking at a lot of shogi game data, and the comments are a bit of a mess. My goal here is to refine them, to make them really *useful* for analysis. I need to strip out all the fluff – you know, the metadata, the introductions to the players, any time-related details. That stuff just gets in the way. What I *want* is the pure essence: the descriptions of the board positions and the rationale behind each move, presented in a clear, flowing narrative. Essentially, I'm trying to distill the core strategic insights from the noise.
