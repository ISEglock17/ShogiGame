**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to Shogi game data. The goal is straightforward: transform these raw comments into something useful for analyzing the actual gameplay. Essentially, I need to strip away all the extraneous noise - things like metadata, player introductions, timestamps, any information that isn't directly related to the board position or the moves themselves.

My process will be quite meticulous. I'll need to develop a set of clear rules, based on my deep understanding of the game and common commentary styles, to identify precisely what should be kept and what should be discarded. The aim is to leave behind a concise and coherent narrative that focuses solely on the tactical and strategic discussions of the game as it unfolded.
