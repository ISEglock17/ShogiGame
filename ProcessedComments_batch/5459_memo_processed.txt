**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of Shogi game records, and specifically, I need to clean up the commentary associated with each move.  The goal here is pretty straightforward: strip out all the noise and get down to the meat of the analysis.  That means anything that isn't directly related to the board position or the reasoning behind a specific move needs to go.  Think personal introductions, time stamps, meta-commentary about the overall game – that's all irrelevant for my purpose. I just want the core, distilled explanation of what's happening on the board and why a player made a particular choice. I think a clear process should be used in order to achieve this goal, this will streamline the process.
