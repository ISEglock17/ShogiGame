Firstly, I'll need to segment the commentary into individual turns, essentially separating out the thoughts and observations for each move.

Next, I'll scrutinize each of these turn-specific comments, comparing them against the established removal rules. Essentially, I'm filtering out the chaff – the irrelevant, redundant, or otherwise unwanted information – leaving behind the core elements of the analysis. The goal is to produce concise and insightful commentary for each move.
