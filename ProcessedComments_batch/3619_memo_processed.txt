My goal is to surgically remove all the noise: the "Welcome to the broadcast!" preamble, the player introductions, the clock updates, the general chit-chat. All of that can go. I need to keep the actual game analysis - the explanations of the board state, the descriptions of the moves, the insights into the players' strategies - but I want to present it as naturally as possible. No choppy sentences or awkward phrasing. I will make sure the text flows and is easy to understand.
