**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform the raw data, peppered with all sorts of irrelevant noise, into polished, concise explanations focused solely on the board and the moves. Think clean, elegant analysis, easily digestible for anyone who understands the game.

My plan? First, I'll meticulously go through each comment, applying a set of rules – I'll need to define these rules precisely, of course – to identify and *eliminate* all the unnecessary clutter. This means ditching the metadata, the introductions, any time-related details, basically anything that doesn't directly contribute to the *understanding* of the position or the thinking behind a move.

Once that's done, I'll focus on the remaining nuggets of wisdom. I'll need to re-structure the sentences, perhaps combine some, break others apart, and generally finesse the language to create a coherent and flowing narrative that captures the essence of the analysis. It's all about clarity and insightful explanation. I'll need to determine the best approach for this to maintain the original intent, but in a more appealing format.
