**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. What I need to do is clean it up, make it more useful for analysis. Right now, there's a lot of extra "noise" in the comments – things like metadata, introductions to the players, time stamps – you know, all the stuff that's not actually about the moves themselves. 

My goal is to distill each comment down to its essence: the *explanation* of the board position and the move that was played. I want natural language explanations, not just a bunch of disjointed phrases. I need to get rid of anything that's irrelevant to understanding the game's flow and the players' reasoning. I need a clear, concise narrative of the game, move by move. It is a cleaning operation to yield a format I can work with.
