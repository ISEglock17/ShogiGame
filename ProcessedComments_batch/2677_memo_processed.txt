**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and the goal is clear: to sculpt the text into something usable for my analysis. Essentially, I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – and leave only the core essence: clear, natural explanations of the board state and the moves being made. I want the commentary to *speak* directly to the key strategic points without any distractions. This clean version will be much more efficient for my processing.
