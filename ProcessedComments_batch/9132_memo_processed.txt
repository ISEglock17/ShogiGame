I want to strip out all the fluff – the metadata about the players, the time stamps, the extraneous introductions – and just keep the good stuff. I'm after the core insights: the explanations of the board state, the justifications for each move. I'm aiming for concise, well-structured sentences that will be valuable for detailed Shogi analysis, not just casual reading. It's about distilling the essence of the commentary, making it a powerful resource for deeper study.
