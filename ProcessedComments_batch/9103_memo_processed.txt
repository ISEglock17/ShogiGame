**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a Shogi game dataset that's loaded with commentary, and the goal is clear: I need to clean it up. The aim is to create a focused, high-quality dataset of the game's actual analysis, which means stripping out all the noise. I'm talking about the meta-details, the player introductions, the time stamps – all that fluff that doesn't contribute to understanding the board state or the moves themselves. My objective is to distill the essence of the game's evolution into a series of flowing, insightful sentences focused on the moves and their impact.
