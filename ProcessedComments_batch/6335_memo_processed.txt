**Cleaning Up Those Pesky Comments**

Alright, so I'm tackling this dataset of shogi game comments. The user wants me to whip these things into shape, and I totally get it. It's a mess of good analysis mixed with a bunch of fluff - you know, the usual suspects: metadata, introductions to players, clock readings... all that stuff that's useful contextually but gets in the way of a clean analysis of the moves themselves. My goal is to extract the juicy bits – the actual board explanations and the reasoning behind each move – and present them as coherent, natural sentences. Basically, I'm aiming to transform these comments from a jumble of info into clear, concise, and valuable commentary that focuses on the shogi strategy. This seems like a straightforward cleaning task, but I'm looking forward to applying my expertise to identify and preserve the best of the data.
