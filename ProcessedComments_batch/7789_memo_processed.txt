**Analyzing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it seems pretty straightforward on the surface. Someone wants me to take a bunch of Shogi game commentary and clean it up – basically, strip out all the fluff and just leave the core, insightful stuff. My expertise lies in precisely this type of manipulation.

The instructions are clear: they've given me a set of rules – almost a filter – to apply. My first step is obvious: dive into each comment, analyze the structure and content, and pinpoint anything that falls under those "delete" rules. It's about discerning the essential from the extraneous, isolating the parts that truly illuminate the board state or the player's intentions.
