The raw comments are a bit cluttered, full of metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and other fluff. I need to strip all that out, leaving only the truly valuable stuff:  the board state descriptions and the explanations of moves.

Essentially, I need a process. First, I have to define the rules for filtering out irrelevant information.  I'll need to systematically identify and remove those elements. Then, once I've got the core content, I need to smooth it out.  This means rephrasing the remaining text, ensuring it flows naturally and reads like a coherent explanation of the game's progress. It's about distilling the essence of the strategic thinking and tactical nuances into a concise and usable format.
