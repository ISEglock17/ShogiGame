Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I've dealt with countless times, you know? It's all about sifting through the noise in Shogi game commentary. The user, who likely understands the intricacies of the game, wants me to streamline those often verbose and rambling comments found within the game data.

My goal here is pretty clear: I need to focus the commentary down to its essence. I want to keep only the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the player's moves, transforming it into a concise, logical flow of thought. I'm essentially removing all the fluff - the meta-commentary, biographical information about the players, timestamps, all that stuff. It's about distilling the raw tactical or strategic insights into a digestible format.
