**Streamlining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this shogi commentary data. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to whip these comments into shape, stripping away all the fluff so I'm left with pure, actionable insights for my analysis. Think of it like a really in-depth data cleanse, but with a specific focus on preserving the essential strategic narrative of each move.

This isn't just about deleting randomly, though. There's a well-defined set of rules to follow – a sort of "data sanitation protocol," if you will. Essentially, I'm surgically removing anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's evolution on the board.

The key targets are these:

*   **Rule 1:** Gotta axe any mention of people who *aren't* actively playing the game. This means no more side-commentary about their ratings, affiliations, or mentors.
*   **Rule 2:** References to previous games, historical precedents, or past events are also out. I'm focusing on the *now*, not the then.
*   **Rule 3:** Time stamps and clock information are irrelevant. The flow of the game, not the speed, is what matters.
*   **Rule 4:** Personal details about the players, their records, or any affiliations are also off-limits.
*   **Rule 5:** I'm not interested in general style descriptions.
*   **Rule 6:** Venue details are superfluous.
*   **Rule 7:** No interruptions for food or other non-game events.
*   **Rule 8:**  And, finally, any metadata related to broadcasts, URLs, or any external information that isn't directly related to the moves themselves gets the boot.

By meticulously applying these rules, I aim to transform these raw comments into a clean, focused dataset that's ready for some serious shogi analysis.
