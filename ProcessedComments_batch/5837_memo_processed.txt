**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm looking at a bunch of comments from shogi game data, and the goal is to make them more useful. Right now, they're cluttered with all sorts of stuff that I don't really need for analysis - meta information about the game, introductions to the players, time stamps... the works. It's like sifting through a mountain of chaff to find a few grains of wheat.

What I *really* want is the core essence: the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a natural, flowing way. Basically, I want to strip away all the noise and get to the heart of the commentary. That'll make my analysis a whole lot cleaner and more efficient. So, time to start thinking about the best way to do this...
