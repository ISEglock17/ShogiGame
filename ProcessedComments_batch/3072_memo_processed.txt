It's about preserving the *meaning*, not just the words. Finally, I'll need to structure the output in a specific format – likely one that's designed for seamless integration with Shogi analysis tools. This is where precision and consistency really matter.

Basically, I'm turning raw commentary into pure, digestible knowledge.
