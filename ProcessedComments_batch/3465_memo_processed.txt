**Processing Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of Shogi game commentary, and the goal is to clean it up. The user, who I assume knows a thing or two about Shogi, wants me to trim the fat and keep only the essential, the analysis itself. It's about presenting a clear, concise breakdown of the game's evolution.

Here's my thought process on how to tackle this:

First, I need to understand the rules of what to *exclude*. I'll analyze each comment chunk, looking for those elements that don't contribute to the core analysis of the board position and moves. Things like extraneous greetings, personal opinions, or irrelevant details have to go.

Next, it's about surgery. I'll meticulously remove those identified parts, leaving behind the valuable nuggets of insight – the description of the board state, the player's intentions, and the rationale behind each move.

Finally, I'll need to organize the information according to the user's desired format. This will involve clearly stating the move number, the cleaned-up commentary, and a brief note on *why* I removed specific parts. This should provide the user with a readily digestible, high-level view of the game's development. My focus is on delivering a product which is both insightful and easy to parse, reflecting the intricate dance of Shogi strategy.
