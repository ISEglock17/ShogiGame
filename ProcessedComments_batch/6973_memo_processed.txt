**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of comments from Shogi matches, and my goal is to refine them. I need to make them more useful for analyzing the actual play. It's all about streamlining the commentary.

Essentially, I want to eliminate all the extraneous noise – that metadata about the game, introductions to the players, or time-related details. The goal is to isolate the pure gold: the insightful explanations of the board state and the individual moves, expressed in natural, flowing language. I need to get rid of anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the strategy and tactics.
