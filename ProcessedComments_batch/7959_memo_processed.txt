**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of shogi game commentary, and the goal is pretty straightforward: I need to refine the comments associated with each move. Basically, I want to strip away all the extraneous noise and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the game itself – the actual board states and move explanations. Think of it like a distilled version, perfect for detailed棋譜解析 (kifu analysis).

The approach is methodical, of course. I've got this set of rules to guide the process, and I'll apply them sequentially to each comment tied to a move.

**Here's the checklist:**

*   **Rule #1: Persona Non Grata:** Any mentions of individuals *not* directly involved in the match are out – that includes their names, ranks, or details about their teacher-student relationships (門下). No extraneous characters.
*   **Rule #2: History Lesson Detour:** References to previous games, past strategies, or any historical context are unnecessary for my goal.
*   **Rule #3: Time Traveler's Ban:** Time-related data needs to go. We're interested in the *position* and the *move*, not the clock.
*   **Rule #4: Personal Dossiers Delete:** Detailed biographies, rankings, player stats, any of those personal details are not needed.
*   **Rule #5: Style Over Substance:** General discussions of playing style or preferred game openings (戦型) are irrelevant to my needs.

This methodical cleaning will give me the refined data that I am looking for.
