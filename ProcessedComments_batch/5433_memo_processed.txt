**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Game Data for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so the goal here is pretty clear. I've got this trove of Shogi game data, complete with all sorts of commentary attached to the moves. But to really sink my teeth into a deep analysis, I need to strip away the fluff. All that biographical detail on the players, the time stamps, the general chit-chat - it's just noise. My focus needs to be laser-sharp on the *actual* Shogi strategies.

What I'm envisioning is a streamlined commentary, focusing solely on the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Think of it as a bare-bones, elegant explanation of the game's evolution, shorn of everything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the positional advantages and disadvantages. This will allow for far more effective data mining and pattern recognition when I start crunching the numbers.
