**Cleaning Up Those Pesky Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I've got this user, who's clearly deep into Shogi. They want to take a mountain of comments from game data and turn them into something actually *useful*.  Basically, they want me to filter out all the noise and get to the good stuff.  I get it -  all that meta-chatter, the player introductions, and the timestamp data...it's just cluttering up the real analysis.

So, the plan is straightforward, which is good because I love elegant solutions. First, I need to define the "rules of engagement," so to speak. What's allowed, and what *absolutely* gets the boot? Then, I go through each comment like a hawk, deleting anything that falls outside those rules. It's like archaeological excavation, only instead of ancient artifacts, I'm unearthing the core insights about the board position and the actual moves.

Once I've stripped away the fluff, I need to polish up what's left. It's not just about deleting; it's about making sure the remaining bits and pieces flow like a coherent sentence or two. That's where the real art comes in. I want the analysis to be natural, easy to follow, and focused on the moves and the thinking behind them.
