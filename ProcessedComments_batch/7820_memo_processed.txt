**Cleaning Up the Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary from Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the noise and keep only the really important stuff. That means stripping out any meta-information, introductions to the players, time stamps – anything that isn't directly related to the board state or the moves being played. I want to end up with a smooth, natural-sounding narrative that focuses solely on the analysis of the game.

My plan is to figure out the best way to do this systematically.
