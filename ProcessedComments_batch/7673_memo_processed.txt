**Focusing the Lens: Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

I need to take these often-cluttered annotations and transform them into something truly useful for analysis. The goal is to isolate the pure essence of the commentary: the explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. Everything else – the fluff, the introductions, the timing details – has got to go.

Here's my thought process: First, I'll dive into each comment and apply a set of rules. These rules will act as a filter, allowing me to identify and excise the irrelevant information. Once I've stripped away the noise, I'll move on to the core of the challenge: crafting those raw fragments into clear, natural-sounding sentences. Finally, I'll package everything up in the required output format, ensuring it aligns with the desired structure. This is all about precision and focus, ensuring the data is primed for deep analysis.
