**Streamlining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm being tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and everything that isn't directly relevant to the actual board position and move-by-move analysis. My objective is to leave behind a smooth, coherent narrative that focuses solely on the game itself.

I'm working with a specific set of rules to guide this process, so I'll be meticulously examining each comment, one by one. Here's what's on my chopping block:

*   **No Names, Ranks, or Affiliations of Non-Players:** Any mention of commentators, analysts, or anyone other than the two players is out.
*   **Historical Detours are Forbidden:** References to previous games, specific prior moves, or any external context are to be removed.
*   **Time is of the Essence, but Not in the Commentary:** Time-related data, like clock readings or time controls, are completely irrelevant and will be eliminated.
*   **Player Bios Get the Axe:** All player profiles, records, ranks, and any identification details are also being removed.
*   **Style Matters, but Not the Player's:** Descriptions of player's style, preferred game types, or similar analysis of their preferences are of no concern.
*   **Environment is Irrelevant:** Descriptions of the venue, weather, or location are being scrubbed.

I'm ready to dive in and get this commentary ready for some serious Shogi analysis. Let's get to work!
