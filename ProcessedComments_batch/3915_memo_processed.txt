**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of Shogi game data, specifically the commentary attached to each move. My job, it seems, is to refine these comments. I need to take the raw, often messy commentary and sculpt it into something useful for serious analysis.

My goal is clear: I want to strip away all the fluff. That means the metadata, the introductions of players, the time checks...anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a particular move has to go. Essentially, I'm aiming for concise, clear explanations of *why* a move was played, focusing on the immediate tactical and strategic considerations.

The process itself is straightforward, conceptually at least. First, I need to apply a set of rules – my expertise should guide me here – to identify and remove all the extraneous information. Then, with the essential content remaining, it's about re-writing the comments to create a flowing, readable narrative. This isn't just a simple deletion; it's a re-construction to ensure clarity and coherence.
