**Structuring and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with refining these Shogi game commentaries, transforming them into something more elegant and insightful.  The goal is clear: remove all the fluff – the extraneous details that don't contribute to understanding the flow of the game – and distill the commentary down to its essential elements. We need to leave behind a clean, natural-sounding narrative that focuses solely on the key moves and the strategic context.

My process will involve meticulously analyzing each move's accompanying commentary. I'll need to identify the core aspects of the move being discussed: the piece moved, its origin and destination, and crucially, any tactical or strategic significance the move holds.  I'll be discarding anything that seems like filler or redundant information, prioritizing the clear presentation of the board state and the implications of each player's action.
