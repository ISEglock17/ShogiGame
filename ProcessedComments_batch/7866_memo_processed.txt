**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I've got a challenge here. Someone wants me to refine a whole bunch of comments from Shogi games. The goal? To take this raw data, this messy collection of notes, and transform it into something truly valuable for analysis. I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock information – all the things that aren't *directly* about the board or the moves themselves. My mission is to isolate those key insights, those explanations of strategy and tactics, and present them as clear, coherent sentences.

Basically, I'm the editor, the one who knows what's critical and what's extraneous. I'll be working through each comment systematically, carefully applying the given rules. First, I'll filter out the noise, getting rid of anything that doesn't contribute to a deeper understanding of the game. Then, I'll finesse what's left, making sure the remaining commentary reads smoothly and naturally. This is about making the data sing, transforming it from a list of fragmented observations into a flowing narrative of the match's most important moments.
