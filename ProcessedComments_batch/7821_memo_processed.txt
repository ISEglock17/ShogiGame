**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my goal is clear: I need to clean it up for analysis. It's cluttered with irrelevant noise – the usual meta-data, player introductions, time stamps – you know, all the stuff that's crucial for human understanding of the context but ultimately hinders my ability to get at the *real* analytical value, the insights buried within the game itself.  My task is to isolate and elegantly present the essential commentary – the explanations of the board positions and the rationale behind each move.  I want what's left to read like natural, flowing explanations of the game's evolution, nothing more, nothing less.
