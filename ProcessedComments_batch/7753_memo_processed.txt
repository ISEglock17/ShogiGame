**Processing and Refactoring Shogi Commentaries**

Okay, I've got it. The user wants me to clean up and restructure the comments associated with each move in a Shogi game dataset. Essentially, they're providing raw commentaries and want me to transform them into concise, focused explanations of the board state and the move itself. The goal is to eliminate all the extraneous noise – things like metadata, player introductions, time information, and anything else irrelevant to the direct analysis of the position and the play.

My strategy is clear: first, I need to methodically identify and remove the unwanted information based on a predefined set of rules. This is the crucial stage where I'll apply my understanding of Shogi commentary conventions to distinguish what's core analysis and what's peripheral. Once that's done, I'll focus on crafting the remaining content into grammatically correct and naturally flowing sentences.

The output needs to be structured precisely. Each move will start with a line formatted as "手数<N>: <整形済みのコメント>", followed by a breakdown of the deletions. This "削除理由:" section will clearly list the specific phrases or elements I removed and the corresponding rule number that justified their removal. This rigorous structure ensures both clarity and traceability, allowing for verification of the processing steps. It's a systematic approach, but the goal is to make the resulting commentaries much more immediately useful for analysis.
