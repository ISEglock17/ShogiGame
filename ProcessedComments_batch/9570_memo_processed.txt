The goal is clear: I need to transform those raw comments into something really useful, cutting out all the fluff. I'm talking about removing all the unnecessary stuff – the meta information, the bios of the players, the time stamps... all of that extraneous noise. What I want to retain, and what I *need* to retain, are the actual insights. I need to keep the valuable explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Ultimately, the objective is to have a cohesive narrative that makes the game come alive.
