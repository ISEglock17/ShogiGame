My process will be methodical, I think. I'll need to go through each comment, one by one, and apply the rules I've been given to filter out the irrelevant parts. The challenge, I suppose, will be in recognizing and separating the useful commentary from the extraneous information, ensuring that what remains flows naturally as a description of the game's evolution. It's about data integrity and extracting true analytical value.
