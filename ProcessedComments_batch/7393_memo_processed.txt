**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this mountain of commentary attached to these Shogi game records. My goal here is to sift through all the noise and extract the truly valuable insights. It's like panning for gold, but instead of dirt, I'm dealing with extraneous text. The goal is to isolate and streamline the explanations of the board state and the moves themselves, removing any fluff that's just cluttering things up. This means ditching the introductory fluff, the player introductions, and the time stamps – all the stuff that's useful in a live broadcast but gets in the way of serious analysis. The real value is the "why" behind the moves, and that's what I'm after.
