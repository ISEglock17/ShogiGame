**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: I need to refine these comments, trimming away the excess fluff and getting to the core of the analysis. It's about taking that raw commentary data and making it useful for real analysis, not just a casual read.

My process will be methodical.  First, I'll establish clear rules for what constitutes noise. Things like player introductions, time stamps, and any general meta-commentary need to go. My expertise lets me see right through that extraneous stuff, allowing me to focus on the essential aspects. I'll be looking for direct explanations of the board position and specific moves - the strategic meat of the discussion.  Then, I'll hone in on the moves that provide insight for my project.
