**Analyzing and Refactoring Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, I've got a new challenge. Someone wants me to refine Shogi game commentary, cleaning it up and making it more focused. The goal is to strip away all the extraneous stuff – the fluff, the irrelevant details – and distill it down to its essence: the board state and the critical moves, explained in clear, natural language. It's about preserving the game's flow and insights, nothing more.

So, here's my plan of attack: First, I need to methodically go through each comment, move by move. This means diving deep into the structure, understanding the context, and identifying what's *essential* for a clear understanding of the evolving game.
