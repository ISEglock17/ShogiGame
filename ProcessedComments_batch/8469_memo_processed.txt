**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Game Analysis**

Okay, so I need to take a look at these Shogi game commentaries and essentially give them a makeover. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I want to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta stuff, the introductions to the players, the time stamps – and get down to the real meat of the analysis. My focus is purely on the board state and the move explanations. I'm aiming for a clean, natural flow of commentary that highlights the strategic thinking, not the peripheral details. Basically, I'm pruning the commentary down to its essence for better analysis.
