**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one: a user wants me to process and refine commentary from shogi game records.  The goal here is precise. We're not just archiving; we're preparing data for deep, meaningful analysis.  The raw commentary often includes a lot of noise - metadata, introductions, timestamps, fluff about the players - things that, while interesting for a casual read-through, just clutter the signal when you're trying to extract strategic insights.

My aim is to distill the commentary down to its essence: the *meaningful* descriptions of board states and move explanations.  I need to preserve the natural flow of language, making the remaining text feel coherent and insightful even after the removal of that initial, context-setting information.  Essentially, I'm cleaning up for *machine consumption*, but also trying to preserve the human element in a focused way.
