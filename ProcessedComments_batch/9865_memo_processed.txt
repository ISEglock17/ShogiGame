**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm taking a look at this task of cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries. Basically, someone wants to take the raw commentary data – you know, the stuff that comes with each move – and strip out all the fluff. I get it. We want to get down to the meat of the analysis, the actual insights about the board state and the player's choices. 

This means ditching all the irrelevant stuff: the metadata, the introductions to the players, the time stamps... all the noise that clutters up the text. My goal is to reshape these comments into something clean and focused, where the only thing that remains is the core strategic commentary. We're talking about restructuring those observations into flowing sentences that will then be valuable for further analysis and understanding.  Essentially, I'm aiming to convert the disorganized stream of consciousness into a streamlined analysis.
