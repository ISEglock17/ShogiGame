So, I'm thinking my approach will be pretty straightforward. I'll need to develop a set of rules – almost like a filter – to identify the stuff that needs to go. Anything that's not directly explaining the current position or the rationale behind a specific move gets the boot. It's all about distilling the essence of the game's commentary, removing the fluff, and leaving behind the pure, distilled insights into the strategic thinking behind each move. This should be a fun challenge, it's about getting to the heart of what makes the match data really interesting!
