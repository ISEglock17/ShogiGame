**Analyzing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and the goal is clear: clean it up for analysis. I need to focus, keep this laser-sharp. The user, they're expecting a polished, streamlined piece of work.  No fluff, just the strategic meat.

My process starts with a methodical approach. I have to go move-by-move through the commentary. The key is to filter. I need to identify the crucial information: the board positions and the actual moves made. All the extraneous chatter, the filler words, the subjective opinions that don't directly contribute to understanding the game's evolution - that's got to go. I need to extract the essence of the game and present it in a digestible and insightful way. Essentially, I'm peeling back layers to get to the core of the strategic decisions that defined the game.
