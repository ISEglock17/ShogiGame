**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game records, and specifically the commentary that goes along with each move. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the fluff and extraneous information to get down to the bare bones of insightful analysis. Think of it like this: I want to distill the raw essence of each move's rationale, leaving behind only the pure, unadulterated explanation of what's happening on the board and why the player made that specific decision.

This means getting rid of all the noise. Stuff like metadata about the game itself, introductions or descriptions of the players (I already know that!), time stamps for each move – that's all irrelevant for my purposes. What I *do* need are the actual explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each individual hand-played move. I need it to flow like natural language. The challenge is ensuring these explanations are complete, and that they are not verbose. It needs to provide just the right level of information to a Shogi expert.
