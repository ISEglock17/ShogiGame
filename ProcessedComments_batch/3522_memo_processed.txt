**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine these comments, making them much more useful for in-depth game analysis. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, player introductions, timestamps, all that stuff. It's distracting and doesn't directly contribute to understanding the actual flow of the game.

What I want is a distilled version. I want the essence: the comments that directly explain the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. I need the analysis to be concise and flow naturally, like a human expert would explain it. So, basically, I'm focusing on the "why" and "what" of each position and move. It's about getting rid of the fluff and revealing the core strategic insights embedded within these annotations. Now, to do this effectively, I'll need to figure out the right cleaning steps.
