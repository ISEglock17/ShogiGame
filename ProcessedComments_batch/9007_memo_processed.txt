The goal is clear: strip out all the fluff and leave behind the pure, distilled essence of the board and the moves. Think of it like a good editing job – removing the unnecessary bits and leaving behind the core narrative.

This is my approach: First, I'll define a set of rules – a kind of linguistic garbage disposal system, if you will. These rules will act as my guide, helping me spot and delete the information that doesn't belong. I'm talking about the metadata that clogs up the works, the intros to players we don't need, and the pesky time stamps. Then, with this tool in hand, I can automatically apply it to the game data, transforming it from a mess into something readable and insightful.
