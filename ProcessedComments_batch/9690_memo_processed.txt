**Focusing the Signal, Drowning Out the Noise**

Okay, so I've got this dataset of shogi game records, and the user wants me to clean up the commentary. It's a pretty straightforward request, really. The core idea is to extract the *good* stuff – the insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves – and throw out the chaff.

I mean, who needs introductions, timestamps, or irrelevant meta-data cluttering up the analysis? My goal is to transform those comments into a seamless narrative that only highlights the strategic reasoning behind each move, preserving the flow and readability for focused study. It's about distilling the essence of the analysis, the raw intellectual power, and leaving a clean, coherent commentary stream for the user to work with.
