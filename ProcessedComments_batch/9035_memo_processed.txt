**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to whip this commentary into shape, eh? It's all about making it lean and mean, perfect for a deep dive into the game. I get it. The goal is to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock updates – and leave behind only the pure, distilled essence: the board's story and the clever (or not so clever!) moves themselves.

So, I'm going to apply these filters: anything that introduces players outside the specific match – their names, ranks, affiliations – that's gotta go. Similarly, any references to precedent or past games, even if they're insightful, are getting the boot. We're keeping our eye on the prize: the *current* game and understanding what's happening on the board.
