**Focusing My Lens: Streamlining Game Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's quite clear what I need to do. I've got a set of comments from shogi game data, and the goal is to refine them. I need to strip away the fluff – the irrelevant noise – and keep only the insightful commentary about the board state and the moves themselves. Think of it like a focused beam of light, illuminating the critical aspects for deeper analysis.

My thinking is, I'll process each comment based on a set of rules. I'll need to be meticulous, since this is all about preserving the true essence of the game play.

The rules are pretty clear:

1.  Absolutely no names, ranks, or affiliations of people *not* involved in the game. It’s about the board, not the background chatter.
2.  Any reference to previous games or precedents? Gone. We're interested in *this* game, in its own context.
3.  Time information? That's right out. Time pressure and clock management, while interesting to players, aren't the heart of the analytical insight I'm after.
