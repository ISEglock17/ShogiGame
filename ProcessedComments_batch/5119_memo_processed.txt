**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Alright, I'm tasked with a pretty common problem in my field: sifting through the noise in game commentary to get at the *real* insights. I've got a dataset of Shogi game comments, and the goal is to clean them up, making them perfect for further analysis of the game's evolution.  

Essentially, I need to strip away all the fluff – you know, the meta-commentary, introductions to players, clock readings, even pleasantries – and leave behind only the pure, hard-hitting analysis of the board and the specific moves being played. I want clean, concise explanations of the positions, strategies, and tactical considerations. It's about distilling the raw tactical and strategic thought from the context.
