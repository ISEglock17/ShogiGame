What I really need are the *meat* of the commentary: the explanations of the board state, the justifications for each move, the strategic reasoning. Basically, I want the core insights presented as natural, coherent sentences, ready for my expert eyes to digest and interpret. I need to surgically remove the irrelevant bits and leave only the pure chess-thinking gold.
