**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game commentary, and the goal is clear: strip away all the fluff. I need to get rid of the extraneous noise – the metadata, the introductions, the clock information – and boil it down to the pure essence of the game. I want to keep only the insightful explanations of the board state and the moves, presented in a way that reads smoothly and naturally. It's about focusing on the game itself, not the window dressing. This will make the data much more useful for in-depth analysis. Now, how to best achieve that...
