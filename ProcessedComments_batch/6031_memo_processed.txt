**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I've seen countless times in my work: refining raw data for meaningful analysis. Here, it's about taking commentary from Shogi game records and transforming it into something useful for serious research. The raw stuff is always a mess, full of noise that just muddies the waters.

The goal is crystal clear: I need to strip away all the extraneous fluff – the metadata, the introductions of the players, those endless time checks – and isolate the *actual* analysis of the game. I want to be left with clean, natural-sounding explanations of the board state and the player's moves, the insights that truly matter. This isn't just about cleaning up the aesthetic; it's about creating a dataset that's ready for deep learning, statistical modeling, and all the advanced techniques I use. I need these comments to be in a form that is digestible, so the important parts can be distilled down into digestible, meaningful data.
