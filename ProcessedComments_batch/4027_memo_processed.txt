**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one, actually: cleaning up shogi game commentary data. The user, I know, needs this for in-depth analysis of the game, likely for something like building a more robust AI or perhaps just for detailed study.  My objective is crystal clear: to strip away all the extraneous noise – the metadata, the introductions, the clock timings – all the fluff that distracts from the *real* meat of the commentary: the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.

I'm essentially becoming a digital editor, but with a specific set of rules. I have to parse each comment, dissect it, and determine what stays and what goes based on these predefined guidelines. It's about preserving the *essence* of the analysis while eliminating anything that's irrelevant to the positional understanding of the game.  It's a matter of crafting a streamlined, focused narrative of the play.
