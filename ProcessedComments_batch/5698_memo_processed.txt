Think of it like this, I want to strip away all the extraneous noise – all the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps – you know, the stuff that's not directly related to the actual game's flow and insights.

My aim is to distill these comments down to their essence. I want only the pure, elegant explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a way that feels natural, a smooth narrative of the game itself. No fluff, just the good stuff that helps dissect the strategies. I need a process to clean these up.
