**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the player intros, the timestamps – and leave only the good stuff, the actual analysis of the board positions and the moves made. I need to make these comments as concise and readable as possible.

The core of my process is pretty straightforward. First, I have to establish a solid set of rules to identify what's irrelevant and what's valuable. This involves recognizing patterns in the comments that I can programmatically detect and remove. Then, I can filter out the unnecessary information and focus on retaining the helpful explanations and strategic insights. It's all about distilling the essence of the game commentary into something that's useful for deeper analysis, and this starts with a strong understanding of what's *not* necessary.
