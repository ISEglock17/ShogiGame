**Structuring Game Commentary for Clarity**

Okay, so I'm presented with a block of Japanese commentary on a Shogi game, and my task is to refine it.  The goal is to distill the essence of the game for an expert audience, stripping away all the fluff that's not strictly related to the board position or the moves themselves.  Think of it like curating a museum exhibit – only the most relevant pieces are on display.

My process will be methodical.  I need to go through each comment, line by line, and judge it against a set of established rules.  Anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the state of the game at any given moment, or the *reasoning* behind a specific move, is out.  This includes things like player introductions, biographical information, speculative predictions, or anything that veers into unnecessary tangential discussions.  Essentially, I'm aiming for a lean, insightful narrative that a Shogi master would appreciate.
