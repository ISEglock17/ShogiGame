**Streamlining the Data for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been given this treasure trove of Shogi game data – a bunch of comments associated with each move. My task? To refine this into something truly useful for analysis. It's about taking the raw material and shaping it into a clear, concise narrative of the game's evolution.

The goal is pretty straightforward: I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff. Think meta-commentary, biographical tidbits, time stamps... anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a particular move. My focus is laser-sharp: I'm after the essence of the game.

The process, as I see it, boils down to a methodical filtering. I'll meticulously read each comment, applying the specified removal rules. It's a matter of identifying and isolating those specific phrases or sections that fall under the "unnecessary baggage" category. The goal is to leave only the gems – the explanations and insights into the player's thinking – so I can truly understand the flow of the game.
