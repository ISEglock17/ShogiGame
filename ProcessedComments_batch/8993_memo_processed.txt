**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine these comments, to make them more useful for in-depth game analysis. Currently, they're cluttered with extraneous information. I need to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that's not directly relevant to the board position and the move itself. What I want are concise, clear explanations of the moves and their strategic implications, presented as natural-sounding sentences. This will make it far easier to understand the flow of the game and identify key tactical and strategic moments.
