**Analyzing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The goal here is to get down to the pure, insightful analysis of the game itself, removing all the noise. I need to strip away any extraneous details: player introductions, the time stamps, anything that's not directly relevant to understanding the board state or the moves being played. It's about distilling the commentary to its most potent form.

Essentially, I'm working with a set of rules to categorize and process each comment associated with a move. I have to analyze the text, determine if it falls under the "keep" or "delete" criteria, and then generate the appropriately cleaned-up comment and a justification for any deletions made.

Currently, I'm focusing on the rules of what to exclude. Rule number one is a quick, easy one: *any* mention of names, ranks, or affiliations that don't belong to the players themselves. That information is interesting, but it's not the core of the tactical analysis I'm seeking to retain.
