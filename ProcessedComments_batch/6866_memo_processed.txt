**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one actually. It seems someone, likely another enthusiast or researcher in my field, wants to clean up commentary from Shogi game records. They've got a pile of these comments attached to each move, and they want to streamline them. The goal is clear: strip away all the extraneous noise – the metadata, the introductions of players, the timestamps, anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. My aim is to be left with natural, coherent explanations of the actual moves and their strategic implications, stripped down to their most essential form for further analysis. They want to be left with just the core commentary on the board.
