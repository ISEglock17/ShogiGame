**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform raw commentary into coherent, insightful analyses of the game itself. No fluff, no extraneous details about the players, the time, or any background noise – just the core strategic observations and explanations.

My process will involve two key steps. First, I'll meticulously scan each comment against a set of established rules to identify and eliminate the irrelevant parts. This means I'm looking for and removing any metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the moves being made.

Then, with the core strategic content isolated, I'll focus on crafting the remaining pieces into a fluent and naturally flowing narrative. The objective is to produce commentary that's both concise and easy to understand for anyone analyzing the game.
