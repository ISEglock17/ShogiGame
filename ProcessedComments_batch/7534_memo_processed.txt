**Focusing the Analysis: Streamlining Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task in my work with shogi game data: cleaning up the commentaries. The user needs the comments in each game record to be streamlined. Basically, they want me to focus the analysis on what *truly* matters – the actual board states and move explanations.

The goal is clear: remove all the fluff. That means stripping out the irrelevant bits: metadata that's not directly related to the game, personal introductions, timing information... anything that isn't essential to understanding the game's flow and the players' thought processes at each turn. The end product should be a series of natural, easily understandable sentences that *only* discuss the board and the moves.

So, the plan is straightforward. First, I need to define the exact rules for what gets deleted. What constitutes "unnecessary information"? Once I have those rules locked down, I can implement them to extract and shape that commentary into something really useful.
