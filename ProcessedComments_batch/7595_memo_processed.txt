**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. The goal is pretty clear: get rid of all the fluff and noise. I need to strip out the stuff that's irrelevant to the actual analysis, like metadata, player introductions, or time information. What I'm after is the core of the analysis - the explanations of the board positions and the reasoning behind each move, presented as cohesive, natural sentences. That's the stuff that will be useful for my work. So, first thing's first...
