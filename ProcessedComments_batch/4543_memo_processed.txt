**Refining Commentary in Shogi Data**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's right up my alley. I've got these Shogi game records, and specifically, I need to clean up the commentary associated with each move. The aim is to make the commentary much more streamlined and useful for analysis.

My goal is to isolate the *actual* analysis and explanation of the moves themselves. That means getting rid of all the clutter: the metadata about the game, the player introductions, and any time-related details. Basically, anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the strategic nuances of the move.

The procedure seems straightforward: I'll carefully review each comment, identify the elements that should be removed based on a set of clearly defined rules, and then delete those bits. Finally, I'll take what remains, the core of the move's explanation, and rephrase it, smoothing out the language to create a clear, natural-sounding analysis. This should give me a much more focused and insightful dataset for further study.
