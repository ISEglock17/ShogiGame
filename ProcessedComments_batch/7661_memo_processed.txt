**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've been tasked with cleaning up the comments within Shogi game data, and my goal is clear: I need to transform these raw comments into something useful for analysis.  Think of it like this, I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – all the stuff that clogs up the signal.  What I'm after is the pure essence: the actual explanations of the board positions and the rationale behind the moves.  I need to distill these comments down to their core, making them flow like natural explanations, but still precise.

My process will be pretty straightforward: I'll carefully review the comments against a set of rules – almost a filter – to identify what needs to go. Anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the *why* and *how* of the game, gets the chop. It's about preserving the analytical nuggets and discarding everything else.  It is a data cleaning process tailored for Shogi game commentary and analysis, and I'm ready to make it happen.
