**Refining Commentary in Shogi Data**

Okay, so I'm looking at Shogi game data with comments attached to each move. My task is to refine these comments, essentially cleaning them up according to specific rules.  I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps – all the stuff that's not actually *about* the board position and the move itself.

My process will be pretty straightforward: First, I'll analyze each comment and use the defined rules to identify and eliminate the irrelevant information.  Think of it like surgically removing the chaff from the wheat. Then, with the core of the commentary remaining, I'll focus on smoothing it into a natural, understandable sentence structure. Finally, the processed commentary has to be outputted in a pre-defined format. It's all about extracting the valuable insights about the board's state and the strategic reasoning behind each move, and presenting it clearly and concisely.
