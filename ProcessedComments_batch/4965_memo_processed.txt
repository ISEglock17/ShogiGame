**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up the commentary accompanying Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff that gets in the way of analyzing the actual game itself. Think of it as pruning the text down to its bare essentials – the insightful explanations of board positions and move choices, delivered in a way that feels natural and flowing. Basically, I want to remove the fluff: the metadata, the introductions of the players, the time stamps... all of that is noise to my analysis. I'm focusing solely on the pure game commentary.
