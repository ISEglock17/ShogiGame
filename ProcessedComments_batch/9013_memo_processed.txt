My process will be straightforward. I'll use a set of defined rules, honed through experience, to identify the noise – the meta-information, the player introductions, the time stamps – and surgically remove it. This leaves behind the core: the insightful commentary on the position, the strategic rationale, the tactical possibilities. My goal is to extract the pure essence of the game's wisdom, stripping away everything that doesn't contribute to a deeper understanding of the moves. Ultimately, this will improve my ability to get the most out of the data.
