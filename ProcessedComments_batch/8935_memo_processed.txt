**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's pretty clear-cut, which I appreciate. My job is to take these Shogi game commentary streams, you know, the ones that come attached to the actual moves and game data, and whip them into shape. I need to make them *useful* for analysis. 

What's the goal? To get rid of all the noise. That means anything that's not directly related to the *board state* or the *reasoning* behind specific moves. I'm talking about metadata, introductions of the players, time checks... all of that extraneous chatter has to go. It needs to be gone.

The process itself is straightforward: I have to apply the established rules. I'll read through the commentary, spot the parts that fit the "keep" category (explanations of strategy, tactical insights, etc.) and discard the rest. The idea is to have clean, readable explanations linked directly to the key moments of the game.
