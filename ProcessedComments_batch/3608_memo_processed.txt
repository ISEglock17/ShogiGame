**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game commentary and my immediate thought is "This needs a serious scrub." My goal here is laser-focused: I want the *pure* essence of the game analysis, the strategic insights that emerge from the board itself.  All the extraneous noise - the meta-data, the introductions of the players, the time stamps - that's cluttering things up and making it harder to spot the *real* gems.  My intent is to isolate the natural language explanations of the positions and moves, stripping away anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the flow of the game. That's the key to making this data really useful for deeper pattern recognition and research. Let's get to work...
