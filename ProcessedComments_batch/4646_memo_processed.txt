**Cleaning Up My Analysis: Refining Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've got this task, and it's right up my alley. Someone's handed me these raw chess game commentaries, and they want them cleaned up. Specifically, they want me to refine the text to focus solely on the game's analysis. My goal is to extract the pure tactical and strategic explanations of each move, the stuff I find truly valuable.

What this boils down to is a deep dive into the comments, identifying and deleting all the "noise". Think: biographical tidbits about the players, the time each move took, or any other metadata that clutters the insightful analysis. The task is to leave only the valuable commentary behind, like a natural flowing text.
