**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a set of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to streamline them – get rid of the fluff and just keep the good stuff for analysis. Basically, I want to filter out the noise. Things like meta-data about the game, introductions of the players, time stamps... that's all irrelevant to my needs. What I *do* want are the actual explanations of the board state and the moves, the analysis that a skilled commentator would provide. I need to make sure the remaining comments read as natural sentences, not disjointed phrases.  The process is pretty straightforward: I'll use a set of defined rules to go through each comment and determine what's necessary to keep and what needs to go. Ultimately, I'll be left with a much cleaner dataset, and that will be a lot more useful for my work.
