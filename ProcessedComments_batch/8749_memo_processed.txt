Essentially, they want me to surgically remove the irrelevant noise and leave behind the pure essence of the analysis, the stuff that truly matters for understanding the game's evolution. It's about preserving the natural flow of the commentary, but only the insightful commentary. And thankfully, they've laid out the rules and procedures, so it should be a straightforward process of identifying and removing the specified elements.
