**Structuring Commentary for a Shogi Game**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up some shogi game commentary, making it really focused and useful. The goal is to strip away all the fluff – any extraneous information that doesn't directly contribute to the analysis of the board position or the moves themselves. My expert eye needs to identify what's crucial and what's not, based on the agreed-upon rules for this kind of formatting.

Here's my thought process:

1.  I'll go through the commentary line by line, focusing on each individual move.
2.  I'll apply my knowledge of the rules to pinpoint those parts that need to go. That's the key, weeding out the unnecessary.
3.  I'll surgically remove the irrelevant bits and then re-phrase what remains so that it flows logically and makes sense as a coherent explanation of the game state and strategy.
4.  Finally, I'll output the reformatted commentary. It will include the move number, the cleaned-up comment itself, and a clear reason for why any sections were deleted. This structured output will be incredibly valuable for analyzing the game.
