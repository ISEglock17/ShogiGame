**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's right up my alley. I need to take a raw dataset of Shogi game commentary – the kind we all get, chock-full of metadata, introductions, time stamps, and the like – and refine it. My goal? To get down to the pure essence of the analysis. I want only the insights that truly matter: the on-the-board evaluations and the descriptions of the moves themselves, all presented in a coherent, natural flow.

My process is straightforward: I'll carefully review the commentary, applying a set of well-defined rules to identify and excise the irrelevant noise. It's about surgically removing the extras, leaving behind only the vital anatomical information required for rigorous study.
