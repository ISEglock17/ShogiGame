**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of game commentary from Shogi matches, and my task is to refine it.  The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform this raw, often messy text into something lean and focused for serious game analysis.  Think of it as stripping away the fat to get to the pure essence of the strategy and the board state.  

Right now, the commentary likely contains a lot of fluff - things like pre-game introductions, player biographies, timestamps, and various other meta-details.  That stuff is interesting for casual viewers, sure, but it's noise when I'm trying to extract meaningful insights about the moves themselves and the unfolding strategic picture. I want only the core explanations of the positions and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a clear and flowing narrative style.  Effectively, I want to reconstruct the game's evolution as perceived by an expert.
