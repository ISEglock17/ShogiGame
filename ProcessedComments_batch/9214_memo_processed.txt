**Cleaning Up Game Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to whip the comments from shogi game data into shape. Essentially, they've got this messy bunch of text attached to the moves, and they want it refined. My job is to act like a text scrubber, removing all the extraneous noise – you know, the metadata, the introductions of the players, those pesky time stamps – anything that isn't directly related to what's happening on the board or an explanation of a move. The goal is to leave behind only the pure, valuable insights into the game, expressed as natural, flowing sentences. It's about preserving the *analysis*, not the surrounding chatter. They want a clean, focused narrative of the match, just the good stuff.
