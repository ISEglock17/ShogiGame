The goal is clear: I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board and the moves themselves. My aim is to distill the commentary into its essence, the natural language explanations of what's *actually* happening on the board. This way, I can feed it into my analytical tools without noise. I need to get started with the proper methodology, so I can ensure this process is done efficiently.
