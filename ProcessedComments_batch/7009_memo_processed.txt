**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I've got a challenge here. Someone wants me to refine Shogi game commentary. The goal is clear: strip away all the extraneous noise and leave behind the pure essence – the board state and the move explanations, presented in a natural, flowing style. It's about data integrity for the purpose of focused analysis.

The task is to methodically process each comment based on a set of well-defined rules. My job is to act as a digital editor, ruthlessly eliminating any information that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's evolution. This means getting rid of:

*   **Irrelevant Names and Credentials:** No player details beyond the actual moves. No mentions of teachers, schools, or lineages.
*   **Historical Distractions:** Any references to past games or precedents are out. They clutter the primary narrative.
*   **Temporal Noise:** Time is irrelevant here. No need for time stamps or commentary about time controls.
*   **Player Biographies and Statistics:**  No player background, rank details, player numbers, or related information.
*   **Style and Pattern Irrelevancies:** Get rid of commentary about a player's style or game type.
*   **Location and Logistics:** Details about the location, weather, hotels, anything outside the board are off limits.
*   **Interruption Notices:** No mentions of meals, breaks, or game restarts. The focus is solely on the strategic flow.

In essence, I'm aiming to craft a lean, insightful commentary that isolates the game's strategic core, ready for in-depth analysis. This is about clarity and precision.
