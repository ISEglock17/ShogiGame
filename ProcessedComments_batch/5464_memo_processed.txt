The goal is to isolate the true analysis of the board state and the moves, stripping away the extraneous noise that's clouding the signal.

That way, when I'm feeding this data into my analysis tools, I'll be working with a focused, valuable resource. This is about making these comments truly *actionable* for my deeper understanding.
