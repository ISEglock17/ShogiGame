The goal is clear: I need to refine these comments, trimming away all the unnecessary fluff – you know, the metadata, the introductions, the timing details – and leave behind only the pure, insightful commentary on the board state and the moves. Think of it as sculpting the text, removing the extraneous to reveal the valuable core.

Then, the real fun begins: I'll carefully reconstruct the remaining fragments into well-formed, natural-sounding sentences. It's about preserving the analytical content, not just removing noise. This process should result in a much cleaner, more focused set of comments, perfect for the kind of in-depth analysis I'm after.
