**Processing Shogi Commentary: Refining for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The user wants me to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the fluff that's not actually *essential* for analyzing the game. My goal is to distill each comment down to its core: the state of the board and the specific moves made, presented in a clear, natural-sounding way. 

The approach is straightforward, really. I'll take each comment one by one and apply the given rules meticulously. It's all about identifying the information that can be discarded, the non-crucial stuff that's bogging down the signal. Then, I need to craft what's left into something that flows well, something that reads like a concise and informative description of the game's evolution. It's about getting the balance right between accuracy and readability, ensuring that the essence of the position and the play is preserved.
