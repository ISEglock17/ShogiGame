**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

I've got a pile of text, presumably accompanying a bunch of games, and it's full of all sorts of extra stuff - meta-information, introductions to the players, time stamps... the whole shebang. What I need to do is strip away all that noise. My goal here is to get down to the pure essence: the actual explanations of the board positions and the reasoning behind each move. I want to be left with natural, readable sentences that shed light on the game's unfolding. It's a matter of extracting the strategic nuggets from the fluff, basically.
