Okay, so I'm looking at this mountain of Shogi commentary data and thinking, "There's gotta be a better way."  I see what the user wants:  clean, focused explanations of the game itself, stripped of all the distracting noise.  It's about getting to the *core* of the analysis, the essence of each move and its strategic implications.  Forget the introductions, the player backstories, and the clock readings.  All that stuff is fluff.  I want pure, insightful commentary on the board state and the reasoning behind the moves.  This isn't about the *who* or the *when*, but the *what* and the *why*.  I need a process to filter out the irrelevant data and refine those insights into digestible nuggets of strategic brilliance.
