**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi commentary snippets, right?  The goal is to strip away all the fluff and extraneous details, leaving only the pure, distilled essence of the board state and the moves being made.  Think of it like getting rid of all the background noise so I can *really* hear the game.

I've got a set of rules to guide me, and I'm going to apply them systematically. Basically, anything that doesn't directly relate to the *current* game on the board is out.

The list is pretty straightforward.  First, names that aren't the players are gone.  Then, anything about past games or historical context gets deleted – precedent is important, but not in this exercise. Time stamps? Gone. Any mention of profiles, rankings, or styles, it's irrelevant. Location details, weather, food breaks, all that context is, shall we say, non-essential. It's like, just give me the moves and the analysis, and that is all I need. My algorithms have better things to do than think about what the weather was like during the game! The goal is to maximize the Signal to Noise ratio.
