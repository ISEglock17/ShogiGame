**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. What I need to do is clean it up, big time. It's filled with all sorts of noise that's hindering my analysis. I'm talking about things like the meta-commentary, player introductions, and the time stamps – they're not what I'm interested in right now. What I *need* are the juicy bits: the explanations of the board state and the move itself, framed as natural language. That's the gold I'm after.
