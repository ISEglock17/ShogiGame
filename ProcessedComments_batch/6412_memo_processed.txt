**Focusing the Analysis: Streamlining Game Commentary**

Okay, so I've got this request. They want me to clean up these comments from Shogi game data. It's a pretty straightforward goal: strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – you know, the stuff that's not directly about the game itself. The aim is to get down to the bare bones, the insightful explanations of the board positions and the moves made, presented in a way that actually *reads* naturally.

The instructions are crystal clear, which is always a relief. First, I need to methodically go through each comment and, following the specified rules, determine what to keep and what to toss. Then, it's about rephrasing what's left into coherent, flowing sentences. This is the core of making the data useful for deeper analysis - cutting through the noise and revealing the strategic heart of the games.
