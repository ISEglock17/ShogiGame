The process I've got to follow is pretty straightforward, and it's something I'm used to doing. First, I need to go through the commentary line by line, or sentence by sentence. I'll need to use some pre-defined rules to figure out exactly what information is supposed to be removed. I'll then delete all the data that's deemed irrelevant to the key information for the analysis of the board state.
