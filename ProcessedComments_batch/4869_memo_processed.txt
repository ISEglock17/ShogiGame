**The Challenge of Clean Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a block of Shogi commentary, and the goal is clear: strip away all the fluff and extraneous details, leaving only the essentials for a proper move-by-move analysis. Think of it like a distilled version, focusing on the core strategic insights, not the pre-game banter or the post-move analysis that's purely flavor. 

It's essentially a filtering process. My focus is on identifying and preserving the critical elements: the board state explanations, the specific move details, and the insightful commentary on those moves. I need to be methodical here. I'll need to go through each comment sequentially.  I need to be vigilant, ensuring that nothing important gets lost in the process, while at the same time, diligently removing anything extraneous.
