**Analyzing and Refactoring Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to take Shogi game commentary and clean it up, making it focused and precise for analysis. My goal is clear: I need to transform verbose commentary into a clear, concise record of the game's key moments. This means I'll have to surgically remove anything that's extraneous - introductions, time stamps, meta-data, and biographical fluff are all on the chopping block.

Essentially, I'm parsing the text, identifying the "signal" from the "noise."  My process will be straightforward. I'll meticulously go through the commentary, applying the rules I've been given to pinpoint irrelevant elements. Once I've excised all the unnecessary information, I'll then smooth out what remains, crafting natural, flowing sentences that clearly explain the board state and the significance of each move.  Finally, I'll package up the refactored commentary in the designated output format. It's all about providing the clearest possible picture of the game's flow and strategic developments.
