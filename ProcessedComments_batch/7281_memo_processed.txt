**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform the raw commentary data – which likely includes all sorts of extraneous information – into something streamlined and insightful for analysis. I'm imagining a process that filters out the noise, keeping only the essential, the heart of the matter: the actual explanations of board positions and move choices, all presented in a naturally flowing style.

Essentially, I'm going to carefully read each comment associated with a move, and I'll be using this pre-defined set of rules to decide what to discard and what to keep. I'll need to be sharp and precise, ensuring that I'm deleting irrelevant meta-information, player introductions, or any time-related details that don't contribute to understanding the game's evolution. It's all about distilling the essence and preserving the strategic understanding for more sophisticated analysis.
