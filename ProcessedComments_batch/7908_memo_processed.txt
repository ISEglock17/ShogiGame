**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up commentary from shogi games. The goal is to distill these comments down to their essence: the actual analysis of the board state and the moves being made. I need to strip out all the fluff that's not strictly relevant to the strategic discussion. That means ditching any metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly pertain to the thought process behind the moves.

Basically, I'm going to carefully read through the comments and, following a specific set of rules, identify which parts are crucial for understanding the game's flow and which are just noise. The aim is to end up with concise, natural-sounding sentences that focus solely on the strategic insights, as if I'm dissecting the game board myself.
