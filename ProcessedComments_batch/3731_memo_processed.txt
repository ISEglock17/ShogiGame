**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm looking at a bunch of comments from Shogi games, right? My goal here is to refine them - to get rid of all the fluff and just keep the good stuff, the actual analysis of the game. Things like, "Player A is on a roll!" or "It's now player B's turn." I want those gone. Also, the introductions, the time stamps... all of that is irrelevant. What I *need* is the breakdown of the board state and the explanations of why a certain move was played. That's the meat of it. I want to end up with clean, natural-sounding sentences that dive right into the strategic thinking behind the moves.  That's my primary focus in this whole process.
