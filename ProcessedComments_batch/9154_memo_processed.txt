**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Dive Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task: I've got a mountain of comments attached to a Shogi game record and need to sculpt them into something useful for in-depth analysis.  The goal is crystal clear: eliminate all the noise, the extraneous bits and bobs, and extract the pure essence of the commentary – the actual analysis of the board and moves themselves.  I need to strip away the meta-data, the introductions of the players, the time stamps... all of that's irrelevant here.  What I'm after is the insightful stuff, the critical evaluations of positions and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a fluid, natural way.  Think of it as refining ore – I'm separating the valuable nuggets of analytical gold from the surrounding rock.  Let's get to work and make this data really shine.
