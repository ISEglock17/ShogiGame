**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the noise and keep only the insightful analysis of the game itself – the actual board states and moves, explained in a clear, flowing narrative. Basically, I want to strip out everything that isn't directly relevant to understanding the gameplay.

My plan involves a two-pronged attack. First, I'll use a set of rules to identify and ruthlessly eliminate all the extraneous material. That means anything that's not about the board position or the thought process behind a move is out the door. Think biographical details, time stamps, tournament introductions – all of that goes.

Once I've purged the comments of the irrelevant stuff, I'll then focus on crafting the remaining snippets into well-formed sentences. I want the commentary to read naturally and coherently, providing a smooth understanding of what's happening on the board at any given moment. I'll need to make sure the structure and phrasing are clear and easy to follow.
