I want to isolate the pure, insightful commentary on the board's state and the moves themselves, presented in a clean, flowing narrative.

I'll carefully examine each piece of text, make a call based on the ruleset, and then either excise the irrelevant bits or preserve the valuable analysis. It's about achieving a streamlined, data-rich resource perfectly suited for in-depth kifu analysis.
