That's the meat of it.

I've got a set of rules for what gets deleted, so that's the first hurdle. Then, and this is important, I'm going to rebuild what's left into sentences that actually *read* like they're a natural part of a commentary. Finally, I'll package up the cleaned-up commentary in exactly the format that's been requested. It's a clean-up and restructuring job, basically.
