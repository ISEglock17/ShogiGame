**Processing Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm tasked with refining these Shogi game commentary datasets. The core goal is to extract the *essential* strategic insights from the often verbose commentary surrounding each move. Basically, I need to strip away all the fluff – you know, the meta-commentary, player introductions, timestamps, any of that non-analytical noise – and distill it down to the raw commentary on the board state and the move itself, while maintaining natural language flow.

The process is well-defined, thankfully. I'll need to meticulously evaluate each piece of commentary based on a specific set of rules to determine what's truly relevant and what's extraneous. Anything marked for deletion will then have a reason coded and recorded. The output format is straightforward: I'll deliver the refined commentary, clearly indicating the move number, and then document the specific removal reason, including the rule number.
