**Cleaning Up Commentary: Focusing on the Game**

My objective is crystal clear: I need to refine the comments associated with each move in these shogi game records. The raw comments are often a bit verbose, chock-full of metadata, introductions of the players, time spent on each move, and other potentially distracting information. My goal is to extract the essential, game-focused insights - the actual board analysis and explanations of the player's choices - and present them as clear, concise, and natural-sounding sentences.  Essentially, I'm aiming to filter out the noise and leave behind the signal, allowing for a focused analysis of the strategic thinking behind each move. It's about distilling the essence of the game from the surrounding fluff.
