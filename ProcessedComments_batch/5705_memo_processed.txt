**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform the raw commentary into something focused and useful for game analysis. I want to strip out all the extraneous stuff - the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it. My aim is to be left with only the valuable insights about the board positions and the moves made, expressed as natural, flowing sentences.

Essentially, I'm going to take each comment and put it through a rigorous filtering process. I'll need to meticulously analyze each one, determining what should stay and what should go, always keeping an eye on the bigger picture of providing clear, concise, and analytically relevant commentary. I'll need to clearly explain the reason for the decision to keep or remove the data as well.
