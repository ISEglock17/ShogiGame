**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one: refining commentary within Shogi game records. The goal is clear: strip away the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, and extraneous time stamps – and distill the commentary down to its essential essence. I need to retain only the insightful explanations of board positions and move selections, presented in a fluid, natural manner.

I'll approach this systematically, utilizing a well-defined set of rules. I'm essentially filtering the noise. My filter has a few key components. Firstly, I'll ruthlessly excise any mention of individuals *not* directly involved in the current game – no names, ranks, or lineage details for irrelevant figures. Secondly, I'll be vigilant for references to prior games or historical precedents; these add context, sure, but for *this* analysis, they're distractions. Thirdly, the temporal information must go – seconds, minutes, any explicit indication of time elapsed is superfluous to the core analysis.

Essentially, I am transforming the comments into pure strategic insights, ready for my preferred analysis methods. This is straightforward and essential for deriving meaningful patterns.
