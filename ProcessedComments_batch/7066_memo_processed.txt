**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my task is to refine it. The user wants me to clean it up, basically. The goal is to strip away all the extraneous fluff – the parts that aren't actually relevant to the analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. I need to leave behind only the natural-sounding explanations of the position and the specific moves made.

My thought process will be pretty methodical: I'll analyze each comment, judging it against the given rules. My job is to recognize and eliminate anything that is deemed unnecessary based on those rules. Once I've trimmed the fat, so to speak, I'll then work on the remaining information, reshaping it into grammatically correct and flowing sentences.
