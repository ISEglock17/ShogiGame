**Processing Chess Game Commentary for Enhanced Analysis**

The goal here is pretty clear: to isolate the *actual* insightful analysis of the game, the stuff about the board positions and the moves made, and get rid of all the extraneous fluff.  Think of it as the difference between a detailed engine report and a casual play-by-play.  We're aiming for the former.

The requestor wants to keep the commentary concise but fluent, transforming it into natural sentences.  The good news is, it seems like the process is very well-defined.  There are clear steps and rules that I can follow, which makes things a lot easier.  I need to dive into those rules to get started. I'm keen to see what the parameters are to guide the process.
