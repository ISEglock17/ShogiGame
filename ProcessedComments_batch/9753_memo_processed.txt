**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Insight**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty typical challenge: a bunch of Japanese shogi game commentary, likely from a database of games, and it's a bit cluttered.  The user wants me to sift through this, keeping only the really valuable parts.  Basically, they want me to filter out all the fluff.  

My goal, as I understand it, is to take these comments and transform them. I need to get rid of anything that's not directly related to the actual game analysis. That means stripping out things like the author's name, general introductions, time stamps – anything that doesn't actually enhance our understanding of the board position or the reasoning behind the moves.  What I'm left with should be a clean, flowing explanation of the play itself, focusing purely on the moves and the analysis of those moves.  Think of it like getting to the core of the strategic discussion, the essential arguments and counterarguments in a given sequence. This will allow the user to focus on the truly important information in the data, the heart of the shogi strategy.
