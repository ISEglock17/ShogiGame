**Refining the Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm presented with a user's request: they want to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, stripping away all the fluff and irrelevant details. Essentially, they want the raw, essential information about each move, focusing on the board state. This is right up my alley!

My process is straightforward: I'll tackle each move's commentary individually, like a puzzle. Using the defined rules and format as my guide, I'll carefully sift through the text, identifying the "noise" – the unnecessary commentary, subjective evaluations, or extraneous details. My goal is to isolate the *core* of each move, the vital information needed to understand the board's evolution. This is all about precision and clarity, getting to the heart of the strategic dance.
