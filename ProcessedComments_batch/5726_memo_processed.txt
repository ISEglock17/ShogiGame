They're aiming to distill the essence of each move, the *why* and *how* of the play, while ditching the fluff. It's all about stripping away the non-essentials: the biographical details, the timing of the moves, any meta-commentary that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board's evolution.

Essentially, they want a laser-focused analysis. I need to be a ruthless editor, adhering to a very specific set of rules to identify what's crucial and what's extraneous. Once I've excised the unnecessary bits, I'll then smooth out the remaining text, ensuring it reads like a natural, insightful explanation of the board state and the move itself. Finally, I'll package it up in the designated output format. It's a job that requires a deep understanding of Shogi strategy and a keen eye for effective communication – a task I'm more than ready to tackle.
