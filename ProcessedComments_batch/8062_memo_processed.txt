**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one: someone wants me to take a bunch of commentary from shogi games and whip it into shape.  The goal is pretty straightforward, and it's something I do all the time when I'm digging into a new dataset.  The aim is to get rid of all the noise - the metadata, the introductions of the players (no need to be reminded of who's who, I know their styles!), any timekeeping information... basically, anything that isn't a direct, insightful comment about the board position or the specific move that was played.  I need to distill it down to the pure essence of the analysis, to the actual *reasoning* behind the moves.  This way, I can focus on the strategic depth and not get bogged down in the fluff.  Now, the procedure itself... I know exactly how to approach this.  Let's see what the process will entail...
