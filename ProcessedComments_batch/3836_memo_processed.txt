**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Alright, so the user wants me to clean up the commentary accompanying shogi game data, huh?  They want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the bios, the time stamps – and just leave the core, insightful explanations of the moves and board states. My goal is to transform those scattered thoughts into concise, naturally flowing sentences that are directly relevant to the game's unfolding strategy.

The process is pretty straightforward. First, I have to be the judge, based on the provided rules, and decide what gets the axe.  I'll mercilessly delete any irrelevant information. Then, I'll stitch together what remains into a readable narrative. Finally, I'll present the output in the prescribed format: move number, followed by the polished comment, and a "Deletion Reason" section clearly listing the removed elements and the specific rules that justified their removal. This will make it much easier to track the analysis process and ensure consistency. This should give the user a clean, focused dataset for their advanced analysis, free from the noise.
