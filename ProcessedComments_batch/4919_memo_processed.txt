They want to strip out all the extraneous stuff – the meta information, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – leaving only the insightful analysis of the board and the moves themselves. Essentially, we're aiming for a stream of natural, readable explanations of the game's unfolding dynamics.

My task is clear. I need to methodically go through each comment associated with a move and apply the pre-defined rules to filter out the irrelevant data.  It's about extracting the pure essence of the commentary – the "why" and "how" behind each decision on the board.
