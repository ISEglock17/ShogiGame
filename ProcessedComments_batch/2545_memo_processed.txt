**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've got this dataset of shogi game comments, and my goal is to wrangle it into something useful for my analysis. It's filled with all sorts of noise right now - metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it. Really, all I care about are the actual board positions and the explanations for each move. I need to get rid of the fluff and leave behind natural-sounding commentary on the strategic thinking behind the game.

Essentially, I need a good rule-based system to automatically identify and eliminate the irrelevant stuff. Once I have that, I can focus on extracting the real gems from the commentary and build a cleaner, more insightful dataset.
