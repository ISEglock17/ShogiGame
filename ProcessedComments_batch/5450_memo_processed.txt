**Processing Game Commentary for Strategic Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with refining commentary from Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the fluff – you know, the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – and focus solely on the valuable insights about the board state and the specific move being analyzed. Essentially, I'm aiming to create a clean, concise, and natural explanation of each move, perfect for in-depth kifu analysis.

The process is straightforward, but the devil's in the details, as always. First, I need to meticulously parse each comment, applying the specific rules to identify and remove irrelevant information. This is the crucial stage where I need to be precise and avoid accidental deletions.

Once the irrelevant parts are gone, I'll then massage the remaining text into grammatically sound sentences. Think of it as rebuilding a fragmented paragraph into a cohesive whole, always keeping the core strategic content intact.

Finally, the output needs to adhere to a very specific format. Each processed comment will be preceded by the move number and the cleaned-up commentary, followed by a list of the specific reasons and rules that motivated the deletions. This structured output is vital for keeping things organized and traceable. It's really the crucial step for auditing and ensuring clarity.
