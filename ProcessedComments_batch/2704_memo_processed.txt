**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I've got a challenge here. Someone wants to refine the commentary in their Shogi game data. The goal is clear: strip away all the extraneous noise – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and so on – and leave behind only the pure essence of the game's unfolding: the board positions and the strategic insights offered by the commentators. Think of it as distilling the commentary down to its most potent form for analytical purposes.

My task is to process these comments meticulously, adhering to a set of specific rules. I need to be precise.  It's about identifying the irrelevant bits, surgically removing them, and then re-framing what's left into coherent, flowing sentences. This is about making the data truly useful, not just readable. It needs to *feel* natural.
