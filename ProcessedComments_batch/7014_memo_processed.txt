I need to leave only the valuable stuff: the insightful analysis of the game, presented in a fluid and readable manner.

So, the process seems straightforward enough. It's all about applying the right rules to filter out the irrelevant content. The key is in those rules, of course. I need to ensure my parsing is extremely precise and focused, just as I would do when reading the game myself.
