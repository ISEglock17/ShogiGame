**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm dealing with a lot of Shogi commentary data and need to get it into a clean, usable format. Basically, I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps – and just keep the good stuff: the actual explanations of the board positions and the move choices. My goal is to end up with concise, natural-sounding sentences that are easy to analyze. 

So the process is pretty straightforward. I'm going to set up some rules, probably based on typical patterns in these commentaries, and then methodically go through each comment, identifying what needs to go and what needs to stay. It's essentially filtering out the noise to reveal the signal, right? Should be a pretty interesting challenge to automate this in an efficient manner.
