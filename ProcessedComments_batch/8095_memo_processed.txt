**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so the task at hand is to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty straightforward: make the commentaries useful again by stripping out all the noise. I'm talking about getting rid of all the extra stuff – the introductions, the time stamps, the player bios, and anything else that doesn't actually help analyze the moves or understand what's happening on the board.

Basically, I want to filter the text down to the core insights about the board position and the move that was just made. I want to keep the "why" and "how" of the play, presented naturally and concisely, without all the extraneous fluff.  This way, the remaining commentary becomes a real asset for studying the game and understanding the strategic thinking behind each decision. Now, let's figure out the best way to do this...
