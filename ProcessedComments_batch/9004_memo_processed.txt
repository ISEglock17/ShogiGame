**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've got this request. They want me to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform these raw comments into concise, focused explanations of the game itself, removing all the noise. Think of it like stripping away the excess fat to reveal the lean, insightful meat of the analysis.

Basically, I'm going to carefully scrutinize each comment, applying my expertise to identify and discard anything that's irrelevant to the actual board state or the moves being made. That means ditching all the meta-stuff – the introductions, the player biographies, the time stamps... all the fluff that a seasoned Shogi player wouldn't need. My objective is to leave behind only those comments that directly shed light on the position, the strategic thinking, and the rationale behind each key move. The end result should be a streamlined narrative that flows naturally, catering specifically to the needs of someone who understands the game. It's about preserving the *insight*, not the extraneous details.
