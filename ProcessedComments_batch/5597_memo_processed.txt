**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

My goal here is clear: to strip away all the extraneous noise – the metadata, player introductions, timestamps, that sort of thing – and preserve only the valuable insights. I want the core, the essence of the game's evolution, presented as clear, understandable, natural language explanations tied directly to the board states and specific moves. This is about building a clean, digestible dataset for further, deeper analysis. I'm keen to ensure the focus is on the *why* and the *what*, not the peripheral details.
