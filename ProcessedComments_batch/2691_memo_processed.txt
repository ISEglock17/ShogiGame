**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments associated with each move and turn them into something useful for analysis. My goal is to streamline the commentary, removing all the extraneous fluff – things like metadata, player introductions, timestamps, you know, the usual clutter – and retain only the core insights: the actual explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. It's about distilling the essence of the analysis into clear, concise, and grammatically correct sentences that a machine learning model, or even I, can easily parse and understand.
