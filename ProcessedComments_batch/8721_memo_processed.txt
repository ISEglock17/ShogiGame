**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform the raw text into something lean and focused for in-depth analysis of the game.  I'm basically stripping away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – anything that doesn't directly illuminate the board state or the moves themselves. My goal is to extract the pure essence of the commentary: the tactical analysis, the strategic considerations, the insightful explanations of each move, presented in a smooth, coherent narrative. It's about preserving the *meaning* of the commentary, not the extra baggage that gets in the way of true understanding.
