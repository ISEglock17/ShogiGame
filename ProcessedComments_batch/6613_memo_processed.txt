**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a really interesting task. It seems someone needs me to refine a set of Shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: they want to transform the often-verbose commentary that accompanies game records into something much more focused and digestible, perfect for deep analysis. 

What I'm hearing is that all the extraneous stuff needs to go. That means things like biographical details about the players, the time spent on each move, and general meta-commentary that isn't directly related to the actual game's progress.  My mission is to surgically remove that noise, leaving behind only the core insights – the insightful explanations of board positions and the reasoning behind each player's moves. They want natural-sounding sentences that illuminate the strategic thinking, not just a bunch of filler.  Essentially, I'm streamlining the commentary into a pure stream of tactical and strategic thought, which is exactly what a serious Shogi analyst needs.
