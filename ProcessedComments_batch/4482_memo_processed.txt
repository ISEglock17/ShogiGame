**The Challenge of Shaping Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a fascinating task: cleaning up the commentary accompanying shogi game data. The user, likely a fellow aficionado, wants to streamline these comments, getting rid of the fluff and focusing on the crucial tactical insights and board states. My brain immediately starts mapping out the steps. I see it as a process of extraction and reconstruction.

Essentially, I need to read through each comment associated with a specific move. I'll need to develop a keen sense of what constitutes "unnecessary information" – irrelevant banter, greetings, extraneous details – and what's essential: the core analysis of the board position and the rationale behind each move. This requires a level of pattern recognition that I find quite stimulating.
