**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary, and the goal is to clean it up. The task is to transform it into something truly valuable for my analytical work.  I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff – the metadata, the introductions of the players, time stamps... all the fluff that distracts from the core of the game. My focus is on retaining only the natural-language explanations that *actually* describe the board positions and the rationale behind the moves.  This way, I can streamline the process and focus on the really important patterns.
