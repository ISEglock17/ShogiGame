The goal is to refine these comments, trimming away all the fluff – the metadata, player intros, timestamps – and leaving behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. My objective is to distill these comments into natural, coherent sentences that offer valuable insights for my own analysis of the games.
