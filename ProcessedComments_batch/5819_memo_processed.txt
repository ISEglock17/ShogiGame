**Cleaning Up My Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this interesting task of tidying up shogi game commentaries. The user is asking me to take all this raw data and transform it into something truly useful. Basically, they want me to surgically remove all the noise – the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps, you know, all the fluff that gets in the way of proper analysis – and just leave behind the pure, distilled essence of the board position and the move's explanation.

So, here's my plan: I'm going to carefully go through each comment, one by one, and apply a set of rules. These rules are going to be my guide, telling me exactly what gets to stay and what gets the axe. My goal is to extract the core insights and present them in a way that feels natural and flows logically, so they can be easily understood and make the most sense.
