My goal is clear: I want those comments to be concise, natural-sounding explanations of the board state and the moves, stripping out all the irrelevant stuff. Things like who's playing, how long it took them, any biographical details, or any meta-commentary that isn't directly related to the actual analysis have to go.  I'm basically aiming for pure, distilled Shogi wisdom in these comments.
