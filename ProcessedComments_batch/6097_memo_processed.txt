**Focusing My Efforts on Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up the commentary within a Shogi game's data.  Essentially, the goal is to surgically remove all the noise – the meta stuff, the introductions of the players, time stamps – leaving behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board and the moves.  Think of it like getting to the core of the strategic discussion.

My process will be straightforward: I'll carefully review each comment, applying a set of rules to identify and eliminate the irrelevant chatter.  Then, I'll work to reshape what remains into concise, readable sentences. Finally, I'll package up the cleaned-up commentary in a format that's easy to digest.

Specifically, I'll be targeting the information that's cluttering up the analysis. Things like mentioning any player that isn't directly involved in the game, their ranks, any associations with their master, and the like will be removed. These are distractions; the actual moves and their implications are what matter. I'm ready to get to work!
