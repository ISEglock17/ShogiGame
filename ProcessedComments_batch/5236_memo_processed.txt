**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with this mountain of Shogi game commentary data, and honestly, it's a bit of a mess. My task is to refine it, to *sculpt* it, really. I need to take these often rambling, stream-of-consciousness comments and strip away the extraneous fluff. You know, all the meta-stuff, the player bios, the timestamps – fascinating in their own way, but utterly useless when I'm trying to analyze the actual *moves* and understand the strategic thinking behind them. My goal is to distill each comment down to its core: the actual explanation of the board state and the reasoning behind each played move. It’s like being a curator, selecting only the most valuable pieces of information from a vast archive. The process will be a systematic one - I'll meticulously apply the rules I have in place to make sure only the essential remains.
