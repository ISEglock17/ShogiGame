I need to refine the comments so that they only contain the really valuable insights. That means stripping out all the fluff – the metadata about the game, the player introductions, and even the timestamps. I want to keep only the pure, concise explanations of the board state and the moves being played. It's like distilling the essence of the game from all the noise.
