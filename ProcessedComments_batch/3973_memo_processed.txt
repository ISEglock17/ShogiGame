**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with these Shogi game commentaries, and the goal is to make them much cleaner and more useful for analysis. Basically, I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock information – and keep only the gold: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves.

So, here's how I see it: I'll go through the commentary piece by piece. First, I need to decide, based on the established rules, whether each phrase or sentence is relevant enough to keep. That's step one: careful filtering. Then, once I've got the essentials, I need to make those retained parts flow like natural, coherent explanations. It's about preserving the *meaning* while polishing the presentation. It is like editing for conciseness and clarity, so the underlying analysis is more robust.
