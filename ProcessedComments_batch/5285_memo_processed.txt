**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, I've got a new task. Someone wants me to refine the commentary that comes with shogi game data. They've got a bunch of text associated with each move, and they want it cleaned up. My goal is to transform this raw commentary into something much more useful for in-depth analysis of the game.

Essentially, I need to strip out all the fluff. All the metadata about the players, any time stamps, introductions, irrelevant information – that's all gotta go. What I *do* want to keep are the actual insights and explanations about the board positions and the rationale behind each move. The challenge is to present those insights in a way that flows naturally, like real human thought during the game. It's about distilling the core strategic commentary into something that feels clean and intuitive for further study.
