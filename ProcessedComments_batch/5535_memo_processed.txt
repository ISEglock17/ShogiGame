**My Task: Refining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've got this interesting project.  Basically, I need to clean up and refine Shogi game commentary data.  The goal here is to transform the existing text into a format that's ideal for in-depth analysis.  It's all about extracting the core insights from the commentary.

I want to strip away all the extraneous noise, the fluff that doesn't contribute to understanding the game itself.  This means eliminating anything that's not directly related to the current board position or the moves being made.  I'm talking about getting rid of the meta-information: who's playing, introductions, time stamps, all that stuff. 

The objective is to refine the commentary down to the essentials - just the commentary on the board state, and an explanation for the moves. It will be the pure essence of the game's unfolding in words.
