**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my task is to refine it. The goal, as I understand it, is to strip away all the extraneous noise – the stuff that's not directly related to the board position or the player's moves, and instead keep the juicy strategic analysis and move explanations. Think of it like a distilled version of the commentary, pure insight.

Essentially, I need to methodically go through each comment, applying the rules I've been given to filter out anything that doesn't contribute to the core understanding of the game as it unfolds. It's a process of careful analysis and considered editing, ensuring that the remaining text flows naturally and offers meaningful insights into the players' decision-making process. I'm essentially honing in on the strategic heart of each move.
