**Cleaning Up These Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of commentaries on Shogi games, and the goal is to make them more useful for analyzing the actual gameplay. What a mess! Right now, they're filled with all sorts of fluff: meta-commentary, biographical details about the players, timestamps, and who knows what else. It's distracting and obscures the good stuff – the actual *analysis* of the board state and the moves being played.

My job is to go through these comments systematically, applying a set of rules, and ruthlessly cut out all the noise. I need to identify the bits that are pure commentary on the game itself and preserve them as natural, flowing sentences. Basically, I'm aiming to create a streamlined, focused narrative that highlights the strategic thinking behind each move, free from all the irrelevant clutter. Think of it as distilling the essence of the game analysis into its purest form.
