**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game comments. The goal is clear: strip away all the extraneous noise and leave only the pure essence – the analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. I want to end up with clear, natural language explanations that would be useful for in-depth kifu analysis.

I've got a set of rules to follow, a filter if you will, to identify and remove the unwanted commentary. I'm focusing on getting rid of things that don't directly contribute to understanding the game's flow. 

I'll be vigilant, watching for:

1.  Mentions of people *other* than the players themselves, including their ranks, affiliations, or any associated schools.
2.  Any reference to previous games or prior examples. Those pieces of historical context are important but not critical for the "on-the-spot" analysis of the current game's situation.
