I want to keep only the insightful explanations of the game as they unfold, making the commentary feel natural and analytical.

Essentially, I'm cleaning up the information to facilitate a proper analysis of the game.

I'll be using the given rules and input/output formats to systematically process each comment. I'll take a close look at each one, identify the parts that can be removed, and discard them in accordance with those rules.
