**Cleaning Up the Chess Commentary**

Okay, so I'm looking at this chess commentary data and the goal is pretty clear: I need to clean it up. The raw comments are often a bit of a mess, and I want to get them into a format that's actually useful for deep analysis. Right now, there's a lot of noise I need to remove. It's things like the metadata about the game, the player introductions, and all the timestamps. These are totally irrelevant to the actual analysis, so they have to go. My focus needs to be on the insightful explanations of the board state and the moves being made. I need to keep the commentary that really *matters*. And, ideally, I'd like the remaining comments to read more naturally, like a coherent sentence.
