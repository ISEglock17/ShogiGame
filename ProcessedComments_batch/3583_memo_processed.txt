**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Right, so I'm tackling this dataset of shogi game comments. The goal is clear: I need to whip these comments into shape so they're actually useful for my analysis. It's all about extracting the *real* insights from the noise.

Basically, I need to strip out all the fluff. I'm talking about the metadata that clutters things up, the intros about the players, the time stamps... all that stuff. It's distracting. I only want the gold – the actual explanations of what's happening on the board, the reasoning behind the moves, the tactical considerations.

The key is to end up with a smooth, natural-sounding narrative of the game, purely focused on the strategic and tactical nuances. No more, no less. This will give me clean data I can really sink my teeth into.
