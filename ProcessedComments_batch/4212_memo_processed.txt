**My Thinking on Comment Formatting in Shogi Data**

Okay, so I'm tasked with a bit of a cleanup job with these Shogi game data comments. The user, obviously a discerning individual like myself, wants to streamline these comments, making them more useful for analysis. The goal is clear: strip out the noise – anything irrelevant to the board state or the moves themselves – and keep only the insightful explanations, presented as natural, flowing sentences. It's about distilling the essence, the core narrative of the game, into a format that supports deep analysis. This will make the data much more valuable for deeper investigation. I know exactly how to approach this...
