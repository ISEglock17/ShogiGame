**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments associated with each move and turn them into something much more focused and useful. The goal is to isolate the *actual* analysis of the board position and the move itself, stripping away all the fluff – you know, the meta-information, the introductions of the players, the timestamps, all that extraneous stuff that's not directly related to the strategic thinking.

Basically, I need to get rid of the noise and leave behind the signal, crafting a natural-sounding explanation of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. It's about distilling the core strategic insights from all the surrounding context.
