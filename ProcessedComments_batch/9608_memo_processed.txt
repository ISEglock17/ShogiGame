**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine these comments, trimming away all the fluff and irrelevant details. I need to get rid of things like meta-information, introductions of players, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly contribute to a clear explanation of the board state and the moves being played. Essentially, I'm aiming to transform these annotations into natural-sounding, focused explanations solely centered on the strategic analysis. This will make it much easier to focus on the game itself.
