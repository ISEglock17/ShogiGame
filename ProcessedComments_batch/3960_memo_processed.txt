**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game commentary and I need to clean it up. The goal is clear: I want to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – basically anything that isn't directly related to explaining the board position or the moves being made. I'm after the insightful analysis, the strategic explanations, the "why" behind the moves, not the preamble.

My plan is straightforward:

1.  I'll define some concrete rules for what constitutes noise.  This will involve identifying patterns in the commentary that consistently point to extraneous information. Then, I'll filter the comments according to those rules.
2.  Next, I'll focus on the remaining text and start to reformat it. My aim isn't just to mechanically remove information but to reshape what's left into more natural-sounding sentences. This part is crucial, as the quality of my analysis depends on the clarity and flow of the commentary.
