**Cleaning Up Commentary: Focusing on the Game**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of game commentary from Shogi matches, and my goal is to refine it. I need to take these often lengthy descriptions and sculpt them down to their essential form. The objective is clear: strip away everything that clutters up the core analysis. That means all the extra noise – the introductions to players, the clock readings, and any metadata that doesn't directly relate to the board state or the moves themselves. My goal is to arrive at smooth, clear explanations of the moves and their tactical implications. I want the essence of the game to shine through, unburdened by extraneous details. The focus needs to be on the *how* and the *why* of each move, not just the *who* or the *when*.
