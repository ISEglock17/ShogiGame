**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary data, and the goal is to clean it up. The task is to transform it into something truly useful for analysis, discarding all the fluff and irrelevant details. I need to retain only the essential insights – the explanations of board positions and move selections, expressed as natural, flowing sentences.

It's about distilling the core commentary, stripping away anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the strategic nuances of the game. That means jettisoning things like metadata, player biographies, and time stamps – all of which, while interesting, are ultimately noise for my analytical purposes. I'm after the heart of the commentary, the 'why' behind each move and its impact on the board.
