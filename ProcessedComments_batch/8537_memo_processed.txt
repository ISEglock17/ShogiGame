**Refining Commentary for Deeper Game Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at these comments that come with Shogi game data. The task is pretty straightforward: I need to clean them up. The goal is to isolate the *actual* analysis and commentary on the game itself, removing all the noise. Think of it like stripping away the excess fat to get to the lean muscle.

My process will be methodical. First, I'll go through the comments with a very specific set of rules, which I'll apply to identify and eliminate anything irrelevant to the board state or the moves being made. This includes all the usual suspects: metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and any other tangential information that's cluttering up the analysis.

Once I've purged the comments of this extraneous material, the next step is to make sure what remains reads smoothly and accurately as a natural sentence. The final output needs to provide an elegant and useful analysis, free of all the unnecessary distractions.
