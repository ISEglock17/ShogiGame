**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a fascinating challenge: a dataset of Shogi game moves, each accompanied by commentary. The goal is to clean up these comments, to refine them into something truly insightful. I need to strip away all the extraneous noise - the introductions, the time stamps, the biographical fluff - and get down to the *meat* of it: the analysis of the board state and the player's reasoning behind their moves.  Essentially, I want to preserve the pure tactical and strategic gems within the text, presenting them as seamless, natural explanations. The process, I imagine, will involve a carefully crafted set of steps, and I'm ready to dive into the specifics of that.
