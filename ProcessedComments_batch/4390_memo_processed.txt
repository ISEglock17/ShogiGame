**Analyzing and Refactoring Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to take a pile of Shogi game commentary and whip it into shape, to make it clean, focused, and actually readable.  My process will be methodical. First, I'll take it one move at a time, parsing through each comment associated with that move. Then, I'll examine each individual sentence within that comment, and that's where things get interesting.  I'll compare each sentence against a predetermined set of rules – a kind of "hit list" for unnecessary information.  If a sentence matches one of these delete rules, *poof* it's gone. If it survives, I'll keep it.  My goal is to distill the essence of the move, retaining the insightful commentary while stripping away the noise.
