**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I've got this Shogi data with all these comments attached, and the goal is to make them really useful for analysis. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, introductions, time stamps, all kinds of stuff that isn't helping me understand the *actual* game.  I need to get to the heart of the matter: the board positions and the reasoning behind each move.

Here's my plan: first, I'm going to carefully go through each comment and use a set of rules to identify and ruthlessly eliminate anything that isn't directly related to the strategic thinking.  Think of it as an editorial process, but with a very specific, technical focus.  Then, with the remaining pieces, I'll work on smoothing them out, making sure they flow like natural language, even if they're quite specific about piece placement or tactical considerations.  It's all about extracting the valuable insights, stripping away the fluff, and presenting the core of the strategic discussion in a concise, easily digestible format.
