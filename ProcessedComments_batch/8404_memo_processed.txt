I get it. When you're running complex algorithms or even just doing some manual deep-dive analysis, that extra information becomes a real distraction. It's like having a beautiful piece of art and then someone's written a grocery list all over the canvas. Leaving only the essential elements of the board position and the rationale behind each player's decisions.  This seems straightforward enough - let's see what we can do.
