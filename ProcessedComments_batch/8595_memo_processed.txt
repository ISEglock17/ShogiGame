**Refining Commentary for Deeper Strategic Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at these Shogi game data sets, and the goal is to make these comments much more useful for strategic analysis. Right now, they're cluttered with extraneous information. I need to get rid of the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps... all that stuff. It's distracting and doesn't contribute to understanding the actual game progression.

So, the process seems pretty straightforward: first, I have to figure out which parts of the comments are *unnecessary* based on some pre-defined rules. Then, I'll excise those elements, leaving only the parts that explain the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. My aim is to re-write what's left into more natural, flowing sentences. This way, I can focus solely on the strategic insights embedded within the comments, making the data far more valuable for my research. It's about distilling the core ideas and removing the noise, ultimately leading to a more focused and fruitful analysis.
