**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary entries. The goal is to isolate the *actual* analysis of the game itself – the thoughts on the board state and the moves being played – and get rid of all the fluff. I'm talking about the stuff that doesn't contribute to understanding the game's evolution: the introductions to the players, the time stamps, the general chit-chat. All that needs to go.

Essentially, I need a reliable method to identify and purge these irrelevant elements. My plan is to start by defining clear rules for what constitutes "unnecessary" information. From there, I can systematically filter through the comments and retain only the valuable insights related to the board and the moves.
