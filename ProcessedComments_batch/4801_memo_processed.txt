**Cleaning Up These Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of cleaning up these comments associated with Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform the messy commentary into something concise and useful for my analysis. I want to keep the valuable insights about the board state and the moves themselves, ditching all the noise that clutters it up.

Essentially, I'm going to be surgically removing the fluff. My plan is to methodically go through each comment, applying the rules I've been given to filter out the irrelevant junk. I'll be looking out for names of people not involved in the actual game, their ranks and affiliations, all that background data about their careers and playing styles, and any mentions of past games, time elapsed, or the location of the match. That stuff doesn't help me understand the flow of the current game. I'm focusing solely on the *here* and *now* of the game.
