**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm dealing with a mountain of commentary data from shogi games, and the goal is to make it useful. Right now, it's a bit of a mess with all sorts of extraneous information cluttering up the actual analysis. My focus is laser-sharp: I need to transform these comments into something clean and insightful.

Essentially, I'm going to take the following approach:

1.  **Sift and Delete:** First, I'll go through each comment, meticulously identifying and zapping all the noise. This includes all the meta-stuff, introductions of the players (I already know who they are), time stamps – anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. My expertise allows me to quickly identify what is relevant.
2.  **Smooth and Polish:**  Once the junk is out of the way, I'll focus on the remaining text. My job is to ensure the core analysis flows smoothly and reads like a natural, logical explanation of the moves being made. This will be an iterative process.
3.  **Format and Deliver:** Finally, I'll get everything into the correct output format. This step ensures the clean commentary seamlessly integrates with my other analysis tools and workflows.
