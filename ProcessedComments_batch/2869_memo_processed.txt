**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm dealing with a request to clean up and format comments from shogi game data. I understand the goal: they want to extract the good stuff – the actual analysis of the board and moves – and ditch all the extraneous noise.  Think metadata, introductions, time stamps...all that fluff that just gets in the way of a good read.

My plan is pretty straightforward. First, I need to define the "rules" of what constitutes noise.  Then I can go through the comments and surgically remove that information. Next, it's about making sure what's left reads smoothly, like a coherent explanation. Finally, I'll need to structure the output to match the format they're expecting.  Piece of cake!
