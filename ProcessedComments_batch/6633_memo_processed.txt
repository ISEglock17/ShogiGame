**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of game commentary from Shogi matches, and my task is to refine it.  The goal is pretty clear: I need to transform these raw comments into something lean and focused, suitable for deep analysis of the games. Think of it like stripping away the excess fat to get to the core of the matter.

Essentially, I want to remove all the fluff. That means ditching any metadata about the game itself, like the date, participants' names (I'll assume I have that information elsewhere), and anything about the time controls. I'll also be getting rid of all the introductions, the player biographies – all that introductory stuff that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the position or the move itself.

My focus is laser-sharp on the *positional evaluations* and the *explanations of the moves*. I want the comments that directly discuss the board state, the strategic implications, the tactics involved – that's the good stuff! I want it to read smoothly, as if it was a focused, flowing explanation of the chess game.
