**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm looking at a pretty standard request here: someone wants me to clean up the commentary from a Shogi game dataset. The idea is to take those often-lengthy comments, which usually have a lot of noise, and strip them down to the bare essentials. I need to get rid of all the extraneous stuff - the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and any other fluff that doesn't actually contribute to understanding the board position or the reasoning behind a specific move.

Essentially, I need to distill the commentary into a series of natural sentences that focus *solely* on explaining the board state and the player's intentions with each move. No distractions, just clear and concise explanations.  The goal is to leave a streamlined, focused narrative of the game's evolution.
