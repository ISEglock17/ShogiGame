That means farewell to the fluff – no more metadata, no player introductions, and certainly no time-related details.  I want clean, concise explanations, focusing solely on the board and the thought process behind each play, transformed into natural, understandable sentences. This stripped-down commentary will be the perfect fuel for my analysis.
