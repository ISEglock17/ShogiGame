**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I've got this Shogi game data with all these comments, and the goal is to make them much more useful for analysis. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, introductions, time stamps... stuff that's just distracting and doesn't actually help me understand the game. My task is to refine this commentary so it focuses purely on the board state and the moves themselves.

My process will be pretty straightforward. First, I need to go through each comment and, following a specific set of rules, identify and remove all the irrelevant bits. It's like cleaning out the excess fluff. Then, once I have the essential information, I'll need to smooth out the language, ensuring that the remaining text reads like natural, insightful explanations of what's happening on the board.  It's about crafting clear and concise descriptions that enhance, not hinder, my analysis.
