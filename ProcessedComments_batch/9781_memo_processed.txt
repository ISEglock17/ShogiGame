**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a really common challenge in analyzing shogi data. I need to take these comment strings, you know, the ones that accompany the games, and refine them. The goal is to isolate the *actual* useful commentary – the strategic insights, the board position descriptions, and the move analyses – from all the distracting noise.

Think of it this way: I'm trying to create a clean, lean dataset for analysis. I want to strip away all the fluff. That means the metadata, the introductions of the players, any time stamps that aren't *directly* relevant to the game's unfolding, and anything else that's more narrative than analytical.  I'm after the core strategic discussion, formatted in a way that reads smoothly and contributes directly to understanding the game's flow. It's about distilling the essence of the game's commentary into a focused, insightful set of notes.
