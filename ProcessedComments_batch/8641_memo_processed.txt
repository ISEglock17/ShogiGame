**Refining Commentary in Shogi Data**

Okay, so I'm looking at this Shogi data, and the user wants me to clean up the commentary associated with each move.  The goal is pretty clear: they want to isolate the *useful* stuff, the actual analysis and explanations of the board state and the moves, and get rid of all the extraneous junk that's cluttering it up. I mean, who needs to know how long it took someone to think or get a biography of the player mid-game?

My plan is straightforward, which is good. First, I'll need to establish some solid rules. What constitutes "unnecessary information" in the commentary? Things like timestamps, player introductions, maybe even generic phrases that don't add anything to the strategic discussion are all on the chopping block. Once I have those rules defined, it's a matter of going through the commentary and surgically removing those bits. Then, the real fun begins: I need to take what's left and smooth it out, make it flow like a proper explanation of the game state. That means filling in any gaps, rephrasing things for clarity, and making sure it all reads naturally, as a complete thought.
