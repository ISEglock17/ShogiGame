**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments, like you'd see in a live game or post-game analysis, and turn it into something much more focused. The goal is to isolate the true meat of the discussion – the analysis of the board state and the moves themselves.

My task is pretty straightforward in principle: identify the extraneous stuff and get rid of it. That means I need to sift through each comment and be ruthless. Bye-bye to all the fluff – no more player introductions, time stamps, meta-data, or anything else that doesn't directly relate to the board's strategic situation or the reasoning behind the moves. Anything that doesn't help me understand the flow of the game, the evaluations, or the insights, has to go. Essentially, I need to extract the essence of the analysis, making it into a concise narrative that focuses solely on the game's evolution.
