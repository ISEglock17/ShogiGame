Essentially, they want me to strip out all the fluff and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the game.

My goal here is to get rid of all the noise: the metadata, the introductions of the players, time stamps, irrelevant biographical details... anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board position or the reasoning behind a specific move. I'll need to identify the elements I need to remove, based on a predefined set of rules, and then get rid of them.

Once I've done that, the next step is to make sure what's left reads smoothly and naturally. I have to transform any fragmented phrases or technical jargon into complete sentences that explain the situation on the board and the player's intentions in a coherent way. Essentially, I'm aiming for clean, insightful, and easily digestible commentary.
