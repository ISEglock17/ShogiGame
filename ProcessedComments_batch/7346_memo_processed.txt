The goal is crystal clear: strip away all the extraneous noise – the metadata, player introductions, timestamps – and distill the commentary down to its pure essence: insightful, flowing descriptions of the board state and the moves being made. Essentially, I need to transform raw commentary into elegant, digestible analyses.

Essentially, I'm aiming for concise and impactful explanations that a fellow Shogi expert would appreciate.
