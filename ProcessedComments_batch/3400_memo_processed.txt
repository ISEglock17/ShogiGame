**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my goal is clear: I need to transform these comments into something clean and focused, ideal for in-depth game analysis. I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff - the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it. The only things that should remain are the natural-language explanations of the board positions and the moves being made. The challenge lies in extracting the *meaningful* commentary and presenting it in a coherent form, as if it were a continuous narrative of the game's unfolding dynamics. This is all about refining data into meaningful insights.
