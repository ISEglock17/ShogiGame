**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've been tasked with cleaning up the comments associated with Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform those raw comments into something useful for analysis. I'm aiming for concise, natural explanations of the board state and the moves made, stripping away all the fluff.

This means getting rid of anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's flow. No more introductions, player biographies, or timing information – that's all noise. My focus will be on the core tactical and strategic elements: the "why" and "how" behind each move. Ultimately, this will give me a streamlined, easily digestible dataset ready for deeper analysis.
