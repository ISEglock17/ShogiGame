Basically, they want me to strip out all the extraneous stuff - metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know, the fluff - and leave behind only the genuinely insightful commentary about the board and the moves themselves. My job is to transform these comments into concise, natural-sounding explanations, focusing solely on the analysis of the gameplay. I get it; focusing on the game is where the value lies.
