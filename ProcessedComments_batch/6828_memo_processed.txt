**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to Shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the truly valuable stuff: the insightful explanations of the board state and the moves being made. I need to distill the commentary down to its essence, making sure what remains flows as natural language. That's the challenge.
