**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi commentary comments from game data. The goal is clear: they want to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – basically anything that's not directly relevant to the actual analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. They want a clean, natural-sounding narrative of the game, move by move.

My task, as I understand it, is to process each comment according to the given rules. It's a three-step process:

1.  **Filtering:** First, I'll need to carefully examine each comment and identify those pieces of information that need to be removed based on the specific rules.  I'll delete all the extraneous stuff.
2.  **Structuring:** Next, I have to take what's left – the core explanations of the board and the moves – and smooth them out into grammatically correct sentences that flow logically.  It's about crafting a coherent narrative from the raw commentary.
3.  **Formatting:** Finally, I'll need to output the cleaned-up commentary in the requested format. This ensures that the results are compatible with their analysis tools.
