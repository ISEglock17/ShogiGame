**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary attached to shogi game data. It's clear what they're after: the raw, insightful stuff. My goal is to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – you know, the stuff that clogs up the signal. I want to be left with only the good stuff, the actual analyses of the board state and the explanations of each move, transformed into cohesive, readable sentences. This is a task that needs precision. I'll need to figure out the right way to differentiate between the essential commentary and the noise, ensuring I don't accidentally throw the baby out with the bathwater. This is crucial for anyone diving deep into the data for strategic research or model training.
