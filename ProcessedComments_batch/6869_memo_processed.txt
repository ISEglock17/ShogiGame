**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up commentary from shogi games. The goal is to isolate the *actual* insights about the game - the analysis of the board position and the moves being played - and remove all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the strategic nuances of the match. What I need is a streamlined, coherent narrative of the game, purely focused on the intellectual exchange on the board.
