**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm tasked with a bit of a cleanup job, but a crucial one for my work. I need to take these Shogi game commentaries, the ones that come with all the data, and make them really useful for analysis. The idea is to have clean, concise explanations of the moves and board states, nothing else getting in the way.

So, here's how I'm thinking of tackling it. First, I'm going to carefully go through each comment, applying those specific rules I've got. That means I'll be filtering out all the noise - the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps... anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the *why* and *how* of each move.

Once I've stripped away all the unnecessary bits, the real fun begins. I'll need to go through what's left and essentially re-write those comments into a proper, natural language. The goal is clarity and flow, making sure those explanations read smoothly and make sense on their own. This is about transforming raw data into something I can really use.
