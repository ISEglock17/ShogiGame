**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I understand the goal here is to clean up and streamline Shogi game commentary, removing the noise and leaving only the insightful analysis. My task is to take the raw comment data and transform it into something truly valuable for kifu analysis.

My process will be straightforward: First, I'll meticulously go through each comment, identifying and eliminating irrelevant information based on a set of clearly defined rules. This means stripping out anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's state or the strategic reasoning behind a move. I'm talking about metadata, player introductions, time stamps – anything that clutters the core analysis.

Next, I'll work to reshape the remaining content into grammatically sound and coherent sentences. This isn't just about deleting; it's about crafting commentary that flows naturally and provides a clear picture of the board and the thought process behind each move.

Finally, I'll structure the output according to the specified format, clearly presenting both the refined commentary and the reasons behind any deletions. This will give users complete transparency into the transformation process and the reasoning behind each modification. The specifics of the deletion rules will need to be made explicit as well.
