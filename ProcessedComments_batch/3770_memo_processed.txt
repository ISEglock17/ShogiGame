**My Approach to Refining Shogi Comments**

Essentially, I need to strip away all the extraneous information – the meta commentary, any biographical details about the players, and time stamps – that's irrelevant to the core analysis of the game. What I *want* to preserve is the pure essence of the commentary: the explanations of the board positions and the specific move-by-move analysis. I want the result to be a streamlined, coherent narrative of the game's unfolding.
