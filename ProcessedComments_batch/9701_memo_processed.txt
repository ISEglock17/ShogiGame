**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these shogi commentary sections from game data. The goal is clear: I need to strip away all the fluff and extraneous details – the metadata, introductions, time stamps, you name it – leaving only the insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. Essentially, I want to distill these commentaries down to their purest form, presenting them as seamless, natural explanations of the game's progress.

My strategy is straightforward: first, I'll apply a rigorous set of deletion rules to identify and remove all the unnecessary information. Once the chaff is separated from the wheat, so to speak, I'll then focus on refining the remaining text into grammatically sound and flowing sentences. This process should yield clean, focused commentaries that are perfect for in-depth kifu analysis.
