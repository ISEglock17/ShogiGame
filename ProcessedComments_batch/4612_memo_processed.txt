I want to transform those fragmented bits of commentary into coherent, flowing sentences that are a pleasure to read and, more importantly, useful for my deeper analysis. This is all about preparing the data for serious research, stripping away anything that might distract from the core strategic insights. This has to be done carefully to ensure the information retained is both accurate and useful.
