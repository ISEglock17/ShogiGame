**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw commentary into something useful for in-depth analysis of the game. That means stripping out all the noise – the meta-data, the player introductions, the time stamps – all the stuff that's irrelevant to understanding the board state and the reasoning behind each move. My expertise allows me to quickly identify these unnecessary elements based on the rules I've defined. The crucial part is leaving behind the actual board and move explanations, but presented in a way that feels natural and fluid, as if someone is walking me through the game.
