**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments associated with moves in a game and filter it down to the good stuff.  My goal is to isolate the insightful, analysis-worthy parts – the actual explanations of what's happening on the board, the reasoning behind moves, tactical evaluations, that kind of thing.  I need to strip out all the fluff.

That means ditching anything that isn't directly related to the game's evolution.  Things like player introductions, meta-commentary about the recording process, time information - all that's just noise. My focus is on turning these scattered comments into a coherent, flowing narrative of the game's strategic and tactical development. It's about preserving the valuable analysis and making it easier to integrate into my own research.
