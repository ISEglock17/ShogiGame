My job is to strip away all the fluff, the introductory remarks, the time-related details, and any extraneous information about the players themselves. It’s about getting to the heart of the *analysis*, not the surrounding context. My goal is to transform these disconnected fragments into a cohesive and natural narrative that accurately explains the strategic flow of the game, move by move. It's a matter of distilling the commentary down to its purest form.
