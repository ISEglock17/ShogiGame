**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of Shogi game records. What I need to do is clean up all these move commentaries. The goal is to strip away all the fluff – you know, the meta stuff, the player introductions, and the time information – and leave behind only the pure, essential analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. My objective is to distill these comments down into natural, concise explanations that are perfect for in-depth kifu analysis. It's about getting to the heart of the strategic thinking without the distractions.
