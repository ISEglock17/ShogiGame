**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so the task at hand is to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty straightforward: strip out all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of all that meta-data, the introductions of the players, time stamps – you know, the stuff that doesn't actually help us analyze the *game* itself. What I want to see are only the *essential* comments that directly explain the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. My aim is to transform all this into a narrative flow, a natural commentary that guides us through the strategic battle.
