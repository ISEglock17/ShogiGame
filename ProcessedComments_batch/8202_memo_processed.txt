**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. What I need to do is clean them up, essentially. My goal is to extract the really useful stuff from each comment - the actual explanations of the moves and the board positions. I want to get rid of all the noise: things like introductions of the players, time information, metadata, anything that isn't directly related to the strategic analysis of the game. I want to be left with natural, readable commentary that focuses purely on the tactics and strategies at play.
