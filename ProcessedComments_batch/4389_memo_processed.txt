**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm looking at a bunch of comments from shogi games, right? My goal here is to get them into a format that's actually useful for analyzing the game itself.  I need to strip out all the noise – the meta stuff, who's playing who, how long it took them to make a move, all that jazz. I want just the good stuff: clear, concise explanations of the board state and what's happening with each move. This should read naturally, like a real breakdown of the game, not a bunch of extraneous information. That's the challenge.
