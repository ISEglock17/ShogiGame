**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of Shogi game comments. The goal, as I understand it, is to get these comments into a clean, concise format that's actually useful for my analysis. I'm talking about stripping away all the fluff – the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps, all that noise that distracts from the core of the game.

What I *need* are the gems: the in-depth explanations of the board state, the justifications for specific moves, the strategic insights. Those are the nuggets I can leverage for my research, right? So, my approach is pretty straightforward: I'll use a set of rules – I'll define these based on what's relevant to my analysis – to identify the irrelevant parts and cut them out. It's about getting to the meat of the commentary, that strategic core.
