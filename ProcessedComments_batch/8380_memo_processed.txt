**Streamlining Game Commentaries for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a mountain of commentary data from shogi games. My primary focus here is to refine this data, stripping away the fluff and concentrating solely on the game's essence. Think of it like a meticulous archeologist, brushing away the sand to reveal the artifacts.

Specifically, I need to identify and remove all the extraneous information clogging up the commentaries. Stuff like introductions of the players, time elapsed, perhaps even unrelated asides. My goal is to transform these comments into focused, readable explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. It's about distilling the core strategic insights, making them more amenable to in-depth analysis.

I'll start by building a set of rules – a framework, really – for identifying the redundant elements. This will be the foundation for the cleansing process. The goal is to obtain natural language explanations of the board and moves.
