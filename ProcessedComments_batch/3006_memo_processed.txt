**Structuring the Game Commentary**

Okay, so the task at hand is to meticulously clean up these shogi game comments. I need to make them as streamlined and focused on the actual moves as possible. Essentially, I'm removing all the extraneous information and adhering to these specific rules.

Firstly, I'm wiping out any mention of individuals *not* directly involved in the game, so that's the first sweep. Secondly, anything about previous encounters or historical context gets the axe – that's irrelevant to this current play. Time markers are gone, profiles and stats are deleted, no mention of playing styles or openings. Location details, any talk of food or breaks, and even the broadcast aspects are completely removed.

Then, there's the substitution process: replace the actual player names with "先手" (Sente, the player who moves first) or "後手" (Gote, the player who moves second) to maintain clarity. All other names disappear.

The output will be formatted clearly: each move number followed by the cleaned-up comment. I have to make sure to note why each deletion happened, linking it back to the specific rule that justifies it. This will make the process easier to follow and also explain what content was removed.
