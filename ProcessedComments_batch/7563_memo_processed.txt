**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of refining Shogi commentary from game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform these comments into concise, insightful explanations of the game's progress, focusing only on the board state and the moves being made.

Essentially, I'm peeling away the extraneous layers. I'll need to go through each comment and meticulously identify the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, any distractions from the actual analysis – and remove it according to a defined set of rules.

Once that unnecessary information is gone, my next step is critical: I'll need to reconstruct the remaining pieces into coherent, well-structured sentences that flow naturally. These should clearly articulate the strategic thinking behind each move and its impact on the board.
