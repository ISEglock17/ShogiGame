**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task. Someone wants me to clean up these Shogi game commentary entries. The goal is clear: they want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. Essentially, they want the raw, insightful commentary, presented as flowing, easy-to-read sentences.

My process will be straightforward, yet precise. I'll meticulously go through each comment associated with a move, carefully examining it against the established rules. Step one, and the most critical part, is to identify and eradicate the extraneous information. It's about surgically removing anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's flow.
