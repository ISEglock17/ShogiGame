Essentially, I need to take the raw input, which I expect to be a mix of board positions, move notations, and potentially some extra, less relevant observations, and turn it into a streamlined, readable narrative of the game's progress.

My plan is straightforward: I'll process the commentary one move at a time, each "hand" as it were. I'll read through each comment and use my knowledge of the rules to filter out any extraneous information. That's the key - removing the noise and keeping only the essential details about the board state and the moves made.
