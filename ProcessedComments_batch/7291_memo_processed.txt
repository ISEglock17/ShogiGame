The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, timestamps, any irrelevant details – and refine the remaining text into concise, natural explanations of the board state and move choices. Basically, I want to extract the pure analytical essence of the commentary.

I'll be working through the comments for each move, methodically analyzing them against a set of rules. This is where my expertise comes in; I need to discern which parts are critical for understanding the game's progression and which are just noise. I have to make a judgement call: what's the core insight here, and how can I present it clearly and efficiently? It's a process of careful extraction, prioritizing the strategic rationale behind each move while pruning away anything that distracts from the core analysis.
