**Streamlining the Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these shogi game commentaries, right? The user wants a streamlined, focused analysis, stripping away all the fluff and just keeping the key information about the board state and the moves themselves. My job is to transform raw, noisy comments into clean, insightful explanations.

Here's how I see it: I need to methodically go through each move's commentary, looking at it piece by piece. I have a set of rules – a kind of filter – that tells me exactly what kind of information I need to remove. Anything that fits those criteria gets the axe. Once I've excised the irrelevant details, I'll need to re-work the remaining text, smoothing it out and making sure it flows naturally as a coherent explanation of what's happening on the board.
