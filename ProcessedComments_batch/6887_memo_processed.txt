**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I've seen before: someone wants the commentary from Shogi game data tidied up. They've got this raw data, probably a mess of moves and annotations, and they're looking for a refined version.  My goal is clear: I need to transform the existing comments. I need to make sure to eliminate all of the extraneous stuff - the metadata, the personal introductions, the time stamps - and leave behind only the actual analysis that describes the position on the board and the reasons for specific moves. It's about distilling the essence of the analysis, the strategic understanding, and presenting it in a clear, concise, and natural language format. It's a classic data cleaning exercise, and I'm ready to get started.
