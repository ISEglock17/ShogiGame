**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of shogi game commentaries, and the goal is clear: I need to clean it up for deeper analysis. Right now, it's cluttered with all sorts of extraneous information – things like player introductions, time stamps, and general metadata.  Frankly, it's making it difficult to zero in on what *really* matters for my research.

What I want is the good stuff, the core of the commentary. I want it boiled down to the essential insights: descriptions of the board state and the nuances of each move.  The goal is to transform this raw, noisy text into natural-sounding sentences that focus on the tactical and strategic reasoning behind each decision.  Basically, I'm trying to filter out the noise and leave behind the signal that will help me truly understand the game.
