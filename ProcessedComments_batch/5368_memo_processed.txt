**Cleaning Up My Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling a data cleaning project, specifically within the world of Shogi. My goal here is to refine the comments associated with each move in a Shogi game's dataset. Essentially, I need to strip away all the extraneous information clogging up the good stuff – the actual analysis of the board and the moves themselves. Think of it like a dense journal entry; I want to pull out the valuable insights and discard the fluff.

My plan of attack is straightforward:

1.  I'll start by defining clear rules for what constitutes noise. This means identifying those telltale signs of irrelevant content, like meta-data about the game, introductions of players, or time stamps. Once I've got my criteria down, I'll flag and remove all of those elements from the comments. The core aim is to leave a concise and readable explanation of the move's reasoning, directly related to the state of the board.
