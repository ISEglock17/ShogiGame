**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a shogi game's commentary data, and my goal is to refine it. I need to take these often-verbose comments and strip away the chaff – the meta-commentary, biographical details, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board's state and the moves' rationale.

Essentially, I want to isolate the core analytical content. My process will involve a two-pronged approach. First, I'll apply a defined set of rules to rigorously identify and delete the irrelevant information. Once the extraneous material is gone, the remaining text will need some linguistic polishing to ensure that it forms coherent, understandable sentences explaining the positions and intentions behind the moves. This will make it far more useful for in-depth shogi analysis.
