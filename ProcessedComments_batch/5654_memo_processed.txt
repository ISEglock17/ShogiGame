**Focusing My Efforts: Refining Game Commentary for Sharper Analysis**

Right now, I'm concentrating on the task of cleaning up commentary within Shogi game data. My goal is to extract the pure analytical gold from these comments, removing all the extraneous noise that's hindering a deeper understanding of the game. That means stripping out all the usual suspects: metadata, player introductions, and the often distracting time information.  I want to be left with only the most relevant insights: the direct commentary on the board position and the specific move being played.  Essentially, I'm aiming to transform these raw comments into concise, flowing explanations that illuminate the strategic reasoning behind each decision. I envision this as a two-step process: meticulously filtering out the unnecessary bits based on a set of well-defined rules, and then refining the remaining text to create a narrative that's both clear and insightful.  This focus allows me to concentrate on what's truly essential for advanced analysis.
