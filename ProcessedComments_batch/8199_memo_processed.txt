**Focusing the Lens: Refining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the goal here is to refine shogi game commentary. I'm aiming to strip away the fluff – the irrelevant details that bog down analysis – and leave behind the core insights: the board state and the critical moves, presented in a clear, flowing narrative. My process will involve meticulously sifting through the commentary, applying a set of clearly defined rules to each comment. I'm essentially performing a surgical strike, removing the noise while preserving the signal. It's about data hygiene, ensuring that the commentary is a lean, mean, analytical machine.
