**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary attached to shogi game data. It's really about getting to the core of the analysis, you know? My goal here is to sift through all the extraneous noise - things like metadata, introductions of the players, time stamps... basically anything that doesn't directly relate to the board position or the moves themselves. I want to boil it down to the essential insights: the actual explanations of what's happening on the board and why certain moves are being made.  The aim is to keep just the explanations of the board and moves as natural sounding sentences.
