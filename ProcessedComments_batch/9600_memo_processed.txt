As an expert, I appreciate the need for data hygiene. The raw commentary is often cluttered with metadata, introductions to players, timing information, and other context that, while interesting, ultimately obscures the core analysis.

My immediate thought is, this is a pattern recognition problem. I need to train a system to discern the crucial parts. I will have to define the crucial parts of the comment, and will probably need to identify what isn't important for analysis.
