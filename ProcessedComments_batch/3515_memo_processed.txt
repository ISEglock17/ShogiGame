**Processing Shogi Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up some Shogi commentary, basically stripping it down to the essentials. I get it, they want a focused analysis, not all the extra fluff. My process is pretty straightforward:

First, I'm going to go through the commentary move by move, like a detective examining clues. Then, I'll be on the lookout for anything that falls under the "delete" category – things that are irrelevant to the actual game's progression or strategic implications.  This means anything that's not directly related to the board state or the player's reasoning behind a move is out.

Once I've identified the junk, I'll excise it based on the established rules. Then, the remaining bits get a polish. I'll make sure the language flows naturally and reads like actual expert commentary. Finally, I'll package everything up according to the specified output format, making sure it's organized and easy to follow.
