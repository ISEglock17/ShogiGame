**Cleaning Up Commentary: Focusing on the Game**

Okay, so I'm looking at these comments from shogi games, and the goal is to make them much more useful. Right now, they're cluttered with all sorts of things that, frankly, I don't need when I'm analyzing the actual moves. I'm talking about metadata, introductions to players, even time stamps – all the fluff that's not directly related to what's happening on the board.

What I *do* want is the core of the commentary: the explanations of the board state and the reasons behind specific moves. I want it to read naturally, like a flowing narrative of the game. So, the process is clear: remove the noise and leave the signal. That's the key.
