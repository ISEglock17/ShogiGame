**Structuring Shogi Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of formatting shogi commentary. Essentially, someone wants me to take raw commentary and clean it up, making it more digestible. My mental checklist for this is pretty straightforward:

First, I need to understand what's in front of me – I have to read each piece of commentary. Then, I'll be looking for specific patterns based on the provided rules, things that need to be trimmed or removed entirely. Once I've pinpointed those areas, it's about applying the rules meticulously, deleting those parts according to the instructions. Finally, the real art begins: taking what's left and shaping it into clear, natural Japanese explanations. I'm essentially distilling the essence of each move into concise and insightful commentary.
