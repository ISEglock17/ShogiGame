**Processing Game Commentaries for Effective Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta stuff, player introductions, time stamps – and just leave behind the pure, distilled essence of the board analysis. I want to keep the explanations of the positions and the moves, presented in a natural, flowing way.

Here's my plan of attack: First, I'll go through each comment and use my defined rules to identify and eliminate the irrelevant junk. Then, I'll take what's left and massage it into a proper, coherent sentence. Finally, I'll package everything up in the format that's expected. Simple, elegant, and efficient. This should make the data much easier to work with for any serious shogi analysis.
