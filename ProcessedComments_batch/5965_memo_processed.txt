**Streamlining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up some Shogi commentary.  My goal here is pretty clear: I need to take a messy, unstructured stream of commentary and transform it into something lean and focused.  I want to get rid of all the fluff – you know, the meta stuff, the introductions, the clock updates – and leave behind only the pure, essential analysis of the board state and the player's moves. It's about distilling the essence of the game and presenting it in a way that's both natural and informative for a *knowledgeable* audience.  Basically, I'm aiming to create a focused, streamlined account of the match, removing any unnecessary distractions so that you can see right to the heart of what's happening.
