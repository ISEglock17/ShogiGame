**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

That means stripping out all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock times – anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. I'm essentially performing a surgical strike, removing anything that isn't directly relevant to the actual game's progress and the strategic thinking behind it. It's all about extracting the core analytical nuggets and presenting them in a clean, coherent format.
