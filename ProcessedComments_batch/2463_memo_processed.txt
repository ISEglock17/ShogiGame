**Refining the Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi commentary transcripts. I understand the task; it's all about making the comments concise and focused on the *current* game. I've got the rules laid out, and they're pretty straightforward. Basically, I need to strip out anything that isn't directly related to the moves being made and the immediate state of the board.

Specifically, I need to purge:

*   **Irrelevant Chatter:**  Any mention of people who *aren't* the players themselves is out. That includes commentators, analysts, anyone not actively playing.
*   **Historical Detours:** References to past games, precedents, or anything not directly related to *this* particular match are gone. The focus is on the present.
*   **Temporal Noise:** All the clock readings, time spent thinking, and timestamps are unnecessary. The commentary should be timeless, focusing on the game itself, not the time it takes.
*   **Background Fluff:** Player profiles, records, and statistics, while interesting, don't belong here. They distract from the flow of the game analysis.
