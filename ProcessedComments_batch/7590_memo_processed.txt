**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Dive Analysis**

Okay, so I've been tasked with a pretty standard data cleanup job, but it's important for the work I do. I need to take these Japanese Shogi game commentaries - which are always a goldmine of strategic insight, but often cluttered with extraneous information - and streamline them. My goal is laser-focused: I want to strip away all the fluff. That includes stuff like background on the players, time stamps, and any general meta-commentary that isn't directly relevant to the board state or the moves themselves.

Essentially, I want to filter the noise and leave only the pure, distilled essence of the strategic analysis. The end product should be a set of comments that read like a continuous, insightful narrative focused solely on the evolution of the game. That way, when I feed this data into my analysis tools, I'll get much cleaner results.
