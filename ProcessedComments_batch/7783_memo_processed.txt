**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with a bit of a clean-up job. I need to take these Japanese shogi game records and really refine the comments attached to each move. The goal is clear: I want to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps... basically anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the actual board position or the thought process behind a specific move.

I want to be left with just the pure, insightful commentary. The stuff that describes the nuances of the position, the tactical considerations, the strategic implications of each play. Think of it as distilling the essence of the analysis, making it lean and focused for deeper study and perhaps feeding it into some machine learning algorithms later. It's about preserving the *wisdom* of the commentators without the clutter. This isn't a simple regex replace, its about careful understanding and extraction.
