Think of it like stripping away the noise to hear the signal crystal clear.

My process will involve meticulously analyzing each comment associated with a move, according to the specific rules laid out. I'll need to identify what's essential to understanding the game's flow and what's just extraneous chatter. The final output will be a refined comment for each move, along with a clear explanation of why any part of the original comment was deleted. This way, the user can understand the rationale behind the cleanup and have the cleanest possible dataset for their Shogi analysis.
