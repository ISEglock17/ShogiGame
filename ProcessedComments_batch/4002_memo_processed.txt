**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and the goal is clear: to strip it down, making it lean and focused on the *actual* analysis of the game.  I'm dealing with data that likely includes all sorts of noise – introductions, biographical details about the players, time stamps, and other irrelevant metadata.  My task is to surgically remove that and leave behind only the core of the discussion: the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.  It's about distilling the commentary into its purest form, something that an expert can quickly grasp and that accurately reflects the game's unfolding analysis. I need to be able to extract the very essence of the commentary, leaving only what is useful to someone who already knows their Shogi!
