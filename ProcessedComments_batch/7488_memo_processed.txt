**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been handed a bunch of commentary from shogi games, and the goal is to make it usable for serious analysis. I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and just keep the good stuff: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. That's the real meat of the analysis, the stuff that tells us *why* things are happening on the board.  It's about distilling the commentary down to its purest form, so I can use it to build a better understanding of the game.
