**Cleaning Up Those Messy Game Comments**

Okay, so I've been tasked with a bit of a cleanup job. It seems someone needs me to refine the commentary that comes with those old shogi game datasets.  The goal is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the noise and keep only the gold. We're talking about stripping out the fluff - all the meta-information, those unnecessary introductions to players, and even the timestamps.  What I'm really after is the heart of the matter - the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a way that actually flows and makes sense.  It's all about making the data more digestible and, frankly, useful for analysis. So, let's get down to brass tacks and figure out the best way to do this.
