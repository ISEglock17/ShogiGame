**Streamlining the Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these shogi commentary snippets. The goal is to get right to the heart of the game, no fluff. My instinct is to zero in on the *moves* themselves and the immediate *strategic context*. I need to filter out all the peripheral noise.

Specifically, I'm thinking I'll systematically remove anything that falls outside of the game's immediate narrative. That means any mention of people *other* than the players is out. References to previous matches or historical records? Gone. Clock readings, time controls, and any details about the time spent on a move? Deleted. The players' bios, their stats, their records? Useless. Any discussion about their general playing styles or the originators of certain strategies, also not helpful here. Finally, anything regarding the venue (location, weather, hotel), the meal breaks, and resumption of play are irrelevant to a clear analysis. I need to keep the commentary lean and focused on the board and the player's choices. This is about pure shogi strategy, after all.
