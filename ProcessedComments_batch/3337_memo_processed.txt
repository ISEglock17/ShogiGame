**Processing Shogi Commentary: Focusing on the Game's Essence**

Okay, so the goal is to refine these Shogi game commentaries, right? I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – the fluff, the biographical details, the time-stamps – and leave only the pure, distilled analysis of the board and the moves themselves. My mission is to reconstruct these comments into coherent, natural-sounding explanations of the game's unfolding strategy.

I've got a set of rules to guide me: anything that isn't directly related to the current position or the immediate move is on the chopping block. That means:

*   **No Names, Ranks, or Affiliations:** Anyone not directly involved in the current play, their background, or their associations are irrelevant.
*   **Forget the Past:** No references to previous games or precedents. The current game is the only context that matters.
*   **Time Flies By:** Get rid of any clock-related data, like time elapsed or remaining.
*   **Background Check? Nope!:**  Profiles, stats, ranks, player numbers – all distractions from the core of the analysis.
*   **Style Isn't Important:** Ignore any comments on playing styles, preferred openings, or any other trend descriptions.
*   **Location, Location, Location? Not at all!:** The location, weather, accommodation - nothing to do with the game play.

The challenge is to apply these rules consistently and elegantly. I need to make sure the summaries are accurate, logical, and flowing, yet completely devoid of unnecessary information. It's about distilling the essence of the game and presenting it in the most potent form possible.
