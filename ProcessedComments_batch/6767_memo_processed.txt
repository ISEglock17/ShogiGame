**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary**

Okay, so I've got this task: I need to take raw Shogi game commentary and surgically remove all the noise. My goal is laser-focused: I want clear, concise explanations of the moves and board states, without any of the fluff. Think of it like a distilled essence of the game, perfect for analysis.

I'm essentially building a filter. I've been given some specific "don'ts," and I'm going to carefully apply them to each comment. Anything that falls under the categories of biographical details, historical context (previous games, records), time references, player profiles (rank, affiliation, etc.), descriptions of playing style or preferred openings, and environmental information (location, weather, etc.) is going straight to the cutting room floor. My job is to leave only the strategic heart of the commentary, the pure mechanics of the game as they unfold on the board.
