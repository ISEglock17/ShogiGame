**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so the task at hand is to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty straightforward: strip away all the fluff and extraneous details – the meta stuff, the introductions, the timestamps – and leave only the valuable stuff. I want to streamline these comments into focused, natural explanations of the board state and the moves being made. The raw data contains so much noise that it's difficult to see the patterns.

Effectively, I'm aiming to transform these into concise, readable analyses of the game as it unfolds, free from unnecessary distractions that an expert would find irrelevant. I need to get to the core of the strategic reasoning behind each move without wading through biographical details or time elapsed. It's about distilling the essence of the Shogi commentary.
