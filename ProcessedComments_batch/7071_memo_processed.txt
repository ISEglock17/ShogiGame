**Structuring Chess Game Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm being asked to take raw commentary from a Shogi match and whip it into shape, to make it more digestible. I get it. The goal here is clarity and conciseness, right? My brain immediately jumps to the process.

First, I need to methodically read through each comment associated with a specific move. That's the foundation. Each move, each comment... I'll take it one step at a time.

Then, for each sentence within those comments, I've got to compare it against a set of predetermined "trash" rules. Basically, I'm filtering out the fluff, the irrelevant bits, the noise that distracts from the core insights of the move. I'm focusing on preserving the essential, strategic commentary. It's about surgically removing what doesn't matter, and ensuring the final output is polished and valuable to a fellow expert.
