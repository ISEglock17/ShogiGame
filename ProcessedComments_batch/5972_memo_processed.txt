**Cleaning Up My Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling a common issue with Shogi game data – the comment sections. Essentially, the user wants me to clean them up. My goal is straightforward: I need to take these often-cluttered comments and strip away the extraneous noise. Things like meta-information about the game, introductions of the players, time stamps, and any other commentary that doesn't directly contribute to explaining the board position or the reasoning behind a specific move needs to go.

The core idea is to distill the commentary down to its essential explanatory value. I'm essentially looking to extract the *core* Shogi analysis from the fluff. The focus will be on the *how* and *why* of the game, leaving behind concise, insightful explanations of the moves and the state of the board. This involves a clear process of identifying the irrelevant parts based on well-defined rules, and then systematically eliminating those sections, leaving only the valuable commentary behind.
