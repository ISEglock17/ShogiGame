**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm looking at Shogi game commentaries and I need to clean them up. The goal is clear: I want to transform the often messy commentary within Shogi game records into clear, focused analyses of the game itself.  I'm discarding all the fluff – no more metadata, player introductions, or time stamps. My primary concern is preserving the actual strategic insights: the explanation of board positions and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a fluid, natural language format. It's about distilling the core analysis down to its essential elements, making it perfect for in-depth kifu analysis.
