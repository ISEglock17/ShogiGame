**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from Shogi games. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw comments into something usable for my analysis. I want to strip out all the extraneous fluff, the metadata, the introductions, the clock updates - anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state and the moves being made.

Essentially, I want to be left with natural-sounding sentences that purely describe the board position or the reasoning behind a specific move. This will allow me to focus my efforts on understanding the *strategy* and *tactics* being discussed, rather than getting bogged down in irrelevant details. I'm keen to get started.
