Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these comments from Shogi game data. The goal is clear: strip out all the fluff and extraneous details, leaving only the pure, insightful commentary on the board state and the moves being made.  Think of it like refining ore – we're after the gold, not the surrounding rock.

Essentially, they're asking me to transform these often sprawling comment sections into concise, digestible analyses ready for deeper, data-driven exploration.

The process is straightforward enough:
1.  **Rule-Based Filtering:** I'll need to define a robust set of rules to identify and eliminate the non-essential stuff – the meta information, the player introductions, the time-stamps... anything that doesn't directly illuminate the tactical or strategic thinking behind the moves themselves.

2.  **Natural Language Preservation:** The important aspect is retaining the flow of the commentary. We're looking to maintain the language of the expert analysts of the game.
