My plan is pretty straightforward. I'll need to go through the commentary step-by-step, move by move. For each turn, I'll carefully analyze the text, identifying what's genuinely valuable for understanding the game's progression. This means I'll be sifting through the noise to find the nuggets of insightful discussion about positions, tactics, and potential future developments. I'll need to be ruthless in cutting out anything that doesn't directly contribute to a clear understanding of the game's unfolding narrative. This is all about precision and focus – leaving behind only the essential elements of the Shogi analysis.
