So, here's how I see it unfolding:

1.  First, I'll need a set of defined rules. This is crucial for systematically identifying and removing irrelevant information within the comments. Think of it as developing a filter, a way to quickly recognize and discard the noise.
2.  Once the irrelevant parts are filtered out, the remaining content needs to be polished. I need to take these fragments of insights and stitch them together into natural, coherent sentences. It's about ensuring clarity, preserving the original intent of the commentator, and making the insights readily accessible for in-depth analysis.

Essentially, I'm transforming raw commentary into a streamlined and focused narrative, ready for deeper examination of the game.
