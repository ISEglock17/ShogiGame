My task is pretty clear: they want me to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, timestamps, everything that doesn't directly relate to the board position or the moves themselves. My goal is to leave only the valuable, insightful commentary that explains the moves and the evolving strategic landscape of the game, presented in a smooth, readable narrative format. Think of it like distilling the essence of the analysis, the purest form of strategic insight.
