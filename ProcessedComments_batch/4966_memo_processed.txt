**Processing Shogi Game Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform these chatty, sometimes rambling commentary streams into concise, readable explanations of the actual game. Think of it like stripping away the fluff – the intros, the "hello everyone," the time checks, and the biographical details about the players – and leaving behind the pure, distilled essence of the strategy and the moves themselves.

My process will be straightforward, really. I'll take it one move at a time, scrutinizing each comment against the given rules. Anything that doesn't directly contribute to the *understanding* of the board state or the reasoning behind a particular move gets the axe. I'll need to be ruthless but fair, making sure not to accidentally filter out anything that helps elucidate the thinking behind the play. It's about distilling the strategic intent. I'm excited to dive in and refine the comments.
