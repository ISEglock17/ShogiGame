**Streamlining Commentary for Sharper Insights**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Go commentary files. The goal here is laser-focused: strip out all the fluff and extraneous details – the meta chatter, the biographical snippets, the time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, distilled analysis of the board and the moves themselves, all presented in fluent, natural language.

I need to process each comment individually, applying a specific set of rules. It's like a surgical procedure, really, removing the unwanted elements with precision.

Here's the breakdown of my process:

1.  **Identification and Removal:** I'll meticulously scan each comment, identifying anything that falls under the "delete" category based on the provided rules. This includes anything about people other than the players, references to past games, time-related information, player profiles, ranks, titles, etc. – anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the current board state or the reasoning behind a specific move is out.
2.  **Language Refinement:** Once the irrelevant bits are gone, I'll focus on what remains, massaging the language into clear, concise, and grammatically sound sentences. The goal is a readable and insightful commentary that flows smoothly.
3.  **Organized Output:**  Finally, I'll output each cleaned comment, alongside the move number and a concise explanation of *why* certain parts were removed. This is important for transparency and to ensure the process's accuracy. This approach streamlines the data for effective analysis and insights.
