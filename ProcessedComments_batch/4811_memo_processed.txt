That means ditching any metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't contribute to understanding the board state or the moves being made. Essentially, I'm aiming to leave behind a seamless narrative that focuses solely on the tactical and strategic reasoning behind each move. This will allow for much cleaner analysis of the game. Now, let's figure out the best way to do this...
