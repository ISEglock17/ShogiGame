**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I've been given this Shogi game data from the user – essentially, a transcript of moves with accompanying comments. My task is to take this raw data and transform it, based on some specific rules, into something much more focused and useful.

My goal here is pretty straightforward: to filter out all the noise. I need to get rid of anything that isn't directly related to the actual game analysis. That means stripping away all the meta-information, introductions, player bios, timing details, anything that doesn't contribute to understanding the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Ultimately, I want a clean, natural-sounding narrative that solely focuses on the positions on the board and the explanations of the plays.
