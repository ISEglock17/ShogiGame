Think of it as distilling the essence of the game from the fluff.

Once those rules are in place, I can systematically go through each comment, apply the rules, and excise the irrelevant bits, leaving behind concise, natural-sounding explanations of the strategic thinking behind the moves. This should give a cleaner data set, easier to analyze for pattern recognition, and ultimately, better analysis.
