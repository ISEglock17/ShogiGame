**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Right, so I'm looking at this dataset of shogi game commentaries. The goal, as I understand it, is to get down to the *real* meat of the analysis. I need to strip away all the extraneous fluff – the metadata, the introductions to the players, the time stamps... all that stuff that's just noise for a deep dive into the strategic elements.

My process will be methodical. First, I'll go through the comments and apply a set of rules, a filter if you will, to identify and ruthlessly eliminate any irrelevant information. Once I've excised all the excess, the remaining text will be the core insights into the board positions and the player's moves.

Then comes the finesse. I'll need to refashion the remaining commentary, the nuggets of strategic wisdom, into properly formed sentences.  It's about making sure the flow of analysis remains, even without all the padding that we've removed.  It is about turning rough notes into polished analysis.
