**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, right? My task is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the fluff and leave behind the actual good stuff - the juicy analysis of the board positions and the moves made. Think of it like stripping away the excess fat from a prime cut of meat.

Essentially, I need to identify and excise anything that isn't directly related to the game's unfolding strategy. That means names and ranks of everyone *except* the players involved, any references to previous games or precedents, and of course, all that pesky time information. It's like curating a museum exhibit - you want the focus to be solely on the art, not the artist's biography or the opening hours.
