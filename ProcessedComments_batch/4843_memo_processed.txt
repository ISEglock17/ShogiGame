**Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of shogi game commentaries, and the goal is clear: I need to clean it up for analysis. There's a lot of noise in there, things that, frankly, just clutter up the important stuff. I'm talking about all the metadata – who the players are, their backgrounds, the time each move took...it's just not relevant to the actual game state or the moves themselves. My goal is to extract the juicy bits: the actual explanations of what's happening on the board, the reasoning behind each move, and any strategic insights. I want the commentary to read as a coherent narrative of the game, free from all that extraneous fluff. It's about distilling the commentary down to its essence, the pure, insightful analysis of the gameplay.
