**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game records. The goal here is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the fluff. I need to strip out anything that isn't directly related to the actual game's progress – the meta stuff, like who the players are, their backgrounds, the time the game took... that's all irrelevant. My primary focus is the *analysis*: I want to leave behind only the clear, natural explanations of the board state and the moves being made. Essentially, I'm streamlining the commentary to get to the core of the tactical and strategic insights.
