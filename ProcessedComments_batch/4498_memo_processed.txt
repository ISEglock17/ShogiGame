**Structuring and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with taking raw commentary from Shogi game data and turning it into something clean and useful. Essentially, I need to sift through these comments and retain only the insightful bits related to the board position and moves themselves, while ditching anything that's just noise for my analysis.

Here's how I'm thinking of tackling this:

1.  **Iterate through Each Move:** I'll process the commentary step-by-step, move by move, so I don't miss anything.
2.  **Apply the Rules:** I'll meticulously check each comment against the pre-defined rules. I need to be *very* precise here, as the accuracy of my analysis depends on this. Based on the rules, I'll identify and excise the redundant parts.
3.  **Refine the Language:** Once the irrelevant parts are gone, I'll need to polish the remaining text. Make sure the remaining phrases are coherent, and sound natural.
4.  **Organize the Output:** Lastly, I'll need to format everything properly. I'll need to clearly show which move I'm referencing, the cleaned-up commentary, and the specific reason why I eliminated certain parts. This is vital for maintaining transparency and understanding what was changed and why.
