**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw commentary into coherent, insightful analyses of the game itself. No fluff, no extraneous details. Think of it as distilling the essence of the game's evolution into something that *I*, an expert, would find valuable for deeper analysis.

My plan of attack is clear. First, I'll meticulously go through the comments, following a set of rules I'll define. Those rules will dictate what to throw out. That means all the meta-stuff - player introductions, timing, and commentary that doesn't directly relate to what's happening on the board or the significance of a move - it's got to go.

Once that initial sweep is done, I'll focus on polishing what remains. I want to ensure the commentary reads smoothly and naturally. It's not just about eliminating the noise; it's about making sure the core insights, the crucial explanations of the positions and moves, are presented in the most accessible and logical way possible. It's about crafting the narrative of the game, move by move.
