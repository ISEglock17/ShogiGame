**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task in analyzing shogi data: cleaning up the commentary. I'm looking at these game logs, and while the play itself is fascinating, the accompanying commentary is often a mixed bag. There's a lot of noise in there – introductions to the commentators, time stamps, irrelevant background details... it's all distracting when I'm trying to zero in on the strategic nuances.

My goal is to streamline this information. I need to strip away everything that isn't directly related to the board position and the move-by-move analysis. Essentially, I'm aiming to extract the *core* insights from the commentary, presented in a clear, flowing narrative. I want to keep the explanations of the board state, the justifications for specific moves, and the potential tactical implications... but lose the fluff. It's about getting to the meat of the discussion, making the commentary a more efficient and impactful resource for understanding the game.
