**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I've seen before: someone wants me to clean up the commentary from a shogi game. They've got a dataset full of these move-by-move annotations, and they're asking me to refine them. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform those raw comments into something readable, something that focuses only on the crucial elements - the state of the board, the moves played, and the strategic reasoning behind them. Essentially, I'm stripping away all the noise, the metadata, the introductions, the clock information, and leaving behind only the pure essence of the analysis. It's about distilling the meaning down to its most impactful form.
