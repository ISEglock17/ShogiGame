**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I need to refine a set of game comments from a Shogi match dataset. The goal here is pretty straightforward, but crucial for my analysis. I want to strip out all the noise – the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps – you know, the stuff that's not directly related to the actual game state or the moves themselves.

My intention is to have the comment stream down to the bare essentials: clear, concise explanations of the board position and the rationale behind each move. Ultimately, this will give me a clean, natural language stream that I can easily integrate into my Shogi analysis workflow. It's all about making the data work *for* me, instead of having to wade through irrelevant details. This kind of clean data is the foundation of really insightful modeling and pattern detection, and that is what I am after.
