**Structuring and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with taking this raw data from a Shogi game and making it clean and useful. Essentially, I've got a bunch of comments jumbled in with the move notations, and my job is to filter out the noise. It's all about streamlining the analysis.

My thinking is, I'll go through the comments, one at a time, in the order they appear.  I've got a set of rules – my "delete list," if you will – that define what's considered extraneous.  I'll compare each comment to these rules and decide: does it stay, or does it get deleted? The goal here is to keep the gold – the insightful analysis of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Everything else is expendable. This will result in a much cleaner, more focused dataset, ready for serious study.
