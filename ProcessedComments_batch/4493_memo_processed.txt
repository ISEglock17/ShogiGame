**Streamlining Game Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task: to clean up these Shogi game commentaries. The goal? To take this raw data, this potentially noisy stream of comments accompanying each move, and refine it into something usable for my serious analysis. I need to focus on the *core* – the insights into the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Think of it like a sculptor chipping away at marble to reveal the statue within.

I'm essentially stripping away all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of the extraneous details that are cluttering things up. Things like:

*   **Irrelevant Names:** I don't care who's watching the game, who they're affiliated with, or their past relationships with the players.
*   **Historical Detours:** Forget about that previous game. I'm focusing on *this* game, *this* moment.
*   **Time-Related Noise:** The clock is irrelevant to my analytical process. I'm only concerned with the *thinking* behind the moves.

Basically, I want to filter out everything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the strategy and tactics unfolding on the board. I need the raw, unadulterated essence of the game to work with.
