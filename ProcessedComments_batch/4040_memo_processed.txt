My thought process is going to be pretty systematic. First, I'll dive into each move's commentary, dissecting it bit by bit. I'll need to define what's considered "noise" – things to be removed – based on the given rules. As I identify those elements, I'll jot down the reason for removal and the rule number that justifies it.

Once the extraneous parts are marked for deletion, I'll then carefully reconstruct the remaining pieces into a smooth, readable sentence. I need to make sure the core information remains intact, while simultaneously making sure that the final product is easy to understand in the context of the game.
