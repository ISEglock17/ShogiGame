**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

I'm after those insightful explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a natural, flowing way.

Essentially, I'm streamlining the data for more effective algorithmic analysis. The rules for what to eliminate are straightforward. First, I need to get rid of any extraneous biographical information about the players, like their names, ranks, or details about their training lineage. That just distracts from the core strategic thinking.

And lastly, any timestamps or time-related information needs to be scrubbed. These are important for human understanding the pressure of time, but for the analytical algorithms I'm interested in, they just introduce unnecessary variables. Let's get to the true meat of the strategic analysis.
