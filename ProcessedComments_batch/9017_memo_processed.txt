**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Right now, they're cluttered with all sorts of things – meta information, introductions to the players, timing details... all the fluff that gets in the way of the core insights about the board and the moves themselves.

My job is to surgically remove that noise. I need to distill each comment down to its essence: the actual explanation of *why* a move was played, what the player was thinking, and what the strategic implications are. I want these comments to be pure Shogi strategy, presented as natural, coherent explanations. It's about getting to the meat of the matter.
