**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game records, and I need to get the commentary into shape. My goal here is laser-focused: I want to strip away all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of all the meta-information, those introductions to the players, the time stamps... basically, anything that isn't directly relevant to *understanding* the board state and the move being made. I want the core of the commentary, the actual *analysis*, to shine through. I want to be left with clean, flowing explanations of the positions and the reasoning behind each move, as if I were reading a condensed but insightful master's notes. This will allow for much deeper and more efficient analysis.
