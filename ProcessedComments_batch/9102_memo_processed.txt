**Focusing the Analysis: Streamlining Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task in my work with shogi data. The user wants me to clean up and refine the comments associated with each move in a game. My primary objective here is to isolate and preserve only the valuable bits – the actual explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. I need to strip away all the extraneous noise: the meta-information, the player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that's not directly contributing to the strategic analysis. The goal is to leave behind clear, natural language descriptions that truly illuminate the game's unfolding. It's about getting to the heart of the strategic thinking without all the clutter. This is an important step in prepping data for further in-depth analysis.
