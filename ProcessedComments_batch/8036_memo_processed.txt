**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: they need to strip away all the fluff and extraneous details to leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board and the moves. Think of it like a distilled essence of the game.

My job is to go through each comment, move by move, and make surgical cuts based on a specific set of rules. I need to automatically recognize and delete anything that's not directly related to the position or the immediate tactical implications.

Specifically, I need to excise any mention of people *other* than the players (no names, ranks, or mentorship information), anything referencing past games or precedents, any timing information, and of course, all the background noise about the players' profiles, records, ranks, and so on. It's about concentrating the mind on *this* game, *this* moment.

It's a matter of eliminating the noise and revealing the signal within. Ultimately, the objective is to create a focused, analytical stream of commentary.
