Right now, they're filled with extra noise – stuff like meta-information, introductions to the players, and time stamps.  My job is to go through each comment, identify the irrelevant parts based on a set of rules, and remove them.  Ideally, what's left will be a concise, natural-sounding explanation of the board position and the move that was played, making it easier to extract key insights.
