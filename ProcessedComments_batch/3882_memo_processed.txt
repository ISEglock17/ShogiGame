**Processing & Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up Shogi commentary, right?  Essentially, they're giving me a set of rules to follow to make it more concise and focused. I see the main goals are to remove extraneous information. First up, anything that's not directly related to the current players – names, ranks, who their teacher is, all of that needs to go. It's distracting from the game itself, which is what we want to highlight.

Secondly, I need to get rid of any historical context or references to previous games. That whole "In the past, X did Y..." or "This mirrors the Z game..." stuff? It doesn't belong in the immediate commentary. It might be interesting in a separate analysis, but not in the live game breakdown. Focusing on what's happening *now* is key.
