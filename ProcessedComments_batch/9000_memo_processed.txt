**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up commentary from shogi games. The goal is to isolate the *actual* analysis of the moves and board state. We need to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it. We're left with pure, concise, and natural explanations of what's happening on the board, and why. Think of it as distilling the essence of the game's strategic discussion into its most potent form for analysis. The details of how this will be accomplished come next.
