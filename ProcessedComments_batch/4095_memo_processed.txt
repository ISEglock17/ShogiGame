**Cleaning Up Those Pesky Comments**

Alright, so the user wants me to whip the comments from shogi game data into shape. I get it - those raw comments are often a mess. Filled with all sorts of noise that I, as an expert, don't need cluttering up the analysis. They're asking me to filter out the junk.

The goal is pretty straightforward: extract the valuable stuff – the actual explanations of the board state and the move being played – and discard all the fluff. Things like who's speaking, how much time's passed, any introductory biographical tidbits, and anything that's not directly relevant to *the game itself*. I want those remaining explanations to flow naturally, forming coherent, focused descriptions of the unfolding strategy. So, in effect, transforming the raw data into something I can really use. This kind of cleanup is a necessary first step towards any serious analysis.
