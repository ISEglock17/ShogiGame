**Cleaning Up Game Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up comments from Shogi game data, huh? Basically, they want me to filter out all the fluff. I get it. We're talking about taking those raw comments - the ones you find scattered around the game recordings - and making them lean, mean, and relevant for *serious* analysis. 

What they're after is just the essential stuff: the board positions, the explanations of moves, the strategic thinking.  All that other stuff? The player introductions, the time stamps, the meta-commentary? It's just noise, as far as they're concerned. They want a clean narrative.  I can totally see the value in that, especially when you're trying to dig deep into the nuances of a game. Sounds like a fun challenge. Let's see how we can tackle this.
