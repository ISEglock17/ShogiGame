**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform raw commentary into something useful for *my* analytical work. That means stripping out all the noise – the meta stuff, player introductions, time stamps... anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind specific moves. I'm focusing on the *essence* of the commentary.

Essentially, I'll be working in these steps:

1.  **Filtering:** I'll apply a set of rules, based on my knowledge and experience, to identify and remove all the extraneous information. This is where I really get to leverage my understanding of what's *actually* important for analysis.
2.  **Smoothing:** After the filtering, I'll rework what's left into coherent, natural sentences. The aim here is not just brevity, but readability and understandability. I want this information to be immediately useful when I'm reviewing the game.
