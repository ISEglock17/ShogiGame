**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary, and the goal is to clean it up. The task is to transform it into something truly valuable for my analysis. Essentially, I need to strip away all the extraneous noise, the meta-data, the player introductions, and the timestamps. My objective is to distill the commentary down to its purest form: concise, natural-sounding explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. I want to retain the core insights and tactical reasoning.
