**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform the raw comments into something useful for analyzing the game itself.  Basically, I want to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know, the stuff that doesn't actually help me understand the board or the moves. I need the meat and potatoes of the commentary – the actual insights into the position and the reasoning behind each play.

So, I'll be meticulously examining each comment, applying the specified rules to determine what's essential and what's expendable. It's a bit like surgical precision, I'm cutting away the extraneous details to reveal the underlying strategic narrative of the game.
