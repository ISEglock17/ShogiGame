**The Challenge of Polishing Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with the task of cleaning up some Shogi game commentary. Basically, someone wants me to take this raw, potentially messy stream of comments and transform it into something presentable, something that flows naturally.  It's like taking a rough draft and turning it into a polished piece.  The core idea is to follow the given rules - I know what those are - and basically trim away all the unnecessary details and fluff. My goal is to extract the essential information from each comment related to a move.
