**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I've dealt with countless times: cleaning up game commentary.  My goal, as always, is to extract the *real* insights from a sea of noise.  This user wants me to take raw commentary data from Shogi games and reshape it into something lean, something focused.  They want only the essentials for deep analysis.

Specifically, I need to strip away all the fluff. That means the metadata – the who, when, and where.  Forget the player introductions and the time spent on each move.  That's all extraneous.  What truly matters are the board states and the rationale behind each move. I need to leave only those clear, concise explanations of why a particular move was made and how it impacts the game.  Essentially, I'm aiming for the kind of commentary that a seasoned Grandmaster would find valuable when reviewing a game, distilled down to its purest form.
