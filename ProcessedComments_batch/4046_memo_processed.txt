**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up commentary from shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to extract the essence of the game analysis, transforming it into clear, natural prose. I want to ditch the noise, the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps – all the stuff that clogs up the signal and get straight to the meat of the commentary.  Essentially, I'm focusing on the actual board positions and the rationale behind each move.  That's where the real insight lies, and that's what I want to highlight. I have a clear plan for how to achieve this.
