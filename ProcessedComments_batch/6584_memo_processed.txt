My job is to transform these raw comments into a clean, concise, and natural explanation of the game's unfolding strategy.

The plan is straightforward: I'll use a set of defined rules to systematically identify and remove the unwanted information. This will involve careful parsing of the text, looking for specific patterns and keywords that indicate elements ripe for removal. The goal is a distilled version that's easier to digest, more focused on the analysis itself, and generally more useful for studying and learning.
