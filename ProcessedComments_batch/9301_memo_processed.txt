**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common challenge: a raw data dump of Shogi game commentary that's cluttered with all sorts of extraneous noise. My ultimate goal here is to streamline this text into something truly usable for in-depth game analysis. I need to strip away everything that's not directly related to the actual moves and board states. Think of it like a meticulous archaeologist brushing away the sand to reveal the artifact.

Basically, I need to methodically comb through this commentary and follow a set of strict rules: identifying and then removing all those annoying bits of metadata, player introductions, time stamps – you know, the stuff that's important for the *experience* of watching the game, but completely irrelevant for the *analysis* of it. Then, and this is crucial, I need to stitch together what's left into coherent, naturally flowing sentences. The goal is to get a narrative about the game's evolution from the commentary, not just an assembly of disjointed phrases.  This cleaned-up version will be much more useful for the type of granular research I like to do.
