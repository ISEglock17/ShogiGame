**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

The goal is to strip away all the extraneous stuff – metadata, introductions, time stamps, you name it – and just keep the juicy bits: the actual analysis of the board position and the moves. They want natural, flowing explanations, not a cluttered mess.

Basically, I need to focus on retaining the core chess-related analysis. It's a matter of identifying and systematically eliminating any comment sections that aren't providing valuable insights into the game itself. It is a refinement process, a process of removing unwanted comments and retaining insightful comments.
