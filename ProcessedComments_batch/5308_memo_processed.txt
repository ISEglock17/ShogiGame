**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw commentary into something useful for in-depth analysis of the game.  I'm basically stripping away all the fluff – you know, the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, the usual noise that doesn't contribute to understanding the *moves* themselves and their rationale. I'm focusing on preserving the actual game analysis and explanations of the board state and the player's intentions.

Essentially, I'll be going through the comments, identifying elements that fit the deletion criteria, and then removing them according to the defined rules. The idea is to leave behind only the truly insightful commentary that sheds light on the game's evolution, move-by-move.  This allows me, and anyone else analyzing the data, to focus directly on the strategic content.
