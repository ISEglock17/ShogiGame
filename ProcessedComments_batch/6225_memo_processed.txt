**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I need to take these Shogi game commentaries and really refine them. The user wants me to focus specifically on the essential parts: the board positions and the moves being made, and the *explanations* of those moves.  Everything else – the fluff, the introductions of the players, the timestamps, the general meta-commentary – that's all noise I need to cut out. My goal is to transform these comments into concise, flowing descriptions of the game's evolution, ideal for analyzing the strategy and tactics.  It's about distilling the essence, the core tactical narrative.
