Essentially, I'm aiming to transform these raw comments into concise, flowing explanations that are truly valuable for further analysis. It's about distilling the essence of the game's unfolding narrative, leaving behind the extraneous details. I'll need to meticulously analyze each line, judging it against the established rules, to ensure only the meaningful insights remain. This is all about enhancing the value of the commentary for my own Shogi research.
