**Cleaning Up Those Pesky Comments**

Alright, so I'm tasked with a bit of a cleanup job here. It seems the user wants me to go through these comments attached to shogi game data and whip them into shape. The goal, as I understand it, is to extract the good stuff – the actual insightful commentary on the board state and the moves played – and ditch all the noise. Think of it as refining the analysis, getting rid of the fluff that clutters things up.

The process itself sounds straightforward enough. I've got to define some rules, some criteria, for what constitutes "unnecessary information." That probably means things like introductions to the players, time stamps, any meta-commentary *about* the comment itself rather than the game. Anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the position or the reasoning behind a specific move is on the chopping block. Basically, I'm going to surgically remove all the distractions, leaving behind a clear and concise narrative of the game's unfolding strategy. Sounds like a good challenge!
