**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with raw Shogi commentary that needs some serious cleaning up.  The goal, as I understand it, is to transform these comments into something polished and readable, removing any fluff and sticking to the core elements of the game's evolution.  Essentially, I need to analyze each comment and make it flow seamlessly.

My process will be straightforward and methodical. I'm going to parse through the commentary one move at a time, processing each comment sequentially. For every sentence within a comment, I will apply a set of pre-defined rules. These rules act as filters, identifying and removing extraneous information -- things that distract from understanding the board state or the significance of the move. I'll focus on retaining only the key tactical insights and descriptions of piece movements. I need to make the comments concise, and accurate, without all the extras that clog up the typical text!
