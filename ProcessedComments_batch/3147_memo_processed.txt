The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform the jumble of notes into clear, concise explanations of the moves and board states. It's about stripping away all the extraneous stuff – the metadata, player introductions, timestamps, you name it – and focusing solely on the actual analysis of the game. My aim is to leave behind only the natural, well-structured explanations of each move and the strategic considerations behind it, for the benefit of us experts.
