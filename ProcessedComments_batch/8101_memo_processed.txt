**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine these comments, right? I want to strip away all the extraneous fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps... anything that's not directly related to the actual analysis of the board position and the moves played. The ideal outcome is a set of annotations that read smoothly, focusing solely on the strategic rationale behind each move and the evolving board state. Basically, I want to make them clear and concise explanations of the gameplay, for easy digestion.
