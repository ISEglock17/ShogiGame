**Focusing My Efforts: Refining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I need to hone in on this Shogi commentary data. The goal is clear: I need to transform the raw comments associated with game records into something truly useful for analysis.  Right now, they're cluttered with extraneous details that just muddy the waters. Think introductions, player backgrounds, time stamps... that's all noise I need to filter out.  

Essentially, I'm aiming to extract the *core* of the commentary – the insightful explanations of board positions and move selections.  I need to retain only the natural language explanations of what's happening on the board, nothing more, nothing less.

The process, at least initially, seems straightforward enough. It breaks down into a process of identifying and systematically deleting the irrelevant information according to a clear set of rules. I have a feeling this will involve some clever pattern matching and probably a good dose of NLP techniques.  The real challenge, I suspect, will be in defining those rules precisely and ensuring I don't accidentally throw out the baby with the bathwater – that is, inadvertently stripping away something crucial to understanding the flow of the game.
