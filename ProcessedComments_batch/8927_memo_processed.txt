**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, which is pretty standard in my line of work: cleaning up and standardizing game data. Specifically, I need to take these raw comments from Shogi game data – you know, the ones that come attached to each move – and refine them. The goal here is pretty clear: I want to strip out all the noise. Things like introductions, time stamps, mentions of who's playing, or any extraneous details that aren't actually *about* the board state or the move itself.

I'm focused on leaving behind the pure, distilled essence of the analysis. That means keeping only the parts of the comment that explain the reasoning behind a specific move or describe the resulting board position. It's about preserving the *strategic* insights, the critical thinking, and the tactical considerations.

So, in essence, I'm aiming for concise, naturally flowing explanations that are ready for further analysis or integration into a curated dataset.
