**Analyzing and Refactoring Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to take raw commentary from a Shogi game and clean it up, making it easier to read and understand. Basically, the user wants me to act as a Shogi commentator, but a really, really efficient one!

Here's my thought process:

First, I need to understand that the commentary is broken down move by move. That makes sense, Shogi is all about individual turns. I'll read through the comments one at a time.

Now, I'll need to use some pre-defined rules to identify what's irrelevant – anything that doesn't add to the analysis of the board or the moves themselves. That's the heart of the cleaning process.

The rules are key. Based on these rules, I'll delete information or maybe even replace certain phrases with cleaner versions. I need to be precise, as even the slightest shift can change the meaning of the comment.

After I've eliminated the fluff, I'll rephrase the core information into natural, fluent sentences. That will make the commentary readable for the Shogi expert.

Finally, I need to present the cleaned-up comment. I'll include the reasons for each specific change and what I removed. It is essential to be as accurate as possible for the users.
