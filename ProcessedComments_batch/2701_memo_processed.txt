**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up some game annotations from a Shogi dataset. The goal is pretty clear: get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player intros, timestamps – and just leave the good stuff. I want to retain the natural language explanations of the board positions and the moves themselves. Basically, I need to turn this raw data into something useful for analyzing the games.  It sounds like a relatively straightforward task, though the devil is always in the details, especially when dealing with the nuances of language. I'll need to figure out exactly *how* I'm going to do this.
