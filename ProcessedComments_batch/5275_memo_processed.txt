**Processing and Refining Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. The goal is to make these comments much more useful for analysis. I need to get rid of all the fluff – any metadata, intros, time stamps, you know, all that stuff that gets in the way of a clear understanding of the board and the moves. Essentially, I'm transforming raw commentary into something concise and insightful, ready for deeper analysis.

Here's how I'm going about it:

1.  **Filtering:** I'll carefully go through each comment, applying my established rules to identify and remove all the extraneous information. This is where I really have to be sharp, ensuring I keep only the truly insightful commentary about the positions and moves.
2.  **Structuring and Rewriting:** Once I have the relevant content, I'll rewrite it, making sure it flows naturally as a sentence. I want these comments to read seamlessly, and for that, I'll need to do some rewriting of the original text.
3.  **Formatting:** Finally, I'll output the processed comment in the specified format: each comment begins with a line indicating the move number, followed by the processed comment. After that, I include a section detailing the reasons for any removals, referencing the specific rule numbers I applied. This is important for traceability and to help users understand the rationale behind the edits.
