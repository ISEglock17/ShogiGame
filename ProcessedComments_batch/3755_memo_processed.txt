**Processing Game Commentary for Strategic Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task. Someone wants to refine the commentary within Shogi game data. The goal is clear: strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the core strategic insights: the board state explanations and the analysis of each move. It's about distilling the essence of the game's evolution into a clear, concise narrative focused solely on tactical considerations.

My approach is straightforward. I'll need to meticulously process each commentary segment, move by move, carefully examining each piece of text against a set of predefined rules. The primary function will be to identify and excise any information that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the positional and strategic implications of the game. It's a matter of surgical precision; removing the extraneous while preserving the valuable commentary that sheds light on the player's reasoning and the strategic landscape of the board.
