**Focusing the Data for Sharper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one: refining chess commentary from game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to distill the often verbose commentary down to its essential components. I need to strip away the extraneous noise – the metadata, player introductions, timestamps, all that – leaving behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves.

My process will be methodical, guided by the provided rules. I'll need to carefully examine each comment associated with a move, identifying the information that's dispensable and removing it with precision. It's about preserving the context, the flow of ideas, the *why* behind the moves, not the peripheral details. This will allow for a much cleaner and more efficient workflow for the next stage, the actual analysis of the game.
