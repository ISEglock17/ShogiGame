**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game commentary and my goal is to really refine it. The goal here is to strip away all the extraneous noise – things like the pre-game introductions, player bios, even timestamps, and other metadata – and get down to the core of what's *actually* valuable for analysis: the on-the-board descriptions and move-by-move explanations. Think about it, all that fluff just clogs up the signal. I want clean, concise, flowing text that focuses solely on the tactical and strategic nuances of the game as they unfold. Basically, I want to transform these raw comments into something that's elegant and insightful. I need to make the comments more usable for my research purposes.
