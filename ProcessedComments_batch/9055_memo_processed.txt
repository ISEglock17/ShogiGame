**Refining Commentary in Shogi Game Data**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my goal is clear: I need to clean it up. The user wants the messy parts removed, leaving only the good stuff - the actual analysis of the board state and the moves.

Specifically, I need to get rid of the fluff: metadata, introductions of the players, time stamps... anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding *why* a move was made. The task is to identify those bits, delete them, and then rephrase what's left into something that flows smoothly, a proper explanation of the game's evolution. It's a bit like taking a dense academic paper and distilling it down to the essential arguments. I see this as a chance to demonstrate my expertise in this domain, by crafting elegant and informative summaries from these sometimes-verbose commentaries.
