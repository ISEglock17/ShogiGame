**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The user wants me to sift through the comments attached to each move and extract only the juicy bits – the actual tactical analysis and explanations of the board state.  The goal is to get rid of all the fluff: the metadata, the player introductions, the time stamps... basically anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the game's unfolding strategy.

Essentially, I need to methodically go through each comment associated with a move and carefully decide what to keep and what to discard, following the user's specific guidelines. It's a bit like being an editor, but for Shogi analysis! I need to ensure that the remaining text reads smoothly and naturally, providing insights into the thinking behind each move.
