Essentially, I want to filter the noise and just get to the core of the commentary: the explanations of the board positions and the rationale behind each move. I'm focusing on preserving the *natural flow* of the explanation, so it reads like a coherent discussion of the game, rather than a jumble of disconnected facts. That means focusing on the tactical and strategic reasoning behind the moves – the "why" and "how" of the game as it unfolds.
