Okay, so I'm dealing with a lot of Shogi commentary data and need to get it into a clean, usable format. Basically, I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps – and just keep the good stuff, the actual insights into the board state and the moves. The goal is to distill the commentary down to its essential explanatory content, written naturally and ready for further analysis. This involves a clear process, which will be followed by more refinements.
