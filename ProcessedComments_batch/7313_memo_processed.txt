**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this mountain of Shogi commentary data and thinking, "There's gold in these hills, but it's buried under a lot of noise." The user's request is clear: they want a refined dataset, stripped of the fluff that clogs up useful analysis. My goal is to extract the essence of each comment - the actual explanation of the board state and the moves made – and present it as clear, coherent prose.  This means ruthlessly excising those tangential remarks about who's playing, how long the game's been going, or any extraneous details that don't directly illuminate the tactical or strategic thinking behind the moves.  Essentially, I'm aiming for a streamlined, focused narrative that gets straight to the heart of the game's unfolding drama, allowing for focused, in-depth analysis of the decision-making process.  This will transform the data from a messy collection of notes into a powerful tool for understanding Shogi at a deeper level.
