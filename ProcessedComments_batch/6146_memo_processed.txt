**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Game Analysis**

Okay, so the goal here is to refine the comments within a Shogi game dataset. I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta-data, the player introductions, time stamps – and leave only the pure, insightful commentary on the board state and the moves themselves. That's the real value, the heart of the analysis.

I've got a set of rules, clearly defined, to guide me. The task is to carefully comb through each comment and apply those filters. Let's see... I'll need to remove any mention of third-party names, ranks, or affiliations. Descriptions referencing past games or precedents are out. Time-related details? Gone. Player biographies, records, ranks, IDs, schools... all unnecessary baggage. Even opinions on a player's style or tendencies, and their preferred opening moves are getting the cut. It's all about making the commentary laser-focused and conducive to pure strategic understanding.
