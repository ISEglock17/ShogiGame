**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary data, and the goal is to transform it into something much more focused and useful. Basically, I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff – the metadata, the introductions, any mention of time elapsed – and get down to the bare bones: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves being played. I want to keep the commentary that provides *insight* into the game, but presented as a cohesive, readable narrative. Think of it like a distilled version of the game's story, with just the crucial plot points.
