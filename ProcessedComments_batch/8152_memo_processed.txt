**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's right up my alley. Someone wants me to sift through these comments attached to Shogi game records. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff, the introductory remarks, the time stamps, the character introductions – anything that isn't directly relevant to the actual board position or the move-by-move explanation.  Essentially, I need to distill the essence of the commentary into pure, digestible analysis.

This means focusing solely on the strategic insights, the tactical reasoning behind each move, and the resulting board state. The language should feel natural and flow logically, as if I'm reading a continuous analysis. The rest, well, it's just noise I need to filter out. It's about getting to the meat of the matter: what happened on the board and why it mattered. This is basic data cleaning and will require some fine-tuning.
