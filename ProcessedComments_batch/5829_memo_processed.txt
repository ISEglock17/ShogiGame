My task is to extract only the essentials and ditch the fluff.

My process will be methodical, step-by-step. First, I'll meticulously read through each and every comment associated with each move. Then, based on the specific rules I've been given – rules I'm assuming are tailored to capture the analytical heart of Shogi commentary – I'll dissect each comment.

My output will be twofold: the refined, polished comment that actually *matters* to the analysis, and a note explaining *why* I removed anything else. This way, I'll be left with a dataset that's ready for a deeper dive, free from irrelevant chatter and focused purely on the game's strategic and tactical core.
