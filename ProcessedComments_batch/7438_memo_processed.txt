They want to be left with pure, insightful explanations of what's happening and why, presented in a smooth, natural-sounding way.

My job, as I understand it, is to take each commentary snippet and work through it systematically, applying the given rules. First, I need to identify and remove all the unnecessary information based on those rules. Then, and this is key, I have to take what's left – the core strategic insight – and rephrase it into a grammatically correct, understandable sentence. That's the challenge. The goal, to extract signal from noise.
