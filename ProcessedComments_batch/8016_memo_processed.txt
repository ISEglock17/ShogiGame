Here's how I'm approaching this:

1.  **Iterative Analysis:** I'm going through the commentary move by move, carefully examining each comment individually.
2.  **Targeted Deletion:** I'm applying a set of predefined rules – a filter, essentially – to identify and isolate parts of the commentary that are deemed extraneous or irrelevant.
3.  **Content Pruning:** Once I've pinpointed the parts that need to go, I'm surgically removing them from the text.
4.  **Natural Language Polishing:** Finally, I'm working to integrate the remaining pieces into coherent, grammatical sentences. My aim is to make the refined commentary easy to understand and engaging for the reader. I have to make sure that the meaning is preserved and that it sounds good to the ear.
