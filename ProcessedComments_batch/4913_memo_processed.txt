**Focusing the Lens: Extracting the Essence of Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up Shogi game commentary, eh? I get it. They want to distill these sprawling play-by-plays down to the pure, unadulterated essence of the game. My job is to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock-watching – and leave behind only the crucial analysis of the board and the moves. Think of it like a perfectly honed knife, slicing through the extraneous to reveal the core truth of the match. I need to leave only the natural-flowing explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each individual move. The rules are clear: anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the flow of the game, has got to go. I need to make the user's data super-digestible for in-depth shogi analysis.
