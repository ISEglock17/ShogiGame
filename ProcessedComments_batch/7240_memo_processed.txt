**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm looking at this data from Shogi games, and it's a mess of comments. My goal is clear: I want to refine these comments, trimming the fat and keeping only the good stuff.  Specifically, I want to toss out all the junk that isn't directly relevant to the analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. That means getting rid of all the meta information, like player introductions, time stamps, and any other commentary that doesn't actually contribute to understanding *why* a move was made or *how* it impacts the game. I just want clean, natural language explanations of the key moves and the resulting positions on the board.
