**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up and formatting shogi commentary. The goal here is to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time checks – and leave only the good stuff: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves being played, presented in a clear and natural way.

I'm thinking, "Alright, time to apply the rules." I've got a list of things to filter out. It's like this:

*   **Rule #1:** Any mention of names, ranks, or affiliations that don't belong to the two players in the current game.
*   **Rule #2:** Anything referencing previous games, precedents, or historical context. We only want what's happening *now*.
*   **Rule #3:** Time is irrelevant. Bye-bye time stamps.
*   **Rule #4:** Player profiles, records, ranks, and numbers. Just the moves, please.
*   **Rule #5:** Descriptions of playing styles or preferred opening variations. Focus on *this* game.

This will get rid of the extraneous details so the core analysis of the moves is much more focused and insightful.
