**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've got this task: I need to take these raw comments from a Shogi game and clean them up. The goal is clear: I want to strip out all the noise – the meta-data, the player introductions, the time stamps – everything that doesn't directly contribute to the *analysis* of the board position and the moves. I want to be left with only the insightful explanations of what's happening on the board, presented in a fluid, easy-to-read style.

The process itself is quite structured.  First, I'll meticulously go through each comment, applying the defined rules to identify and remove all the unnecessary information. Think of it as a methodical filtering process. Then, with the essential comments remaining, I'll work on smoothing them out – rephrasing, connecting ideas, ensuring they read as natural sentences within the flow of the game.

Finally, I'll need to package the results in a very specific format.  Each comment will start with the move number, followed by the refined commentary itself. Below each line, I will provide the reasons for the deletions, citing the specific rules used. This will help with transparency and provide a clear audit trail.
