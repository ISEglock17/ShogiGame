**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of Shogi commentary, aiming to refine it for proper analysis. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly relate to the board position or the moves themselves. My objective is to distill the commentary down to its pure, analytical essence.

My approach is straightforward, really. I'll need to develop a set of rules, a sort of filter, based on what information is deemed irrelevant and what should be kept. Essentially, I'll be sifting through the comments, identifying and deleting the unnecessary information to leave a clean, coherent narrative of the game's evolution. It's about getting to the heart of the strategic insight.
