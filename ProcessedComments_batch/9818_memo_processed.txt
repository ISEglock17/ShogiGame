The objective is clear: keep only the insightful explanations of the board state and the moves, presented in a fluid, natural way. This means stripping out all the noise—the metadata, introductions of the players, time stamps, and anything else irrelevant to the strategic analysis of the game itself. I need to focus on distilling the essence of the commentary, leaving behind only the pure tactical and strategic gems that an experienced observer would find most valuable. My process for doing this will begin with a defined set of steps that I need to establish.
