**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so the task at hand is to clean up these comments from shogi game data. The goal? To take the messy commentary, filled with all sorts of irrelevant noise, and distill it down to the pure essence of the game. We're talking about stripping away the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time checks, anything that doesn't directly illuminate the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. My aim is to leave behind only the clear, concise explanations of the positions and the thoughts behind the player's choices, presented in a natural, flowing style. It's about bringing the game itself to the forefront.
