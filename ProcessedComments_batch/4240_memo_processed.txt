**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game data, specifically the commentary for each move. My task, as I understand it, is to meticulously clean up these comments. I need to get rid of all the extraneous noise – the stuff that's useful to a casual viewer or historical record, but just clutters the technical analysis. I'm talking about metadata, introductions of the players, time readings, anything that isn't directly relevant to *the game itself*. My goal is to extract the pure, distilled essence of the commentary: the actual explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a natural, coherent flow. I'm after the kind of commentary that's immediately useful for deep strategic analysis, not historical fluff.
