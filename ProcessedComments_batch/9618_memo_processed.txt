**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm looking at this chunk of data – it's all those comments that come with Shogi game records. My goal here is pretty clear: I want to strip away all the fluff and get down to the core of what's *actually* happening on the board. We're talking about removing everything that's irrelevant to analyzing the moves themselves - you know, the meta stuff, player introductions, time stamps, all that noise.

Essentially, I want to refine these comments into something clean and focused, something that describes the board state and the move being played in a natural, flowing way. There are a few rules I need to keep in mind, and the process is pretty well-defined, which is good.  This will make my subsequent analysis much more efficient.
