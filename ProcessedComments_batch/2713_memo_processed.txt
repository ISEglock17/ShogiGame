**Processing Shogi Commentary: Streamlining the Analysis**

First, I'm going to go through the commentary move by move. As I read each comment, I'll be referencing a set of pre-defined rules that identify redundant or irrelevant information. Anything that falls under those rules gets marked for deletion.

Then, I'll move on to a second set of rules that governs replacement. The key one here is to standardize the player names. Instead of specific names, I'll swap them out for "Sente" (先手) for the first player and "Gote" (後手) for the second. This helps remove the specifics and focus on the flow of the game for wider analysis, making each commentary digestible by a more general audience.
