**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw commentary into coherent, insightful analyses of the game itself. No fluff, no extraneous details—just the stuff that truly helps understand the board state and the moves being made.

My plan? First, I'll meticulously go through the comments and apply a rigorous set of rules to identify and eliminate anything that doesn't contribute to the core analysis. Think meta-commentary, biographical tidbits about the players, or time-related information. That stuff isn't helpful here.

Once I've stripped away the unnecessary bits, the real work begins: I need to take what's left and craft it into a clear, natural-sounding explanation of the board and the specific moves. I want the final result to feel like a flowing, logical breakdown of the game, free of jarring shifts or inconsistencies. The goal is a commentary that *I* would find helpful if I were analyzing the game.
