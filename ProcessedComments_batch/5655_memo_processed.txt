The goal, as I understand it, is to refine the comments, stripping away anything that won't contribute to a meaningful analysis of the board positions and moves themselves.  Basically, I'm aiming to keep only the explanations of what's happening on the board, described in a clear and natural way.

I've got the rules laid out, and I'll work through each comment one by one, making the appropriate cuts.  Let's see, I need to keep an eye out for these things in particular:
*   I'll be eliminating any mention of people's names that aren't the players themselves, along with their ranks or affiliations.
*   Anything about prior games or similar matches gets the axe too.
