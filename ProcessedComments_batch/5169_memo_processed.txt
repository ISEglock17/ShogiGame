**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to streamline the comments, to focus them. I want to remove all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – and distill them down to the real meat: clear, concise explanations of the board state and the moves being played. Essentially, I'm trying to leave behind only those annotations that would be genuinely useful for someone digging into the game's analysis. This will make it far easier to integrate these comments into any kind of automated parsing or analysis pipeline.
