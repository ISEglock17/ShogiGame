**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one actually. It involves cleaning up Shogi game commentary data, you know, the stuff that accompanies a Kifu. The goal is clear: strip away all the extraneous noise, the meta-commentary, biographical fluff about the players, the time stamps... everything that isn't directly related to the actual moves and board states. My job is to leave behind only the truly insightful analysis, the explanations of why a move was played, the strategic considerations, and the tactical nuances. I want to leave it in its most natural form, readable by a human, and easily consumed. This way, the Kifu data is more consumable.
