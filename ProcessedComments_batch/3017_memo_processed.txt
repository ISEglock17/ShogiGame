**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a bunch of comments associated with Shogi game data, and the goal is pretty clear: I need to whip these annotations into shape. The raw data is messy; there's a lot of noise I need to filter out.  

Essentially, I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata about the game, the player introductions, the time stamps... all that stuff. My focus needs to be laser-sharp on the *actual* Shogi insights: the descriptions of the board state and the explanations of why certain moves were made.  I want to preserve that core analysis, but present it in a way that reads smoothly, like a coherent piece of writing.  It's about crafting a clean, insightful narrative from the sometimes disjointed annotations that typically come with game records.
