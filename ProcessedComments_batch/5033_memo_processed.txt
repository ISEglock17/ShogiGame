**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I've got a challenge here. Someone wants me to refine commentary from shogi game data. They're aiming for that pure, elegant analysis – just the insights into the board and the moves themselves, free from all the fluff. I get it. We want the distilled essence of the strategic thinking, not the biographical details.

My task is to meticulously sift through each comment, move by move, and apply a specific set of rules to clean it up. I need to be ruthless about removing anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's unfolding dynamics. That means the names of non-players, their ranks and affiliations are gone. Any mention of past games or precedents? Poof! Time stamps? History. Player stats, profiles, even their assigned numbers within the Shogi world? Out with the old, in with the new.
