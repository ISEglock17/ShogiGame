**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine these comments, right? I want to pull out the truly insightful analysis of the game, the stuff that *actually* matters to a serious player. Forget the fluff, the introductions to players, the clock information – all that's just noise.

Essentially, I need to strip away the irrelevant metadata and personal flourishes and leave behind the core strategic discussions and the concrete explanations of each move, presented as cohesive, understandable sentences. I need to make these annotations more useful and easier to digest. Let's start by figuring out the best way to do that.
