**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The goal here is to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know, the stuff that's useful contextually but a distraction when you're trying to analyze the actual moves and strategy. I need to distill the commentary down to its essence: the explanations of the board positions and the reasoning behind each player's moves. It's about preserving the *strategic narrative*, not the biographical details.

Essentially, I need to create a refined dataset of insightful, stand-alone sentences that would be suitable for machine learning or advanced chess move analysis. This is a crucial task for any work on strategic understanding.
