The goal is to strip away all the extraneous stuff – the fluff, the irrelevant details – and leave behind only the genuinely insightful commentary on the board positions and specific moves. Essentially, I need to distill the essence of the analysis into clear, natural-sounding sentences.

My process will involve a two-pronged approach. First, I need to define the "deletion targets." I'll create a set of rules that clearly identify what information is considered unnecessary for the purpose of focused game analysis. Anything falling under these rules will be flagged for removal.

After which I can do further analysis.
