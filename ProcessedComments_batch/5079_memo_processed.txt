**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm dealing with a lot of Shogi commentary data, which is great because it means there's a wealth of information available. But, as we all know, raw data is rarely useful in its current form. My goal here is to refine this commentary, streamlining it for analysis and removing the clutter that often creeps in.

Specifically, I need to get rid of all the extraneous details: the meta-commentary, player introductions (we know who's playing!), time stamps, and anything else that isn't directly related to the actual board state or the moves being made. I'm after the core insights: the tactical and strategic reasoning behind each move, the analysis of the position, and the potential lines of play.

The process is straightforward: I'll use a set of predefined rules to identify and filter out these non-essential elements. This should allow me to extract the critical commentary – the real meat of the analysis – and present it in a clean, coherent, and digestible format. The objective is to make the commentary ready for more in depth analysis.
