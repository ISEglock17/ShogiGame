**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Comments for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. I get it. The raw stuff is always a mess – filled with irrelevant noise that just muddies the water. The goal here is to strip away all the fluff: the metadata, the introductions, the clock readings, everything that isn't directly relevant to the actual *play* and the *analysis* of the game. We're talking about streamlining the comments so that what's left is pure, uncluttered explanations of the board state and the moves being made. Essentially, turning the rambling notes into a coherent narrative of the game's unfolding strategy. This will be much more useful for any serious research into the game!
