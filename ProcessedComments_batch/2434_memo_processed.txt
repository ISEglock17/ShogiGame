**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up and streamline Shogi game commentary, removing all the fluff that's not strictly relevant to the analysis of the moves themselves. My goal is to distill the commentary down to its essential form: clear, concise explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.  No extraneous details like meta-commentary, player introductions, or timing updates - those just clutter the signal.  I need to focus on the meat of the matter.

My process will be straightforward, but exacting. First, I'll identify the commentary elements that need to go based on a set of clearly defined rules.  Anything outside the scope of the game's immediate tactical and strategic considerations gets the axe.  Next, with the chaff removed, I'll need to carefully reconstruct what's left into grammatically correct and naturally flowing sentences.  The aim is a commentary that's both informative and a pleasure to read, a testament to the beauty and complexity of the game.  Essentially, I'm looking to produce a streamlined and focused narrative of the game's critical moments.
