**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Game Analysis**

Okay, so the goal here is to refine the comments associated with each move in a Shogi game's data. I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff – the meta details, player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move. What I'm aiming for is a concise, pure commentary focused solely on the critical elements: the current board configuration and a clear explanation of *why* that move was played. It's about distilling the essence of the commentary into something truly useful for deep analysis, free from distractions. This is a crucial step towards making the data more accessible and insightful for serious Shogi study.
