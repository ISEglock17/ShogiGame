**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform these often-chaotic remarks into concise, natural-sounding explanations of the game's state and moves. It's about stripping away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps – and honing in on the actual analysis of the board and the player's choices. The raw comments are a mess, so this process is crucial. The end result needs to be something that a fellow Shogi enthusiast, like myself, can easily follow and learn from. This will improve the readability of the comments, and will allow me to integrate them into my own analysis.
