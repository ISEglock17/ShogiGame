**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this interesting dataset of Shogi commentary. My goal here is to refine these comments, stripping away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know the drill – and leaving only the pure, insightful commentary on the board positions and specific moves. Essentially, I want to create a lean, mean analytical dataset.

My plan? I'll meticulously go through each comment and apply a strict set of rules. Based on these rules, I'll identify and excise all the irrelevant information. It's a bit like surgical precision, removing the unnecessary connective tissue to get to the heart of the matter – the actual strategic analysis. The end result? Natural-sounding, focused explanations of the game that are perfect for deeper analysis and model training.
