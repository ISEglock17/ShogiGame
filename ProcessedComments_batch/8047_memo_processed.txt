**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up commentary from shogi game data. The goal is clear: strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know, the stuff that's irrelevant to the actual analysis of the game itself.  I need to get to the core of the commentary, the meat of it – the explanations of the board state and the moves, presented in a fluid, natural way.

Fortunately, there's a well-defined set of procedures and rules to guide me through this process.  I understand I need to carefully analyze each comment, apply the rules to identify the parts that need to be removed, and then execute that deletion. The process seems straightforward; it's a matter of meticulous application of the defined rules to each piece of commentary.
