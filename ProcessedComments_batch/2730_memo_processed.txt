**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm tackling this interesting task of tidying up commentary in Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform raw comments attached to these games into something much more useful for analysis. Basically, I want to filter out all the extraneous noise – all the metadata, the player introductions, the time stamps – and focus solely on the meat of the commentary: the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.  I need to distill these comments down into natural, flowing sentences that are directly relevant to the gameplay and its strategic implications.
