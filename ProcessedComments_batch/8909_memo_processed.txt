**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of cleaning up these shogi commentary sections from game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform these raw comments into something useful for analyzing the game itself.  Right now, they're cluttered with all sorts of extraneous information - names of commentators, their ranks, mentions of who their teacher was, even stuff about past games.  It's distracting and dilutes the core commentary on the board state and the moves.

I've got the rules laid out, and I'm going through each comment systematically.  First up, I'm getting rid of any mention of folks who aren't the players in the current game. That means no more mentioning commentator names, their ranks, or anything about their mentors.  Then, I'm removing any references to past games or precedents.  It's all about keeping the focus squarely on this specific match and what's happening right now.  I need to keep the valuable insights about the board's condition and the rationale behind each move, but all the surrounding fluff has got to go. I need the raw, unadulterated analysis.
