**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my task is to refine it. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform raw comments from game datasets into clean, focused explanations of the board state and the moves being made. Essentially, I need to strip away all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of metadata, player introductions, time stamps, any irrelevant context, really. I want to zero in on the core strategic insights, the tactical reasoning, the "why" behind each move, and present this information in a way that feels natural and flows like a coherent narrative.
