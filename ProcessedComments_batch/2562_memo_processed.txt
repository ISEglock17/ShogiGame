**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up Shogi game commentary, huh? My goal here is straightforward: I need to transform the raw commentary into something focused and useful for analyzing the game. I want to strip away all the extraneous fluff – the metadata about the game itself, the introductions to the players, the time information, all that noise – and leave behind only the *meat* of the analysis.  Just the explanations of the board state and the moves, presented in a clear and natural way.

Essentially, I'll need to define some clear rules for what constitutes unnecessary information. Once I've got that sorted out, the process is pretty much a two-step dance: first, I filter out all the irrelevant stuff based on those rules, and then I work on polishing what remains into grammatically sound sentences. That's the core of it, really.
