It's about distilling the essence of the commentary into something that can actually be useful for in-depth kifu analysis.

Basically, I'm presented with a set of comments associated with each move, and my job is to filter them based on some pre-defined rules. The idea is to make them concise and relevant to the actual game being played. The process seems pretty straightforward if I focus on the relevant portions. Let's see how I can get this done.
