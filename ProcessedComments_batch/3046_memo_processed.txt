**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary attached to shogi game data. It's that typical situation: lots of interesting insights are buried under all sorts of extraneous junk. The goal, as I understand it, is to extract the really useful stuff—the strategic analysis, the explanations of moves, the reasoning behind choices—and leave everything else behind. We want to end up with a smooth, readable narrative about the game's flow, unburdened by metadata, player introductions, or clock information. It's about getting to the *heart* of the game. That's what I need to do: separate the wheat from the chaff, so to speak.
