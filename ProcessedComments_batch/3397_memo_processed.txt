**Cleaning Up Commentary: Stripping Out the Fluff**

Okay, so I've got this request from a user, and it's something I've dealt with a lot in my own work. Basically, they want me to clean up the commentary from shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty clear: get rid of all the extraneous stuff that's clogging up the analysis. I'm talking about the meta-information, the player introductions, time stamps, all that noise. What I want to leave behind is the pure, distilled essence: the explanations of the board state and the moves themselves, all presented in a way that actually flows naturally. It's like taking a complex equation and simplifying it down to its most elegant and useful form. This is going to be my process.
