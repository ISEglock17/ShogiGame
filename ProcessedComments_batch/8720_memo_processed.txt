**Structuring and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my goal is clear: I need to refine these comments based on specific rules. Think of it like a text "de-cluttering" process. The core idea is to remove all the fluff – any metadata, player intros, time stamps, and anything else that's not directly relevant to the board state or the move itself. My job is to get down to the essence: the actual explanations of what's happening on the board and why.

My process will involve two primary steps. First, I'll meticulously go through the comments and, using the rules as my guide, flag and eliminate the unnecessary bits. This is like a targeted "find and delete" operation, only smarter. Then, I'll take what remains – the core commentary – and ensure it's grammatically sound and flows naturally as a sentence.

Finally, the output needs to adhere to a specific structure, almost like a code snippet. The first line needs to present the move number and the cleaned-up comment. After that, I will include the "reason for deletion," so that the process is transparent and traceable, explicitly stating the phrases that were removed and the specific rule that applied to them. This ensures the output is both useful and self-explanatory.
