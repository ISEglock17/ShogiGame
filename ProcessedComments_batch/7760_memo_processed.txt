**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task: a user wants me to clean up Shogi game commentary data.  The goal is pretty clear. They want to strip out all the noise – all the extraneous stuff that's not directly related to the actual game's progress.  Think meta-information, player introductions, time stamps – anything that isn't core to the analysis of the board state and the moves made.

My job is to go through these comments and apply the specified rules.  Essentially, I'm surgically removing the fluff and leaving behind the pure, distilled explanation of the positions and the moves.  I'm focusing on preserving the natural flow of the commentary, but ensuring it's laser-focused on the game itself.  This means I'm concentrating on transforming raw comments into something more immediately useful for deeper analysis.
