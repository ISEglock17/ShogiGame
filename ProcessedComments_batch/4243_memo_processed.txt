**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

I'm talking about getting rid of all the fluff, the metadata, the personal introductions, and the time stamps – you know, all the stuff that's interesting for casual viewing but just clogs up the works for my purposes.

What I'm really after are the core insights: the commentary that actually digs into the board positions, explains the thought process behind the moves, and lays out the strategic considerations. I want to be left with natural, coherent explanations that I can then feed into my analysis pipeline.
