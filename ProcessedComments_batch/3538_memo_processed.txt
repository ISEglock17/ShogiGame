My goal, essentially, is to strip away all the extraneous noise. I need to get rid of the fluff: the meta-commentary, the player introductions (I already know who they are!), time stamps, all that.  What I'm after is the *meat* of the analysis. I want to keep the valuable insights, the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented as clear, natural sentences.  I need to go through each comment, apply my established rules (that's key, I have a pretty rigorous system here) to determine what gets tossed and what stays, ensuring only the truly insightful bits remain. That's the first step.
