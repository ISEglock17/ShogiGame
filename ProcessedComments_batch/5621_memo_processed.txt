**Processing and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with a familiar problem: cleaning up and standardizing commentary from shogi game datasets. The user wants the data stripped down, refined to its essence.  The goal is crystal clear: to isolate the meaningful analysis of the game, the real insights into the board state and the moves, and present them in a smooth, readable format. I understand the importance of this, it's about making the data ready for proper analytical processing and downstream machine learning. I'm focusing on removing the extraneous noise – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – all the things that clutter up the core commentary. I'm going to follow the established rules, each step, each piece of commentary, will be filtered, edited, and refined to get to the core analysis of the moves.
