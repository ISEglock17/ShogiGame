They want to strip away all the extraneous noise – things like who's speaking, the time elapsed, any background info about the players or tournament, and any general introductions. The goal is to isolate the pure, focused explanation of the board state and the move itself, crafting a natural and clear sentence. Essentially, they want the raw, insightful analysis, free from the chatter that often accompanies it. That's a reasonable request, and one that requires some careful processing. Time to get to work.
