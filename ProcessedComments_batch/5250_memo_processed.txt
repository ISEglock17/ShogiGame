**Streamlining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of Shogi game commentary and my task is to refine it. The goal is to make it more digestible and focused, kind of like curating it for a more informed audience. I need to be able to identify and remove all the fluff – any extraneous information that doesn't actually contribute to understanding the game's flow or the reasoning behind the moves.

So, how do I approach this? Well, it's pretty straightforward. First, I'll read through each comment. Then, I'll essentially apply a mental filter, looking for those specific elements that are deemed unnecessary. The ones I'll be keeping are those that describe the board state, or explain the thinking behind a player's move. That's the essence of what I'm keeping.  Anything else? Gone. It's about clarity and conciseness, really.
