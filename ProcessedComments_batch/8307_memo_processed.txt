**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

I need to strip out all the fluff – you know, the introductory remarks about the players, the time stamps, anything that's essentially meta-information and irrelevant to the strategic depth of the game itself.

Essentially, I'm aiming for a streamlined narrative, focusing solely on the *why* behind each move and its potential implications. Think of it like distilling the essence of the game's intellectual content. I'm going to follow a specific process to achieve this, making sure to remove any extraneous details and leaving only the insightful analysis.
