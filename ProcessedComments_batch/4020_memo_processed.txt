**Structuring Game Commentary for Clarity**

Okay, so I need to take this raw data of Shogi game comments and really refine it. The goal is to strip away all the extraneous noise – anything that isn't a clear explanation of the board state or a specific move – and leave behind only the truly insightful commentary. I want to produce clean, natural language that flows well and helps a player understand the game's progression.

My plan is pretty straightforward. I'll need to go through each comment, one move at a time, and carefully evaluate it against my pre-defined rules for what's valuable and what's not. This is going to be a meticulous process, but in the end, I'll have a significantly improved dataset that's much more useful for analysis and learning.
