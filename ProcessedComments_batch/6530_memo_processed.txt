**Streamlining the Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these shogi commentary snippets. The goal is to get rid of the extraneous bits – anything not directly related to the board position or the moves themselves – and smooth out the language. I'm imagining this is to make it flow better for someone following the game, perhaps to be used as a training resource or perhaps for a more concise viewing experience.

Here's how I'm going to tackle this. I'll read through each comment, one by one. For each sentence within the comment, I'll apply the removal rules. If a sentence fits the criteria for removal, it's gone. If not, I'll keep it. The end result should be a streamlined, yet still informative, play-by-play. It's really about distilling the essence of the game and making sure the important information is front and center. I believe, as a subject matter expert, that the resulting document will be significantly more valuable to a student or enthusiast of the game.
