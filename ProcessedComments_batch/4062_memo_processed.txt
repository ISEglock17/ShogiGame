They're filled with metadata, introductions, time information, and other stuff that's frankly getting in the way of a clean analysis. My goal is to streamline these comments, getting rid of all the noise and keeping only the essential details - the actual board state descriptions and the commentary on the moves themselves. I need to make the narrative flow. It's about removing the extraneous and leaving the core insights. I'll need to develop a set of rules for which kinds of information to exclude, but ultimately, it's about clarity and focus.
