Anything that doesn't contribute to understanding the intricacies of the moves themselves needs to go.

Once that's done, the second phase begins. It's time to take the remaining nuggets of wisdom and transform them. My objective is to make the commentary a fluent, concise, and analytically precise explanation. I'll re-phrase these insights for clarity.
