**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this raw commentary data from shogi games, and it's a bit of a mess, isn't it? My goal here is pretty straightforward: I want to strip out all the fluff. I'm talking about the meta stuff, the player intros, the time stamps... all that stuff that's useful for following the game, but honestly just gets in the way when I'm trying to actually *analyze* the moves themselves.

What I'm after is the pure, distilled essence of the commentary – the actual descriptions of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.  I need it presented as coherent, natural-sounding sentences.  Think of it as refining the raw data into something much more valuable for my research. So, let's get down to business. Time to get rid of the noise and focus on the signal.
