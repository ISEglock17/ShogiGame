**Structuring Shogi Commentary for Clarity**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's right up my alley. Someone wants me to take a messy stream of shogi commentary and whip it into shape, focusing on the core strategic elements. I need to distill each comment down to its essence: the current board state and the critical moves that shaped it.

The process is pretty straightforward, thankfully. First, I have to meticulously analyze each comment, move by move. It's like a linguistic archaeological dig, separating the wheat from the chaff. My goal is to extract only the information that contributes to understanding the flow of the game – no superfluous chatter, no unnecessary asides. Just the essential data: what the board looks like and the specific actions taken. This will make for a far more insightful and engaging commentary, ultimately.
