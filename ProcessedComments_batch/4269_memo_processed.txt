I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – that metadata junk, the player introductions, time stamps – you know, the stuff that clogs up the signal. What I want is the pure essence of the commentary: the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. I'm after the insights, the strategic justifications, the *why* behind the *what*. This isn't just about removing data; it's about refining it, sculpting it into a coherent narrative of the game itself, focused solely on the crucial aspects of the position and the play.
