**Streamlining Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tasked with refining these Shogi game commentaries, and the goal is crystal clear: I need to extract the pure essence of the game from all the extraneous noise. My focus is on the board state and the moves themselves, stripping away anything that doesn't contribute to that core narrative.

Essentially, I'm surgically removing the fluff. That means getting rid of all the meta information that a skilled analyst wouldn't need: the introductions of the players, their rankings and affiliations, any historical context about previous games, the play-by-play of the clock, and the pre-game build-up. All of that is irrelevant to the actual analysis, right? I'll be using the provided rules to guide me, each one acting as a surgical instrument to cut out the unnecessary details, leaving behind the concise and naturally flowing descriptions of the game itself.
