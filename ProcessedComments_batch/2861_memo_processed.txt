**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi commentary snippets. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock updates – and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the game itself.  I want to present it as naturally flowing explanations of the board state and the moves made.

My process will be methodical.  I'll go line by line through each comment, applying the rules I've been given. Let me walk you through my thought process:

*   **First, I'll filter out anything unrelated to the moves:** Anything that mentions people who aren't currently playing, their ranks, affiliations, or past connections to the game is out.
*   **Next, I'll remove any historical context:** References to previous games, past precedents, or anything that doesn't directly relate to the current board configuration is going to be discarded.
*   **Time checks? Gone:** I don't need any updates on how much time is left on the clock.
*   **Player bios? Not relevant:** No need to keep track of their profiles, records, ranks, or even their playing style tendencies.
*   **Focus on the analysis:** This should leave me with the core of the commentary – the actual analysis of the board position and the rationale behind each move.
