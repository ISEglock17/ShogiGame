**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I'm actually quite familiar with: cleaning up and refining commentary from Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform this raw commentary into a leaner, more focused resource for detailed Kifu analysis. I see a lot of noise in these comments -  those introductory paragraphs, player biographies, and time stamps? They have their place, but they're not useful in the context of deep strategic study. My objective is to strip all that out, leaving behind only the distilled essence: the direct descriptions of the board state and the moves, explained in flowing, natural prose.  Essentially, I want to create a streamlined, high-quality data set perfectly tailored for my own, and others', serious Shogi research.
