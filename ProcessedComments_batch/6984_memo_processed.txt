**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with a familiar challenge: taking messy commentary from Shogi game data and turning it into something useful. My goal here is precise. I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that noise – and distill it down to the core: clear, concise explanations of the board state and the moves being made.  It's about extracting the *analysis*, the strategic insights, and leaving behind the extraneous details.

My process will be straightforward. I'll need to define a clear set of rules for identifying and removing the irrelevant information. Once that's done, it's just a matter of cleaning up the remaining text, ensuring it reads smoothly and coherently as a natural explanation of the game's unfolding events. This involves a good understanding of Shogi terminology and typical commentary styles, of course.  Essentially, I'm aiming to create a focused, readily digestible set of comments perfect for serious game analysis.
