**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments associated with Shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to distill them down to their purest form: insightful explanations of the board state and the moves made.  I need to remove all the fluff – the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps... anything that doesn't contribute directly to the *analysis* of the game.

Essentially, I need to apply a set of rules to each comment and decide what's essential and what's extraneous. It's about preserving the *meaning* of the commentary while discarding the noise. I'll need to be quite precise in how I define those rules, ensuring I don't accidentally remove information that actually *does* contribute to understanding the game's flow. It will be an interesting challenge of linguistic precision and Shogi comprehension.
