I'm talking about the meta-data, the player introductions, time information... basically anything that doesn't directly relate to the board position or the moves themselves. My objective is to refine the commentary down to natural-sounding sentences that offer insight into the game's flow. It's about distilling the core analysis. I have a plan in mind to accomplish this task.
