**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Comments for Sharper Analysis**

Alright, so a user, a fellow aficionado of the game, needs some help streamlining their data. They've got these game logs, rich with commentary, but they want to distill them down to pure, actionable insights for their analysis. It's a familiar challenge, I've had to do this countless times myself!

Basically, the goal is to *surgicaly remove* all the extraneous noise from the comments that accompanies the move data. Think: the fluff about who's playing, the overall context, the clock ticking... None of that matters for the core analysis. What *does* matter is the pure, distilled explanation of the positions and moves. The goal is to isolate the essence of the game and how each position evolves.

So, the task at hand is to systematically process each of these comments. To do this, I will carefully apply a set of rules, the specifics of which are not important, and use them to distinguish the chaff from the valuable wheat. Anything deemed unnecessary according to these rules must be excised, leaving behind the concise explanations of the positions.
