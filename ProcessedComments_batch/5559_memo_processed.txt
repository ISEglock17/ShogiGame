**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these shogi commentary sections within game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to surgically remove all the noise – the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps, and any other fluff that isn't directly relevant to analyzing the actual game state and moves.  I need to get down to the bare bones of the insightful stuff: clear and concise explanations of the board positions and the rationale behind each move. My process will involve a two-step method. First, I'll apply the established rules to systematically identify and eliminate all the irrelevant information. Then, with the essential explanations extracted, I'll refine them to create natural, readable sentences.
