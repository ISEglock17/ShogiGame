Okay, so I'm presented with a task that's right up my alley: refining Shogi game commentary. The user wants me to take raw comments, which are probably full of irrelevant details and filler, and distill them into clean, concise explanations of each move. It's about data cleaning and information extraction, but with the added wrinkle of natural language generation.

The process I'm envisioning is pretty straightforward:

1.  **Iterate through each move's commentary.**  I'll need to break down the input into manageable chunks, one move at a time.
2.  **Apply the Deletion Rules.** I need to be a ruthless editor! I'll scan each commentary, identifying and removing anything that matches the pre-defined deletion criteria. That's the core of the cleaning.
3.  **Synthesize a Natural Sentence.**  Once I've stripped away the fluff, I need to craft what's left into a human-readable sentence. This isn't just about stringing words together; it's about making sure the resulting comment accurately and smoothly conveys the essence of the move. It's about quality.

Basically, I'm taking noisy data and transforming it into useful, digestible information – something that's essential for anyone studying Shogi or following a game.
