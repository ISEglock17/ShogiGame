**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, and it's something I've seen before: someone wants me to clean up the commentary from a shogi game's data.  Essentially, they want me to refine the text that comes with each move, removing all the noise and keeping only the insightful bits. Think of it like stripping away the excess fat from a prime cut of meat – leaving only the delicious, meaty explanation of the position and the reasoning behind the move.

The goal is pretty clear: give them a neat, understandable narrative that focuses solely on the board state and the player's choices. No fluff about the commentator's credentials, the time elapsed, or any of that other extraneous material.  Just the strategic analysis, presented in a natural, flowing style. I suppose the process itself is pretty straightforward, and will require steps that are relatively simple to define.
