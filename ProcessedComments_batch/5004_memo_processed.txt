**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with shogi commentary, and the goal is clear: strip away the fluff and leave behind the pure essence of the game. My task is to transform raw commentary into a clean, focused narrative of the board and the moves.

First, I'll dive into the text and meticulously identify the elements that are irrelevant to the core analysis. Things like meta-information, player introductions, and the ongoing clock ticks—gone. My guiding principle will be the rules of what's *essential* for understanding the game's flow and strategic nuances.

Then, the remaining pieces will get a makeover. I'll smooth out the language, connecting the insights about the position with the moves being played. The objective is to produce concise yet eloquent statements that are easy to comprehend.

Finally, I will format the information precisely as requested.
