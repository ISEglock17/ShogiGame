**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of Shogi game commentary, the kind you find attached to the moves in a game record. My goal? To refine this commentary into something truly useful for in-depth analysis. I'm dealing with messy text, and it's my job to make it clean.

Essentially, I need to strip away all the chaff. Things like metadata about the game itself, introductions of the players, time spent on each move – all this peripheral stuff needs to go. My focus is laser-sharp on the actual explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each individual move. That's the real meat of the commentary, and that's what I want to preserve.

So, the process is pretty straightforward: I'll carefully examine each comment, applying a specific set of rules to determine what's vital and what's extraneous. Anything deemed irrelevant, according to those rules, gets excised. The goal is a streamlined, coherent narrative that focuses solely on the tactical and strategic considerations of the game. That's what will yield the most insightful analysis in the long run.
