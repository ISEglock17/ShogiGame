**Cleaning Up My Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of refining these Shogi game commentary datasets. The core of it is pretty straightforward, but it's a bit tedious. Essentially, I need to go through each comment attached to a move and strip away all the extraneous stuff. That means getting rid of those time stamps, the player introductions, any meta-commentary that isn't directly related to the board position or the move itself, and all that fluff. My goal is to extract the essence – the actual explanation of *why* a move was played, what the thinking was behind it, or any insightful analysis of the current board state.  The challenge lies in creating the rules, because it needs to be precise.  It is important to remove the noise without removing the value.
