Okay, so I'm presented with a task: a Shogi data set full of commentary, and it needs some serious cleaning up.  Essentially, I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – and leave behind only the genuinely insightful analysis of the board position and the moves themselves. My goal is to transform these scattered comments into coherent, readable explanations.  It's about extracting the essence of the strategic thinking and tactical considerations present in the game. I need to make the comments more useful for deeper analysis.
