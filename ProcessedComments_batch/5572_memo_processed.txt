The goal is clear: they want to transform this raw commentary into clean, focused explanations of the game. Essentially, they want me to strip away all the extraneous noise - the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, and any other irrelevant fluff. What they're looking for is a concise narrative, sentence by sentence, that describes the board state and the reasoning behind each move. It's about distilling the essence of the game's unfolding strategy. I need to be able to identify which parts are actually useful for someone analyzing the moves themselves.
