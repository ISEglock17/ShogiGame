**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The goal here is to get the comments into a form that's actually useful for analyzing the games. I need to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time information, you know, the stuff that's not directly about the board state and the moves.  I want to focus solely on the insightful explanations of what's happening on the board and the rationale behind each move.

Basically, I'm going to methodically go through each comment associated with a move and process it according to the pre-defined rules. The idea is to surgically remove the irrelevant bits and then refine what's left into coherent, naturally flowing sentences that really explain the nuances of the game. That's the core of what I'm aiming for.
