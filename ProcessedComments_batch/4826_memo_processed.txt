**Cleaning Up Game Comments for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of shogi game comments, and the goal is clear: I need to clean them up. I want to isolate the truly valuable information for my analysis – the strategic insights, the explanations of moves, the descriptions of the board state. Essentially, I need to strip away all the fluff.

This means getting rid of anything that's not directly related to the actual game's progress. That includes all the metadata – player introductions, biographical details, time stamps, and any other commentary that's not about the board positions or move justifications. I want to transform these raw comments into concise, natural-sounding sentences that focus solely on the "why" and "what" of each move. That way, I can focus on the heart of the strategic insights.
