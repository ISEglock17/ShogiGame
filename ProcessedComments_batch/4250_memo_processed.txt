**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a bunch of comments from shogi game records. My goal here is to refine them into something usable for my analysis. I need to get rid of all the noise: the metadata, the introductions, the clock information – all the stuff that doesn't actually contribute to understanding the board state or the moves themselves.

So, the process in my mind is pretty straightforward. First, I need to define the rules clearly: what constitutes unnecessary information? Once I've got that solid, it's about systematically going through each comment and ruthlessly deleting anything that falls outside those rules. Then, the real work begins: the remaining fragments need to be stitched together into coherent, flowing sentences that actually explain the key aspects of the position and the reasoning behind the moves. This isn't just a simple filter; it's about crafting a narrative from the raw data, highlighting what truly matters to the game.
