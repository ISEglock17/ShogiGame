**Structuring and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I've got this request to take a Shogi game's commentary and really whip it into shape. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to distill the commentary down to its essential components, focusing on the board state and the actual moves made. All the extraneous fluff and analysis that's not directly related to *what's happening on the board* needs to go. I mean, my user, being the expert they are, wants clean, concise information for their analysis, not a novel!

I'll be tackling this move by move, systematically working through each piece of commentary. It's a structured process:

1.  First, I'll be meticulously examining each comment against the pre-defined rules. These rules are key, they dictate what gets cut and what stays. It's about surgically removing anything that doesn't contribute to the core understanding of the game's progression.
2.  Once I've purged the unnecessary bits, I'll focus on the remaining text. My objective here is to present what's left as a natural-sounding, coherent sentence. It has to make sense on its own, conveying the move and its impact clearly.

It's about providing the most distilled form of game analysis. It should be elegant, but it will be technical.
