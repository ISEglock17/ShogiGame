**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to really refine them, to make them useful for deep analysis of the games themselves. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, introductions, character descriptions, and time stamps – all the stuff that's interesting for following a game live, but just clutters up the raw analysis data I'm after. I need to strip all that out, leaving behind only the pure commentary on the board state and the moves themselves. My goal is to reshape the content into a series of natural sentences that are each focused on the mechanics of play.
