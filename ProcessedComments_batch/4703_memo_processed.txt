**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game's Essence**

Okay, so I've been asked to work with these comments attached to shogi game data, and the goal is pretty clear: I need to refine them. I want to strip away all the extraneous noise – things like metadata, introductions to players, time stamps... anything that doesn't actually contribute to our understanding of the board position or the moves themselves. My objective is to extract the pure analytical gold, leaving behind only the concise, natural-sounding explanations of the plays being made. It's about getting down to the core of the shogi analysis, making sure the comments are streamlined and focused on what's truly relevant to the game's evolution.
