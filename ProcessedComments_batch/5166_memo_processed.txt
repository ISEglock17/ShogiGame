**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I've got a challenge here. Someone, probably a fellow analyst, wants to streamline the commentary attached to shogi game data. They're looking to strip away the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that noise – and isolate the *pure* insights about the board and the moves. The goal is to get a natural, cohesive narrative focused solely on the game's unfolding dynamics.

My task is to take these comments, move by move, and systematically filter them based on some clearly defined rules.  It's about precision.

**The Filter Rules:**

1.  Anything that mentions someone *not* directly involved in the current match - names, ranks, or any affiliation outside the current game - is out.
2.  Any reference to previous games, precedents, or past encounters gets the boot.

I need to apply these rules ruthlessly, leaving only the essential, objective analysis. This will make the data much easier to work with.
