**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Sharp Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this interesting dataset of shogi game commentary, and my goal is to really refine it. My task is to go through all these comments, you know, the ones that come with the game data, and whip them into shape. I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps... all that stuff that's just noise when I'm trying to actually *understand* the board position and the move choices.

Essentially, I'm aiming for a streamlined, clear narrative of the game's evolution, focusing solely on the board itself and the reasoning behind each move. So the process is straightforward: I'll use some rules I've got in mind (maybe even build some formal ones, depending on how complex this gets) to identify what needs to go. Anything extraneous gets the boot, and the meat of the analysis – the explanations of the board and the moves – will be polished into natural, flowing sentences. This is all about getting down to the core of the strategy and the thinking that went into each game, no distractions!
