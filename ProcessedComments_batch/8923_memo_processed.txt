**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so the task at hand is to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty straightforward: strip away all the fluff and irrelevant details. I'm talking about getting rid of all the metadata, like who's playing, how much time they've taken, and any introductory bits. The real meat is in the analysis itself – the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. That's what I want to preserve and refine.  My primary focus is to ensure that the remaining text reads smoothly and naturally, like a concise and accurate analysis of the game.
