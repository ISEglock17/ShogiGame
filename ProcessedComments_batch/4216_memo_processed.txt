**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Insight**

The goal here is pretty clear: I want to refine these comments, strip away all the extraneous stuff – the metadata, the introductions, the timestamps, all that noise – and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves.

Essentially, I want to transform those often-cluttered annotations into clean, flowing explanations that really get to the heart of what's happening on the board. This way, I can focus on the strategic depth and tactical nuances of each move, without having to sift through a bunch of irrelevant information.  I'm aiming for a concise and direct approach.
