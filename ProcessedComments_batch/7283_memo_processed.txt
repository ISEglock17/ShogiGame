The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff – the metadata, the introductions, the clock information – and leave behind only the pure, distilled essence of the analysis. It's about preserving the natural flow of the commentary, but *only* if it pertains to the board state or the moves being made.  This should be straightforward enough; it's about separating the signal from the noise.
