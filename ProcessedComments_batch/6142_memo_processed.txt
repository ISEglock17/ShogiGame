**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary**

Okay, so I've got this task: I need to take raw Shogi commentary and refine it. The goal is clear: I want to strip away all the extraneous fluff and boil it down to the essential elements - the game's unfolding story, move by move. Think of it like a distilled essence of the match, ready for deep analysis.

I'm essentially operating as a filter. I've got these specific rules in mind, and my job is to apply them meticulously. Anything that falls under these categories is going to be excised, discarded like unnecessary baggage.

Let's see: I'm not interested in side conversations, the background noise. So, any mention of people *other* than the players themselves, their ranks, affiliations – that's all getting dropped. Forget about historical context or previous encounters, too. This is about the *now*, the game in progress.

Time stamps? Gone. Personal profiles, statistics, stylistic predilections of the players? Not relevant to understanding the *moves*. And anything about the venue, the weather, the hotel they're staying at... that's just flavor text, not substance. I'm after the heart of the game, and I'll keep the focus where it belongs. Let's get to work!
