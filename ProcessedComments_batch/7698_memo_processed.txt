**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform the raw text into something useful for serious kifu analysis. I need to strip out all the fluff. That means the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps – all of that stuff is just noise in this context. 

What I want to be left with is pure, unadulterated commentary on the board state and the moves themselves. I'm after concise, insightful explanations of what's happening on the board, described in a natural, flowing style. It's about distilling the commentary to its essence and making it easier for me, as a seasoned analyst, to get the valuable insights I need. This will involve removing all the extraneous information, leaving only the important bits.
