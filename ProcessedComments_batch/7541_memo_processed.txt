**Streamlining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm being tasked with cleaning up shogi commentary. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player intros, timestamps, you name it – and leave behind only the essential, the actual *chess* content: board positions and insightful move analysis.

Essentially, I'm working with a set of rules. Think of them as my guidelines for what to discard and what to keep. I need to go through each move's commentary, and based on these rules, I make the call to prune away parts of the text.

Here's the gist of my "to-delete" list:
*   Any mention of individuals *not* directly involved in the game, including their credentials.
*   References to precedents or historical matches.
*   Time-related information – every second and minute detail is irrelevant.
*   Detailed biographies of the players. We don't care about their ratings or who their teacher was.
*   Subjective assessments of playing styles or inclinations.
*   Information about the place of play.

Basically, I'm refining the commentary to a pure form, focusing on the strategic and tactical aspects of each move.
