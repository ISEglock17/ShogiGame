**Refining Commentary in Shogi Game Data**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up the comments accompanying each move in a Shogi game dataset. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform those raw comments into something useful for analyzing the game, stripping away all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of the extraneous stuff - the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps, you name it. Basically, I want to isolate the pure, natural language explanations of the board state and the moves themselves.

Thankfully, the process is pretty well-defined, and the rules are laid out clearly. I've just got to follow them to the letter. First, I need to analyze each comment and determine exactly what information needs to go, based on the established criteria. Then, once I've identified the junk, it's just a matter of executing the deletions. It's a straightforward process, really, just requires careful attention to detail and a good grasp of what's crucial for the analysis.
