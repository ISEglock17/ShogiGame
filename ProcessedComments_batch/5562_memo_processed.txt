I need to keep only the good stuff: the actual *explanations* of the board positions and the moves that were played.  Essentially, I need to extract the core strategic insights and present them as a coherent narrative, so someone can easily read and understand the flow of thought behind the game.  It's about crafting a clean, focused narrative of the strategic considerations, and distilling the essence of the game.
