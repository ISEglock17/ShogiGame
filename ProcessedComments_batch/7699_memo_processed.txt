**Processing Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is to take a bunch of raw commentary data and transform it into something useful – focused, insightful explanations of the game itself. No fluff, no distractions, just pure analysis.

Here's my thought process: First, I need to understand the rules of what to keep and what to ditch. Things like metadata about the game, introductions to players, time stamps – those are out. I'm after the core: the board position descriptions and the reasoning behind each move.

Once I've identified and removed all the extraneous information, I'll have a bunch of fragments left. Then, it's time to put those fragments together, to re-write them, re-form them, into clear, coherent sentences that explain the game's flow and strategy.

Finally, I need to format the result to match the required output format. That's the last step. It's all about making the information easily digestible and helpful for someone analyzing the game or studying the player's moves.
