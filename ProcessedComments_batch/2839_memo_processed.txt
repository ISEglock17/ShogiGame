**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: I need to refine these comments, trimming away the excess fluff and getting to the meat of the analysis. It's about taking that raw data, those comments attached to each move, and turning them into something clean and focused.

I'm specifically targeting the extraneous bits - the meta information, the introductions, the time stamps... all the stuff that clutters up the core insights.  My process will involve applying a set of rules, a sort of filter, to identify and remove these irrelevant elements. My aim is to isolate the key aspects of the position and the specific explanations for the moves, leaving a coherent and insightful narrative about the board's evolution. It's about distilling the essence of the commentary into something that's really useful for deep analysis.
