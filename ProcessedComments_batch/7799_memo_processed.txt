So, I'm going to meticulously go through each comment. I know I have to be ruthless about certain kinds of information. Anything that isn't directly about the board state or the move itself has to go. Specifically, I'm looking to eliminate:

*   **Rule 1:** Anything about people besides the two players themselves—no mention of third-party commentators, their ranks, or anything about their training lineage. Get rid of the fluff and keep the core.

Basically, I'm aiming for a clean, concise, and analytically useful set of commentary.
