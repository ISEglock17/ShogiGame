Then, with the extraneous details excised, I'll focus on the remaining content and make sure it reads well and flows naturally. It has to make sense on its own, conveying the reasoning behind each move in a concise, insightful way. Ultimately, the refined commentary should feel like a direct window into the evolving strategic landscape of the shogi match.
