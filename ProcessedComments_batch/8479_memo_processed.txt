**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform the raw comments into something useful for *deep analysis*, stripping out all the fluff that a seasoned Shogi player like myself doesn't need. Think of it like a meticulous edit, aimed at getting down to the core of the tactical and strategic discussions.

Here's how I see this playing out:

1.  **Filtering the Noise:** First, I'll systematically identify and eliminate all that irrelevant stuff - the metadata about the players, biographical details, timing information, and any redundant commentary. My focus is laser-sharp on the *actual* analysis of the board and the moves.
2.  **Structuring for Clarity:**  Once I've got the essentials, I'll work on making the remaining text flow seamlessly. I'll rephrase and structure the commentary into a natural, logical sequence, highlighting the key insights about the board state and the reasoning behind each move. It's about distilling the essence of the analysis, so it becomes a valuable resource.
