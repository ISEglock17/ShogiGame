**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I've been tasked with a pretty common problem in my line of work: cleaning up the commentary from shogi game data. I need to take these often verbose and chatty comments and distill them down to their essence. The goal is to isolate the really valuable stuff – the actual analysis of the board state and the moves played – and ditch all the fluff.

Think of it like sifting through a mountain of ore to find the pure gold. I'm after the nuggets of strategic insight, the explanations of why a certain move is good or bad, the identification of key positions and tactical considerations. Stuff like who's playing, how much time is left, or a little background on the players? That's just noise. My focus is the **game itself**. My ultimate aim is to have a lean, mean, **analytic machine** where I can analyze the most important parts of a game more easily.

This is all about efficiency. I need to make the data much more usable for deeper analysis down the line.
