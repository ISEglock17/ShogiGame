**Refining Commentary in Shogi Games**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments in the Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform the raw text into something useful for analyzing the moves themselves. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – introductions, time stamps, meta-commentary that isn't really relevant to the actual board position or the player's reasoning behind the moves.

My approach will be methodical. I'll need to develop a set of rules, a sort of filter, to identify and then systematically remove all the extraneous information. It's all about distilling the essence of the comment down to the bare bones – a clear, concise explanation of the state of the board and the strategic considerations behind the player's choices. This requires some careful parsing and a good understanding of what constitutes meaningful commentary from a game analysis perspective.
