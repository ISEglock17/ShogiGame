**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Dive Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty typical challenge: a mountain of commentary attached to Shogi game data. My goal, as I understand it, is to transform these messy ramblings into clean, focused insights. I need to strip away all the fluff, the meta-chatter, the biographical details of the players, the time stamps... all of that is noise to my expert eyes.

Essentially, I need to isolate the *core*.  What is being said about the board state, the strategic implications of each move, the reasoning behind the play?  That's the gold I'm after.  I'll need a set of defined rules to guide this process – a clear criteria for what gets the axe and what stays.  I imagine this will involve pattern matching, keyword identification, maybe even some natural language understanding to tease out the relevant pieces.  It's all about distilling the essence of the game's unfolding narrative into something truly digestible for rigorous analysis.  Let's get to work.
