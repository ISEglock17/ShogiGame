**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a Shogi game record and accompanying commentary, and I need to clean it up. The goal is clear: I have to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that noise that's just getting in the way – and leave behind only the genuinely insightful analysis of the board positions and the actual moves made. I need it to flow like natural language, too. No clunky, disjointed statements allowed. The focus is purely on the strategic and tactical understanding of the game as it unfolds.
