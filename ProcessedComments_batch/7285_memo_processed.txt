**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine them, to get rid of all the noise. I need to strip out the fluff – the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps, you know, all the extraneous stuff that doesn't contribute to the core analysis. What I *really* want is the essence: the explanations of the board state and the move itself, rendered in clear, flowing language. I want these annotations to be a clean, concise record of the game's key moments, not a historical document. This is for my own research, so I need to cut down the clutter.
