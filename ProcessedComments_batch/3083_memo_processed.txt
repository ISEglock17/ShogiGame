Think of it this way: I want to get down to the bare bones, the meat of the discussion about the board positions and the actual moves played. Things like who's playing, how long the game took, biographical details, or even just general pleasantries, they're all unnecessary distractions. I want to leave only the pithy, insightful commentary that gives me a true feel for the game's unfolding drama. This will give me clean data for further analysis.
