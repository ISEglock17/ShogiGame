**Cleaning Up Those Pesky Comments**

Alright, so I'm tackling this dataset of shogi game comments. The user wants me to whip these things into shape, and I totally get it. It's a goldmine of analysis, but it's buried under a ton of noise. We've got metadata clogging things up – player introductions, time stamps, all that stuff that's useful contextually, but not when you're focusing on the *actual* analysis of the moves.

My goal here is clear: distill those comments down to their essence. I want to keep the valuable insights – the explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Think concise, natural language. No more "Player A said X at time Y," just straight-up, "This move defends against the threat of..." You know, the good stuff. It's about preserving the *wisdom* of the comments, not the fluff surrounding it. Let's make this data truly usable for understanding the strategies!
