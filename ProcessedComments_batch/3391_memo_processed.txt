**Focusing the Lens: Refining Commentary for Sharp Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common challenge in my work: raw commentary from Shogi games.  It's a goldmine of potential, but often buried under a mountain of irrelevant information.  My goal is clear: to strip away the noise and get down to the pure essence of the game's evolution. I need to distill those comments into lean, focused explanations of the board state and the moves, the strategic thinking laid bare.  Think of it as forensic analysis, but for the flow of a Shogi match.  Anything that doesn't contribute to that understanding has to go - the metadata, the player introductions, the time stamps... all extraneous baggage.  The real value lies in the nuanced interpretations of each move and its impact on the board.
