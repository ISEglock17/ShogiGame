**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and I need to clean it up. The goal here is pretty clear: I've got a mountain of text, but I only want the gold – the actual *insight* into the game.  I need to strip away all the fluff. That means ditching the metadata, the player introductions (I already know who they are!), and the timestamps – that stuff clogs up the signal.  My job is to extract the pure analysis, the explanations of why a move was played, the evaluation of the position, the strategic thinking.  I want to be left with a stream of natural-sounding sentences that accurately convey the game's unfolding in a way that's useful for in-depth analysis.  I'm ready to figure out the best way to tackle this.
