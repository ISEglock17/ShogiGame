**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Comments for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game records. They're filled with all sorts of extras – metadata, player intros, timestamps – stuff that's just noise when I'm trying to analyze the actual moves and understand the strategy. My goal is to extract the *meaningful* bits: the on-the-board explanations of why a move was played, and leave the analysis in a natural, readable format.
