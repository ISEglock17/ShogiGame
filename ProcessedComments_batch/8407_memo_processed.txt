**Streamlining Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff and keep only the insightful analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. I need to get rid of anything that's irrelevant to pure tactical understanding.

This means I'll be meticulously going through each comment, move by move, and applying these rules:

First, any mention of people *not* directly involved in the game, their names, titles, or affiliations? Gone. No distractions.

Second, I need to excise any references to past games, precedents, or historical context. We're focused on *this* game, *this* moment.

Third, all time-related details? Out. We're concerned with the flow of the game, not the clock.

Next up, all the pre-game bios, records, rankings – anything that sets the stage but doesn't contribute to move-by-move understanding – that's all irrelevant and gets cut.

Finally, I'm going to eliminate descriptions of playing styles or tactical tendencies before or after they directly impact the board. I need this data to be as lean and focused as possible for my analysis.

The aim is to distill the commentary down to its essence: the strategic and tactical heart of the game.
