Okay, so the user wants me to do some heavy lifting with these Shogi game comments. They've got a pile of data, likely a database or file full of commentary accompanying Shogi games. The core problem is that this commentary is cluttered. It's got all sorts of extraneous stuff – metadata about the game itself, introductions to the players, time stamps, you name it. The real gold, the meat of the insights, is buried.

What they *really* want is the essential stuff: the specific board positions, the explanations of moves, and the strategic thinking behind them. The aim is to make the remaining text flow like natural explanations, readable and understandable. My goal is to extract the pure analytical nuggets from the raw data and make them digestible for further study. It’s like refining ore into pure metal.
