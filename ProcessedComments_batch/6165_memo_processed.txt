They need me to take this mess of comments, which are essentially raw data from a game, and transform it into something usable, something *analytically* valuable.

Essentially, I need to strip out all the fluff. I'm talking about the metadata, the player introductions, the time stamps – the stuff that's interesting for a casual viewer but clutters the information I need. What I *do* need are the actual explanations of the moves and board positions. I'm after the core insights, the rationale behind the strategies, the tactical considerations that experts like myself would be interested in.

The goal is to leave behind a concise and natural-sounding commentary that reads more like a coherent analysis than a jumble of raw notes. Think of it like distilling the essence of the game into a pure, insightful narrative. This is the gold I'm after.
