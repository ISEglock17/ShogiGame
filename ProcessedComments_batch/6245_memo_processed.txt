**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm dealing with a lot of Shogi commentary data, and honestly, it's a mess. I need to get this stuff into a clean, usable format for my analysis. Right now, it's packed with all sorts of extraneous information – things like meta-context, player introductions, and timing details. My goal is to strip all of that out and leave only the crucial bits: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves being made.

Essentially, I'm aiming for the kind of concise, insightful commentary that helps me *understand* the game better, not get bogged down in the fluff. So, my plan is to go through the comments systematically. I'll need to define clear rules about what constitutes "unnecessary information" and then apply those rules diligently to each comment. Anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the current board position or the reasoning behind a specific move gets the boot. It's a bit of a clean-up operation, but the end result will be a much more valuable dataset for my research.
