**Structuring and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up and structure some Shogi game commentary, and this is right up my alley. I need to take this raw text and really refine it, removing all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta stuff, the introductions, time checks, all that fluff – and leave behind only the core analysis of the board position and the actual moves made.

My goal is clear: strip away everything that isn't directly related to the game's evolution and present the remaining insights as cohesive, easy-to-understand sentences.  It's about getting to the heart of the strategic thinking without the distractions.

The process is straightforward: first, I'll go through the commentary and, based on the established rules, identify and eliminate all the parts that don't contribute to the technical analysis of the game. Then, I'll work on smoothing out the remaining text, making sure it flows naturally and effectively communicates the key points of each position and move. It's about preserving the analytical essence, while enhancing its readability.
