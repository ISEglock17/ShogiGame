**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi commentary logs. Their goal is clear: they want to extract the core strategic insights from the noise. I understand the task – to trim away all the fluff, leaving only the essential explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. My thinking is to approach this systematically, using the defined rules as a guide.

First, I need to filter out the irrelevant biographical details. Names of commentators who aren't actively playing, their ranks, affiliations – that's all excess baggage that can distract from the actual game analysis. Similarly, any references to past games, precedents, or time-related information are off the table. They don't contribute to understanding the present board position.

Essentially, I'm focusing on the *present*. I'll remove any commentary that wanders into player profiles, win/loss records, or discussions of their general playing styles or preferred openings. This will streamline the data and keep it laser-focused on the evolving strategic battle unfolding on the board. This way, I'll be able to quickly gain more insight into the moves and tactics.
