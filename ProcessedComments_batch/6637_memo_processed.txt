**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to refine these comments, essentially stripping away the fluff and leaving behind the core strategic analysis of the moves. I need to get rid of anything that isn't directly related to the position on the board or the reasoning behind a specific move – no more introductions, time stamps, or extraneous details. Just the good stuff, the clear explanation of why a move is played and its impact on the game. Essentially, I'm aiming for concise, useful insights that help build a strong understanding of each stage of the match.
