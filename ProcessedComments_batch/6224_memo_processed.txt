**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of cleaning up these shogi game annotations. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff and keep only the gold – the insightful commentary on the board positions and specific moves. Essentially, I'm filtering out the noise to focus on the signal.

My approach is systematic, rule-based. I've been given a pretty solid set of guidelines, and I'll be methodically applying them to each comment associated with each move.

Here's the gist of what I'm looking to eliminate:

*   **Irrelevant Names & Credentials:** No more mentions of non-players' names, ranks, or who they studied under. I'm solely interested in the game itself.
*   **Historical Distractions:** Any references to past games, precedents, or similar situations are out. It's about this specific game, this moment.
*   **Time-Wasting Tidbits:** All timing information needs to go. The clocks are irrelevant here.
*   **Player Bios & Stats:** Any background information on the players – their records, ranks, and numbers – is just background noise. Only the game matters.
*   **Stylistic Generalities:** Tendencies towards a particular style or set of attacks are not going to be kept.
*   **Venue Details:** Locations, weather, hotel info... all extraneous. These details aren't relevant to understanding the moves.

It's about isolating the tactical and strategic insights, removing the context. That's the essence of the work.
