**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task. Someone wants me to clean up and refine comments associated with Shogi game data. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff – any metadata, player intros, timestamps – anything that isn't directly related to the actual moves and the board state analysis. Basically, I need to distill each comment down to its essence: a clear, natural-sounding explanation of the board or a specific move.

Essentially, I'm thinking of it as a process of surgical precision. I need to be able to identify what's crucial for the analysis and what's extraneous, then remove the unnecessary bits according to provided rules and formats.
