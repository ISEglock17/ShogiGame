My goal is to transform those scattered notes into coherent, natural-sounding explanations of the game.

The process is pretty straightforward, and I've got a system for it. It's all about following a set of rules: I'll carefully analyze each comment, identify the irrelevant pieces of information based on those rules, and just...delete them. Basically, I'll be editing the comments down to their essence, the strategic heart of the game. No problem.
