**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Focusing on the Game Itself**

Alright, so the task at hand is to clean up these comments that come with Shogi game data. The goal? To take all the excess noise – you know, the meta stuff, the introductions to players, the time stamps – and strip it all away. What I'm after is the good stuff, the real meat of the analysis: the insights into the board state and the explanations of why a particular move was played.  Essentially, I want to create a natural-flowing narrative of the game, free from any distracting clutter. Let's get to it.
