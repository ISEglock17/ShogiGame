**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is to focus specifically on the game itself – the analysis of the board position and the moves made. We want to strip away all the extraneous noise, the meta information, player introductions, time stamps, you name it. The aim is to get a clean, coherent narrative of the game's unfolding strategy.

The process is pretty straightforward, thankfully, with a very detailed set of rules to guide the editing. It's essentially a three-step process: identify and delete the unwanted elements based on the rules, then rephrase what's left into natural, readable sentences. Finally, format the output as requested.

The first step in pruning the commentary is to identify the non-essential content based on a clear set of guidelines. This is the first and most critical rule: anything that isn't directly related to the game's flow will be excised. This includes any mention of people outside the direct game itself.
