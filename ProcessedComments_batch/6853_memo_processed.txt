**Focusing the Signal: Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I've got this data set, a bunch of Shogi game records.  What I really need to do is refine the commentary accompanying each move. Right now, it's a bit cluttered. There's a lot of noise in there – metadata, quick character introductions, time stamps, you know, the usual things that accumulate during a live game broadcast.  

My goal here is pretty straightforward: I want to surgically remove all the unnecessary information and isolate the truly insightful stuff.  I'm after clean, natural language explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Think of it like a distilled essence of the strategic thinking, ready for deeper analysis.  

Essentially, I'm aiming to transform raw commentary into a streamlined and digestible form, primed for sophisticated pattern recognition and game theory applications. It's about optimizing the signal-to-noise ratio in my data. I'm ready to dive into the process...
