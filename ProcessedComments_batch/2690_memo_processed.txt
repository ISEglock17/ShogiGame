**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task in my field: cleaning up the mess that often comes with game annotations. Someone wants me to take a bunch of commentary from shogi games, and strip away all the extraneous fluff.  I mean, who needs to know about the players' life stories, the clock readings, or the meta-commentary about the broadcast? It's all noise when I'm trying to analyze the actual moves and the ideas behind them.  My goal is to distill these comments down to the pure essence:  a natural-sounding explanation of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.  That's where the real insight lies.  Now I need to start figuring out *how* I'm going to accomplish this. It's time to get to work.
