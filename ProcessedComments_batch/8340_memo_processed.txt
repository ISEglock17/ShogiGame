**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to really refine them, to make them useful for deep analysis of the games themselves. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, introductions, time stamps, you name it. It's all cluttering up what I actually need: clear, concise explanations of the board state and the moves being made. I want to get to the heart of the strategic thinking, the tactical nuances, the real *why* behind each move. So the first thing I need to do is filter out all the extraneous details. I want only the pure commentary, crafted into a smooth, readable narrative that explains what's happening on the board.
