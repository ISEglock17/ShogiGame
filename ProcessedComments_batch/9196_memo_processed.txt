**Focusing the Lens: Refining Commentary for Sharp Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task in my work: cleaning up commentary from shogi game data. I need to take these often-verbose transcripts, overflowing with extraneous details, and distill them into something truly useful for analysis. My primary goal here is laser-focused: I want to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the pure essence of the game's unfolding: clear, concise explanations of the board state and the moves made. The aim is to make the data more amenable to computational processing, and to create a more direct connection between my analytical focus and the actual events of the game.
