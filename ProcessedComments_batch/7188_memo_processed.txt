**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward: I need to transform raw commentary into something useful for serious game analysis. That means stripping out all the noise – the meta-commentary, player introductions, time stamps, you know, all the fluff that doesn't contribute to understanding the *actual* game states and moves. 

Essentially, I need to distil these comments down to their essence: clear, natural language explanations of the board positions and the reasoning behind each move. That's the real gold here for my work, the pure strategic insight. My plan is simple: identify, then excise, the irrelevant bits based on a set of clearly defined rules. That should get me to the good stuff, the core tactical and strategic elements.
