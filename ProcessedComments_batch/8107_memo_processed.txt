**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments associated with each move and turn them into something much more focused and useful. My goal is to extract the *core* analysis of the position and the moves played, stripping away all the fluff that gets in the way.  Things like player introductions, time information for each move (that's all in the timestamps anyway), and any general meta-commentary need to go. I just want the pure, insightful explanation of what's happening on the board. So, I need to come up with a set of rules – almost like a filter – to identify and remove all that extraneous stuff.  The end product should be a series of coherent sentences that break down the board state and the reasoning behind each move, as if I'm walking myself through the game again.
