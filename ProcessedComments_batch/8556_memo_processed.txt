**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this Shogi commentary cleanup. The user wants me to go through these game data comments and essentially distill them down to their purest form – the insightful explanations of the board state and the moves, stripping away all the fluff. I get it. We need to eliminate anything that's not directly relevant to the strategic analysis.

Here's how I'm thinking I'll approach it. First, I'll need a solid set of rules to identify the unwanted bits. This means recognizing and removing the meta-commentary, like author introductions, time stamps, and any general context that doesn't actually help us understand *why* a particular move is good or bad. Then, and this is crucial, I'll need to figure out how to take the remaining information and shape it into proper, flowing sentences. It's not just about deleting; it's about re-articulating the essence of the analysis in a clean, clear way.
