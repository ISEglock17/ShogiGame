**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, right? My task is pretty straightforward: get rid of the fluff and distill the comments down to their analytical core. I need to take the raw data and transform it into something lean and mean, perfect for in-depth analysis of the game.

The goal is to strip away all the extraneous noise – anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind the moves. That means stuff like introductions to the players, their rankings, their schools, timing information – all of that goes. My focus is purely on the board itself and the insights into the players' decision-making process.

Essentially, I'm aiming to extract the essence of the commentary, leaving behind only the natural language explanations of the positions and moves. It's like distilling a fine wine – removing all the sediment to reveal the pure flavor.
