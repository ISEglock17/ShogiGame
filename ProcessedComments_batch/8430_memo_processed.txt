**Cleaning Up Commentary: My Approach to Sharpening the Narrative**

Okay, so I'm tackling a project that's right up my alley. Someone wants me to refine the commentary that comes with shogi game records. The goal is clear: I need to strip away all the extraneous noise, the meta information, the fluff – anything that isn't directly related to the game's unfolding drama on the board. Basically, I'm aiming for a concise, fluid narrative that focuses solely on the board positions and the player's moves, each explained in a natural and understandable way.

My process will be methodical, guided by a specific set of rules. I'm essentially hunting down and eliminating any comments that fall outside of the core game analysis. I'm keeping an eye out for these specifically:

*   **Personas:** Gone are the mentions of anyone not directly involved in the current match, their ranking, their affiliation, and their past.
*   **The Past:** Any reference to prior games or historical context is flagged and excluded.
*   **The Clock:** Time stamps and time-related comments are on the chopping block.
*   **The Player's Backstory:** Player profiles, records, ranks, and affiliated groups have no place in the analysis.
*   **The Player's Playstyle:** Any talk of playing style or preferred openings must be removed.
*   **The Setting:** Location details, weather reports, and hotel mentions are strictly off-limits.
*   **The Intermissions:** Comments about meal breaks, rest periods, or match resumption also need to go.
*   **The Non-Essential Details:** Any other information that doesn't directly help in understanding the evolving board position or the moves is up for review.
