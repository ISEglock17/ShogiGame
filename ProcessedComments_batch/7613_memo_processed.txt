**Processing and Cleaning Up Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The goal is crystal clear: I need to transform raw comments from game data into polished, easily digestible explanations of the moves and board positions. Essentially, I'm stripping away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – anything that isn't directly relevant to analyzing the game's flow. My job is to leave behind clear, concise, and grammatically sound descriptions of the strategic decisions being made on the board.

It's a straightforward process, governed by a set of rules, and I'll methodically work through each move's commentary, applying those rules to sculpt the text into something truly useful for deeper analysis. That's the essence of what I'm doing.
