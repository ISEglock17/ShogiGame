**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary datasets. The goal here is pretty straightforward, but the devil's in the details, as always. Basically, I need to take these comments, which are probably full of all sorts of extra stuff – metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and strip it all away.  I want to be left with the good stuff: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves, presented in a way that *feels* natural and flowing.  

So, I'm thinking I'll proceed in a couple of clear steps. First, I'll need a set of defined rules to go through the commentary and decide what gets the axe. I'll need to be very precise about what constitutes "unnecessary information".  Then, the real work begins: taking what's left and smoothing it out into grammatically correct and coherent sentences. It's about taking the fragmented pieces and building them into something that someone would actually *want* to read as they're analyzing the game.
