**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one: I need to clean up the commentary associated with a Shogi game record. The goal is straightforward, and frankly, critical for any serious analysis. I've got to strip away all the extraneous noise – the metadata, the introductions of the players, the time stamps – and leave behind the core: the insightful, insightful, *useful* explanations of the board state and the moves being played. It's about distilling the essence of the commentary into something lean, something I can actually *use* for my research. Think of it as preparing the perfect ingredient, ready to be added to the recipe. It's the first step in unlocking true understanding.
