**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Dive Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with refining commentary from Shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to extract the pure gold – the insightful analysis of the board state and moves – and discard the noise. That means getting rid of all the fluff. I'm talking about the metadata that's not directly related to the strategic play, any background on the players, or even those annoying time stamps. The goal is to leave behind only the coherent, human-readable explanations of *why* moves were played and *what* they mean on the board.  It's about distilling the essence of the analysis.
