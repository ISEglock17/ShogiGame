What I really want to focus on are the *actual* explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. My goal is to end up with a smooth, readable narrative that focuses purely on the strategic reasoning behind the plays. It should be a coherent explanation, free of all the distractions, the sort of commentary I'd expect to see from a quality analysis.
