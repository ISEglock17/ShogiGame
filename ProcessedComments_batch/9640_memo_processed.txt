**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up the comments attached to shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform these sometimes clunky or overly detailed annotations into concise, readable explanations of the board state and the moves being made.

Essentially, I'm going to have to dive into each comment and surgically remove all the noise – the meta-information, the player introductions (I already *know* who's playing!), any time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the analysis of the move. It's about distilling the core ideas.

Then, once I've got the essentials, I'll work on making them flow naturally. This isn't just a data cleaning exercise; it's about making the commentary itself better and easier to understand for someone reading through the game record. It's a matter of crafting the information into clear, focused statements.
