**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary attached to Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the actual, valuable stuff: clear, concise explanations of the board state and the moves being made. Essentially, I need to distill the commentary down to its essence, making it useful for further, more focused analysis. It's a bit tedious, but the payoff will be much cleaner data for my work. So, the first step is to figure out the best way to do this.
