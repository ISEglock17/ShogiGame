**Streamlining Chess Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some chess game commentary, which is right up my alley. The goal is clear: the user wants a focused analysis, not all the fluff. I need to strip away anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the board state and the moves being played. That means ditching the introductions, the player biographies, the time stamps - everything except the core insights into the positions and the reasoning behind each move. My task is to transform these cluttered comments into a clean, flowing narrative that a serious player or analyst can immediately use. It's about getting to the heart of the game, highlighting the strategic thinking and tactical nuances. I'll need to develop a systematic approach to identify and remove the irrelevant portions while preserving the crucial explanations of the game.
