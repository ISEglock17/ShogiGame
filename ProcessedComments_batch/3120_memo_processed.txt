**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with these Shogi game comments, and the goal is clear: clean them up for analysis. I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, valuable insight: the board states and the rationale behind each move. That's the essence of the game's evolution, the key to truly understanding the strategies at play.

My process will be straightforward. First, I'll meticulously go through each comment, applying my established rules to identify and remove all the unnecessary elements. This is where experience really comes into play, as I've seen countless variations and patterns in these comments. Then, the challenge becomes *reforming* what remains. I'll need to transform the fragmented bits of commentary into coherent, flowing sentences that capture the nuances of the game as it unfolds. It's about distilling the core ideas and presenting them in a way that's both informative and engaging. This will make the data much more useful for any subsequent analysis.
