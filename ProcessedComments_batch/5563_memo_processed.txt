The goal is clear: I need to strip away all the fluff – you know, the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that stuff that's useful for a casual viewer but gets in the way of serious analysis. My job is to distill the commentary down to its purest form – clear, concise explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented as natural, flowing sentences. It's about getting to the heart of the game's evolution, not just the play-by-play.  This isn't about simply removing extraneous characters; it's about making the remaining commentary insightful.
