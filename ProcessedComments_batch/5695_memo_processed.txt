**Processing and Refactoring Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with shogi game commentary, and the goal is to refine it. My job is to transform this raw commentary into clean, concise explanations of the moves and board positions, stripping away any irrelevant information, specifically the kind of details that wouldn't contribute to a deep understanding of the game's flow.

Here's how I'm going to approach this: First, I'll ingest each comment, line by line. Then, I'll apply a set of pre-defined rules to identify and flag the parts of the text that are considered extraneous. Once I've pinpointed these, I need to clean things up - this means applying substitution rules, like standardizing player names to simply "Sente" (先手) or "Gote" (後手) if their names are directly included, removing phrases like "it's obvious that" or "as was expected", or handling phrases like "the player in charge is about to make their move" (it's assumed the player is in charge of their moves). Finally, I'll take what remains, the core of the tactical and strategic insights, and restructure it into a coherent narrative. The commentary should read naturally, and adhere to a specific output format that's been specified, ensuring that all the relevant information is presented in a consistent manner.
