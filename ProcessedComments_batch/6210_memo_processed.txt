**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Streamlining for Deeper Analysis**

My goal is to refine these comments into something truly valuable for my analysis. Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, introductions, time stamps...all the things that aren't *directly* about the board state or the moves themselves. That's got to go.

The process is straightforward enough in theory: first, I need to define the rules of what constitutes "noise" – what's irrelevant and can be safely discarded. Once I've established that filter, I can start systematically removing those elements from the comments. 

Then, and this is where it gets interesting, I can focus on crafting the remaining snippets into more cohesive, natural sentences.  This will make it far easier to read and parse the commentary.
