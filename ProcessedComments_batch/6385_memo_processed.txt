**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and I need to clean it up. The goal here is pretty clear: I want to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the metadata, the introductions of the players, the time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the moves being made. Essentially, I'm aiming to transform that raw data into a narrative that flows naturally, focusing solely on the strategic considerations that make the game interesting. This is all about distilling the commentary to its essence, ensuring that it’s concise, relevant, and a pleasure to read.
