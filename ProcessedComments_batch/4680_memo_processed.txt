**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've got this task of cleaning up and refining commentary from Shogi games, right? The user wants me to go through these comments from game data and turn them into something really focused and insightful. Essentially, they want the raw gold - the actual explanations of board positions and moves - without all the fluff that often gets bundled in.

My goal is to surgically remove all the noise – the meta-commentary, player introductions, time-related details, you know, anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the game's flow and strategic nuances.  I'll be following a set of rules (presumably provided or implicit from context) to identify what needs to go.  Then, after I've stripped away all the unnecessary bits, I'll work on smoothing out what's left, ensuring it reads like a coherent and natural explanation of the game's crucial moments.  That's the core of it.
