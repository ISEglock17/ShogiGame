**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm looking at a dataset of comments from Shogi games, and the goal is to clean them up. I need to get rid of all the extra fluff – the stuff that's not actually *about* the game itself. That means no more metadata, no player introductions, and certainly no time stamps. I want to be left with the core of the analysis, the insightful explanations of the board state and the moves being made, presented as a clear, flowing narrative. Essentially, I'm aiming for a concise and valuable breakdown of the strategic thinking behind each move, leaving out all the noise.
