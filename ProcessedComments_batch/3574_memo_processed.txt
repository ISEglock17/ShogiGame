**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of refining Shogi commentary from game data. The goal is clear: I need to transform these comments into concise, readable explanations of the board state and moves, stripping away all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of the metadata, player introductions, and timestamps – anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the game's flow.

Here's how I see this working: First, I'll need a set of rules, essentially a checklist, to identify and flag the information that needs to go. Once I've filtered out all the extraneous details, I can then focus on the real task: crafting the remaining bits into a cohesive and flowing narrative that highlights the strategic nuances of each move and the resulting changes on the board.
