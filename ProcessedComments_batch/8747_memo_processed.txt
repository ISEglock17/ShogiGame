**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this dataset of Shogi game comments. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to whip these comments into shape so they're useful for deep analysis of the games. Think of it like stripping down a complex piece of code to its essential functions.

Right now, the comments are bloated with extra stuff that's just noise for my purposes. We're talking metadata, player introductions (who cares who they are, I just want the game!), time stamps (irrelevant to the move's impact), and anything else that's not directly related to the actual positions on the board or the moves themselves.

My focus is laser-sharp: I need to preserve the *meaningful* commentary – the juicy stuff that explains the board states, the tactical considerations, the strategic thinking behind each move. And ideally, I want to leave it in a format that reads as natural language, not just a bunch of disjointed phrases. This is where I can start extracting the insights I want!
