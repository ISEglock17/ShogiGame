**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Comments for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi game comments. They've got a dataset full of them, but it's cluttered.  My job is to extract the juicy bits – the actual game analysis – and discard the noise. Think of it like a really detailed signal-to-noise ratio problem.

Essentially, I need to strip away anything irrelevant to understanding the board state and the moves themselves. That means getting rid of all the metadata: who's playing, their ranking, their affiliation, even time information. My focus needs to be laser-sharp on the commentary that directly explains what's happening on the board, move by move.

I'll be tackling this systematically, following a set of specific rules for what to keep and what to toss. The first rule is straightforward:  *anyone* mentioned who *isn't* directly involved in the game – no name, rank, or affiliation. I'll need to develop a smart way to detect these non-player mentions and filter them out.
