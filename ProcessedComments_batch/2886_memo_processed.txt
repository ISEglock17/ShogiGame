**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments and turn it into something useful for analysis. My goal here is to strip out all the extraneous fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know the drill – and just leave behind the core insights about the board state and the moves themselves.

It seems pretty straightforward in principle, just a matter of following a few rules. First, I need to identify and remove all the information that doesn't contribute to explaining the game. Then, once I've got a clean slate, I'll need to rephrase what's left into natural, understandable sentences. Easy peasy, really. This will allow for more focused, data-driven insights.
