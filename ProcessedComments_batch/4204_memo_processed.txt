**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Sharp Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling this interesting dataset of shogi game commentary, and my goal is to really refine it. Basically, I want to take these raw comments attached to each move and boil them down to their most potent essence. The objective is to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, the introductions, the time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, insightful commentary about the board state and the moves themselves. My focus is laser sharp: to extract the core, the essence of the analysis.
