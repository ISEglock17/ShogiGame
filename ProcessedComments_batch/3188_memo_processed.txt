**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I've got this task of cleaning up and refining the comments found within Shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to streamline these comments, essentially filtering out the noise and focusing on the actual strategic insights that matter for analysis. I need to get rid of all the extraneous stuff - metadata, introductions of the players, time stamps - and leave behind only the crucial analysis tied directly to the board state and individual moves.

My approach is pretty straightforward:

1.  **Filtering:** I'll begin by systematically going through each comment and applying a set of rules to determine what's truly relevant and what needs to be removed. This will be the heavy lifting, the first pass to get rid of the obvious fluff.
2.  **Smoothing:** With the core strategic content isolated, the next step is to make it read more naturally. This might involve restructuring sentences, filling in gaps, and ensuring the remaining comments flow together coherently as a coherent analysis of the game.
