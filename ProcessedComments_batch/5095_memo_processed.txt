My mental process is pretty straightforward. First, I need to establish a clear set of rules for identifying and removing the extraneous information. This requires a sharp eye for the common elements found in these comment streams. Then, it's about re-weaving what's left into coherent, flowing prose. This second part involves understanding the language nuances used within shogi analysis – ensuring the tone and terminology feel right.
