**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments associated with each move and turn them into something much more focused. The goal is clear: I need to isolate the crucial information about the board state and the reasoning behind each move, and ditch all the extraneous noise.

Right now, the comments are cluttered with all sorts of things that aren't directly relevant to analyzing the game. There's metadata about the game itself, introductions to the players, timestamps, and other fluff. My job is to act as a filter, removing all that clutter and leaving behind only the pure, concise analysis of the moves themselves.
