**Structuring Game Commentary for Clarity**

Okay, so I'm presented with a block of text, presumably a commentary on a Shogi match. My task is to refine this commentary, ensuring it's both clean and focused. The user wants the essence of the game to shine through, not extraneous details.

My process will be methodical. I'll break down the commentary into individual "moves" or time steps. Each move's associated commentary will be meticulously examined against a set of predetermined "deletion rules". Any information that falls into the "unnecessary for understanding the game's flow" category will be excised. The goal is to retain only the critical elements: the board state explanations and the descriptions of the moves themselves, all presented in a coherent, flowing narrative.
