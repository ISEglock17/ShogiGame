**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary data, and my goal is to refine it. I need to make the text useful for analyzing the games.  The key is to remove the noise and keep the signal.  I want to strip out all the fluff that a human might enjoy reading – the meta information, the introductions of the players, time stamps – and focus *only* on the critical analysis: the positions on the board and the explanations of the moves themselves, all presented in flowing, natural language.  Essentially, I want to create a clean, dense, and insightful record of the game's key decisions. I've got a plan for how to do this.
