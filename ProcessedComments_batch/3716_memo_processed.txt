**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to refine the text accompanying the moves, stripping out all the fluff. My goal here is laser-focused: I need to isolate the insightful, relevant commentary related to the board positions and the specific move choices made.

This means I need to become a digital editor of sorts. I'll have to meticulously comb through the comments, following a specific set of rules. I'll be vigilant for anything that's not directly related to *why* a move was played or *what* it accomplishes on the board.  That means the personal introductions, the time-stamping, and any general, non-analytical observations get the boot.  I need to leave only the distilled essence of the expert analysis. It's all about making the data ready for further, in-depth analysis of the game's evolution.
