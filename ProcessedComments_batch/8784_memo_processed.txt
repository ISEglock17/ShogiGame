**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I need to take these Shogi game comments and really clean them up. The user wants me to get rid of all the extraneous junk – the metadata, player intros, timestamps – and just leave the good stuff: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves. They want natural-sounding sentences, not just a bunch of disjointed phrases.

Essentially, I see this as a two-step process: First, I'll need to create a specific set of rules to identify and remove all the unwanted information. This will involve understanding what types of phrases and data are universally irrelevant to the analysis of the game's actual moves. Then, I'll have to figure out how to best turn the remaining fragments into coherent, understandable sentences. This will take some careful thinking about how to connect these thoughts together.
