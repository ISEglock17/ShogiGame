**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with refining commentary from Shogi game data. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to strip away all the fluff – you know, the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that noise that's irrelevant to pure game analysis. I want to leave behind only the genuinely insightful commentary on the board positions and individual moves, expressed in a natural, flowing way.

Essentially, I'll be applying a set of rules to each piece of commentary. First, I'll identify and discard anything that violates those rules. Then, I'll work on smoothing out what's left, making sure it reads like proper prose, not just a collection of disconnected phrases. Finally, I'll package the results according to the specified output format, ensuring a clean and focused deliverable.
