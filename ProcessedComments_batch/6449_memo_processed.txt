**Processing and Cleaning Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one actually. It seems someone needs me to refine and standardize comments found within Shogi game records. My goal, as I understand it, is to extract the pure, analytical commentary about the game itself, the strategic insights into the moves, the reasoning behind the choices – all the good stuff. I need to strip away the noise.  This means eliminating anything that's not directly related to the board position or the player's thinking at that moment.

I've got a set of rules, clearly defined, and I'll need to meticulously apply them to each comment associated with each move.  It's a straightforward process, really. First, I'll identify and excise the irrelevant parts: the meta-data, the player introductions (I already know who's playing), timing information, and any extraneous fluff.  I'll then be left with the core of the commentary.

It's about getting down to the essence, ensuring the remaining text is a concise, natural explanation of the move and its implications on the board. This, I imagine, will be immensely useful for further analysis, like running it through an engine, or refining my understanding of these games.
