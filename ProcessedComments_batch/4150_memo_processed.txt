**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Comments for Sharper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common challenge: I've got a mountain of comments attached to Shogi game data, and it's a mess. My goal is to refine these comments, to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and distill them down to pure, actionable insights about the board state and the moves themselves. I want the essence of the analysis, the tactical and strategic observations, presented in a clear, flowing narrative. This is crucial for efficient post-game analysis and for building robust training datasets for AI or even just my own study. My approach needs to be surgical, removing the extraneous without accidentally discarding anything valuable about the game's actual unfolding.
