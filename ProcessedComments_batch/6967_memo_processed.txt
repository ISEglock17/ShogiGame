**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I need to take a look at these Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to streamline them, to get rid of all the fluff and just leave the core analysis – the actual insights into the moves. I'm thinking, anything that's not directly related to explaining the board position or the reasoning behind a specific move needs to go. That means things like:

*   **Metadata:** Game dates, player names, tournament details, that sort of thing.
*   **Introductions:** Any background information on the players or the game itself.
*   **Time stamps:**  We don't need to know how long each player thought about each move; that's not relevant to understanding the strategy.
*   **Non-analytical commentary:** I need to remove the sentences that are not useful for a deeper study of the game's tactics.

Essentially, I want to filter out the noise and leave only the pure, distilled essence of the expert commentary on the moves themselves.
