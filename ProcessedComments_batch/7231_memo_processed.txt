Essentially, I'm going to carefully go through each move's commentary, and, following the specified rules, make smart decisions about what gets discarded and what gets preserved. It's a surgical process, ensuring that the valuable strategic insights remain while the extraneous details are neatly excised.  This will make the data much more useful for future analysis.
