The process itself is well-defined, thankfully. It breaks down into a few distinct steps.

First, I need to methodically comb through each comment, applying a set of clearly established rules to identify and ruthlessly eliminate the irrelevant information. It's about surgical precision here – anything not directly contributing to the explanation of the moves goes.

Then, once I've got the core content, I need to shape it into something coherent. It's about crafting those raw analytical insights into complete, grammatically sound sentences. Basically, I'm transforming scattered notes into a readable, flowing narrative of the game's critical moments.

Finally, I'll need to organize the refined commentary according to the specified output format. This could involve, for instance, structuring the data to be easily used by analytical tools, or preparing it for presentation in a more user-friendly form. So, it's about making sure the information is not only accurate but also accessible and useful for the user.
