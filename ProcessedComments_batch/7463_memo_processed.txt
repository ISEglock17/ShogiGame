**Structuring and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm dealing with shogi game commentary, and the goal here is pretty clear: I need to whip these comments into shape. Think of it like a textual distillation – stripping away the extraneous bits and focusing on what truly matters for analysis. I want to highlight the critical information: the board positions and the actual moves played, all presented in a way that feels natural, flowing, and easily digestible.

The core of this is following the provided rules to the letter. Each individual comment needs to be carefully examined, judged against those rules, and then sculpted. It's about discerning what's essential and what's noise, and then ruthlessly discarding the latter. The process involves a deep dive into each piece of commentary, a critical eye, and a firm grasp of the underlying principles to get this right.
