**Refining Commentary in Shogi Data**

Okay, so I'm presented with this Shogi game data, and my goal is to really polish the commentary for analysis. I'm focusing on cleaning up the text that accompanies each move. It's often cluttered with all sorts of extraneous bits – you know, the meta stuff, introductions of players, time stamps... all that jazz that's not actually *about* the board position or the player's reasoning behind the move.

My plan is pretty straightforward. I'll need to define some clear rules about what's considered "noise" and needs to go. I'll scrutinize each comment, identify the stuff that falls under those rules, and just... get rid of it. The real payoff comes next: I'll carefully rephrase what's left into natural, coherent sentences that really highlight the strategic thinking behind the moves and the evolving board state. Essentially, I'm aiming to distill the essence of the analysis, creating a clean dataset ripe for deeper understanding.
