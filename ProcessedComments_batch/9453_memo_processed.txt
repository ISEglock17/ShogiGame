The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform these raw comments into something useful for analyzing the actual gameplay. That means stripping out all the noise – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you know, all the fluff that doesn't contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind specific moves. I want to be left with concise, natural-sounding explanations of the game as it unfolds, focusing solely on the board and the moves. The process is pretty straightforward, and I'll lay it out accordingly.
