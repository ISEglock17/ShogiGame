**Refining Commentary for Deeper Chess Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to shogi game data. The goal here is to strip away all the fluff – you know, the meta stuff, the player introductions, time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, distilled insights into the board state and the moves being played. I want to end up with clear, concise explanations of the moves themselves.

I've got a set of rules to guide me, and I'm going through each hand's comment meticulously. I'm focusing on weeding out any mention of people *other* than the players involved, getting rid of any unnecessary historical context like past games or precedent, and anything else that doesn't directly speak to the on-board strategic thinking. The aim is to create a streamlined, focused narrative of the game's evolution that aids deeper analysis.
