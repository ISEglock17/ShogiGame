**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. What I need to do is clean them up, essentially. The goal is to isolate the *actual* analysis and explanation of the moves themselves, removing all the extraneous noise.  Think about it, most of these commentaries have a bunch of fluff - player introductions, timing details, maybe some unrelated historical context. It's clutter! I want to streamline this so that I'm just left with a clean, concise breakdown of the board state and the reasoning behind each move.  That's the core of the analysis, and that's what I'm aiming for.  Get rid of the distractions, focus on the strategy.
