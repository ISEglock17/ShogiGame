**Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Things like metadata about the game, introductions of the players, time stamps – they're just noise for my purposes. What I need are the pure insights, the explanations of the board state and the moves, presented in a way that actually flows like natural language. Essentially, I'm transforming raw commentary into something readily digestible for deeper analysis of the game's strategies. This process, as I understand it, requires a methodical approach, removing the clutter and revealing the core ideas.
