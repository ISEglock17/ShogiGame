Okay, so the user wants me to clean up Shogi game commentary. They have data from matches, and it's full of extra stuff – metadata, player introductions, time stamps – you know, all the clutter that gets in the way of actually *analyzing* the game itself. My job is to transform this commentary into pure gold for the analytical process.

Here's the plan: first, I need to understand the defined rules. Then I need to identify the extraneous information in the commentary according to these rules. Finally, I will remove that unnecessary fluff and refine the remaining text into smooth, coherent explanations of the board state and the moves being made. The ultimate goal is to generate a pristine dataset containing only the core game insights. It's about getting to the meat of the matter.
