**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to Shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to take these raw comments and transform them into something truly useful for analysis. I need to strip out all the noise – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – essentially anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the moves themselves. My objective is to distill these comments down to the core essence: clear, flowing explanations of the board positions and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a natural, readable format. This is about creating a refined commentary that truly helps shed light on the game's evolution.
