The goal is pretty clear: to take these comments, which are often a jumble of analysis, personal asides, timing info, and player introductions, and distill them down to just the pure, insightful explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. Essentially, I need to extract the essence of the analysis and present it in a flowing, readable format.
