**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to distill them down to their core essence: the analysis of the board state and the moves themselves. Essentially, I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – that meta-information, those introductions, time stamps... anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the flow of the game.

My plan is straightforward: first, I'll apply a set of rules, gleaned from my expertise, to identify and discard those irrelevant elements. It's a matter of discerning what's crucial for the *analysis* versus what's just context. Then, once I've got the essentials, I'll finesse the remaining text, shaping it into clear, flowing sentences. This requires a level of judgement and understanding of the game itself, to preserve the nuances of the commentary while removing the clutter. It's about getting to the heart of the insights shared during the match.
