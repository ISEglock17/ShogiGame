**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Comments for Analysis**

Okay, so the goal here is pretty clear: We've got a mountain of comments attached to these Shogi game records, and it's time to refine them. My mission, as I see it, is to extract the pure analytical gold from the noise. I need to strip away all the extraneous stuff - the fluff, the introductions, the clock-watching, the side notes - and leave behind only the essence of the game.

What I'm after are those concise, insightful explanations of *why* a move was made, *what* it accomplishes on the board, and *what* the strategic implications are. It's about distilling the raw tactical and strategic commentary into a clean, coherent narrative. I want these comments to read like a natural explanation, not a laundry list of metadata. This isn't just about deleting; it's about *transforming* the data into something truly valuable for deeper analysis and understanding of the game.
