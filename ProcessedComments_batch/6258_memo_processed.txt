**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Basically, I want to strip away all the fluff that's irrelevant to the actual gameplay analysis. That means anything like metadata, introductions of the players, time stamps, or other non-essential details need to go. My focus is on retaining the valuable insights related to the board positions and the actual explanations of each move. That's the core of the valuable data.
