What I need to do is distill the commentary down to its purest form: concise, natural-sounding explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. That's the real gold we're after, the gems that will drive our understanding of the game further. So, the first step is figuring out how to identify and then systematically remove the non-essential stuff. I imagine I'll need to develop some sort of system or filter to accomplish this task.
