**Cleaning Up Those Shogi Comments**

Okay, so I've got this dataset of Shogi game comments and the goal is pretty straightforward: get rid of the noise and leave behind the *good stuff* - the actual board analysis and explanations of moves. Basically, I want to refine these comments into clean, natural sentences that actually help understand the game's flow.

I'm starting by carefully examining each comment according to a predetermined set of rules. I'll need to identify and strip out all the extraneous details: the metadata, the introductions of players, any time-related information – anything that doesn't contribute directly to the strategic analysis. It's a bit of a surgical process, but the payoff will be a much cleaner and more insightful dataset for further analysis.
