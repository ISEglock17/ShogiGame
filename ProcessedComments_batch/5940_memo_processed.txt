**Cleaning Up the Commentary: Streamlining for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I've got this set of comments associated with a shogi game, and my goal is to wrangle them into something truly useful for analysis. It's pretty straightforward, actually. Basically, I want to strip out all the noise – you know, the metadata, the introductions to players, the time stamps – all the stuff that's not directly about the *board* and the *moves*. I need to retain only the comments that are offering insight into the game itself: the explanations of the positions, the strategic reasoning, the evaluations of the candidate moves. My focus is on the core, the essence of the game's unfolding.
