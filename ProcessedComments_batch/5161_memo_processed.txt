**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up commentary from shogi games. The goal is to isolate the *core* analytical insights—the stuff that truly helps understand the board state and the moves being made. I need to strip out all the fluff: meta-commentary, biographical details about the players, time stamps, and any other irrelevant noise. My process will be pretty straightforward:

First, I'll go through each comment and apply a set of rules. These rules will act as a filter, allowing me to identify and excise the unnecessary information. Think of it like a very precise pruning of a sentence.

Next, I'll take what's left and smooth it out. I'll make sure the surviving pieces of commentary flow logically and read like natural, coherent explanations of the game. I want the commentary to be easy to digest for anyone familiar with the game.

Finally, I'll output the refined commentary in the specific format I was given. No surprises there. It's all about delivering a clean, focused, and truly useful resource for shogi analysis.
