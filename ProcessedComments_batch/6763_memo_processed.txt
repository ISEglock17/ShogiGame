Basically, they want me to leave behind only the genuinely insightful comments about the board position and the actual moves being played, presented in a smooth, readable format.

My plan is straightforward: I'll go through each move, one by one, and carefully analyze the associated comment. I'll need to figure out how to separate the wheat from the chaff, so to speak. I'll have to determine what to keep and what to discard, and also provide the reasons for my decisions. This is going to be about understanding the nuances of the game, not just blindly deleting text. It's about preserving the *useful* information for deeper strategic understanding.
