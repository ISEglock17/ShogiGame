**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Here's how I see it: I've got a set of rules – my expertise, really – for identifying and removing the noise. That means ditching the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps, all that stuff. It's about getting down to the core of what's happening on the board, move by move.

Then comes the finesse. Once I've stripped away the unnecessary bits, I'll need to smooth out the remaining text. Make sure it reads as a coherent explanation of the strategy and the flow of the game, not just a collection of disconnected phrases. It's about ensuring clarity for whoever is analyzing this, myself included.
