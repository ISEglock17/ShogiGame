My goal is to distill each comment down to its core: the actual explanation of the board state and the reasoning behind each played move. It’s like being a curator, selecting only the most valuable pieces of information from a vast archive. The process will be a systematic one - I'll meticulously apply the rules I have in place to make sure only the essential remains.
