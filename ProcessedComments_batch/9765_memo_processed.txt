**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deep Dive Analysis**

I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta stuff, the introductions to players, time stamps – all the stuff that's not actually relevant for a deep-dive analysis of the board positions and the strategies employed. What I really want is the core explanation of *why* each move was played, the tactical reasoning, the strategic implications, all rendered in natural, flowing language. Essentially, I need to distill each comment down to its essence of board state and move explanation. That's the key to making this data truly valuable for my research. So let's get started on the process.
