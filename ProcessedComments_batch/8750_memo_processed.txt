**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a Shogi game record and tasked with cleaning up the comments. The goal is clear: I need to transform those raw comments into something useful for serious Shogi analysis. That means stripping away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps... anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind a particular move. Essentially, I need to get to the heart of the matter: the actual explanations of the positions and the strategic thoughts that drove each play. I need to retain only the comments that provide genuine insight into the game, presenting them as naturally flowing sentences that could have come directly from a skilled analyst. This is a crucial step in preparing the data for deeper, more sophisticated Shogi analysis.
