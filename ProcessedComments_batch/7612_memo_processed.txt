**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, right? My task is pretty straightforward: get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, distilled essence of the board state and the moves themselves. Think of it like stripping away the excess fat to reveal the lean, muscular core of the game's unfolding narrative.

I've got a specific set of rules to follow, a kind of internal checklist. I'll go through each comment, one by one. First, I'll identify and excise the irrelevant bits, following those rules meticulously. It's like a surgical procedure, removing the extraneous tissue. Then, and this is crucial, I'll reshape the remaining information into something that reads naturally, something a human analyst could understand and appreciate. Finally, I'll spit out the refined comments in the requested format, ready for further, deeper analysis. That's the plan.
