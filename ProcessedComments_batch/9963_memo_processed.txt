**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my goal is clear: I need to clean it up. The task at hand is to take that raw text, likely full of extraneous details, and transform it into concise, insightful analysis. Essentially, I'm aiming to keep only the gold – the explanations of the board state and the significance of each move. Everything else – the meta-commentary, biographical tidbits about the players, the time spent thinking – that's all noise I need to filter out. My focus is on delivering a final product that's pure strategic analysis, expressed in natural, flowing language. I need to get to the heart of the game, move by move.
