Essentially, I want to create a clean, narrative flow of the game's evolution, focusing solely on the strategic thinking behind each move.

I'll be working through the comments on a move-by-move basis, applying a specific set of rules to determine what needs to go and what gets to stay. It's a process of careful filtering and judicious editing, ensuring that the remaining commentary provides a coherent and insightful analysis of the game's progression.
