**Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm diving into this shogi commentary data, and the goal is crystal clear: I need to whip these comments into shape for some serious game analysis. Right now, it's a bit cluttered with all sorts of extraneous information that's just getting in the way. I want to keep only the good stuff – the actual explanations of the board positions and the move-by-move strategies.

I've got a specific set of rules to guide me through this process. It's all about surgically removing the noise and leaving the pure essence of the game. So, any mention of people *not* involved in the current game – their names, ranks, who their sensei is – that's gotta go. Similarly, references to previous games or precedents are irrelevant for this purpose. Time stamps? Gone. Any background stuff on the players, their stats, styles, or affiliations?  Delete. Anything that isn't a direct insight into the specific move being made, or the position on the board, has to be removed.
