**Streamlining the Commentary: My Approach**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these shogi commentary snippets. The user wants them polished, focusing on the game itself, not all the extraneous noise.  Here's how I'm thinking about it:

First, get rid of anything that doesn't relate directly to the players and their moves. That means *poof* goes anyone else mentioned – no "Takahashi 9-dan says..." or discussions about the "review team." It's about the players, their strategy, and the unfolding game.

Then, I need to eliminate any references to the past. "This move mirrors a previous game..." or "Last year, the same situation arose..." – those are interesting in context, but not the core of the play-by-play.  We're keeping it focused on the *current* board state.

And finally, the time-check chatter needs to disappear. That includes things like "It's 11:09," "He's taking 43 minutes," or even a dramatic "He's down to the wire!" We can assume knowledgeable users can keep track of that info separately. The goal is a clean, concise analysis of the moves themselves, not a time log.
