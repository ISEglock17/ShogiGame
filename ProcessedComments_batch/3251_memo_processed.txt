**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task: a Shogi data set full of hand-written commentary, and I need to clean it up. The goal is clear - distill these comments down to their essence, the *actual* Shogi analysis, removing all the fluff. I'm talking about getting rid of the meta-data, the player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that's not directly explaining the board state or the reasoning behind a specific move.

Essentially, I need to perform a kind of linguistic surgery on these comments. I'll need a set of clear rules to identify and flag the information that's considered extraneous. Once I've excised all that unwanted material, I'll then smooth out what remains, transforming it into a coherent, natural-sounding explanation of the game's unfolding. It's about preserving the *insight* while discarding the noise. I need to get to the heart of what's being said about the game itself.
