**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with Shogi game commentary and my goal is clear: to sculpt it into something truly useful for analyzing the games. I need to strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps – everything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state and the moves being made. My focus should be laser-sharp on the *actual* analysis and insightful explanations of the positions and variations. I need to make sure I present a clear and readable commentary.
