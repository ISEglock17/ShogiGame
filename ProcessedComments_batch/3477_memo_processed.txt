**Cleaning Up My Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary data from shogi games. Essentially, they want me to take a messy collection of comments associated with each move and turn them into something much more focused and useful. My goal is to extract the pure, insightful explanations of the board state and the moves themselves, removing all the extraneous clutter.

This means I need to sift through each comment and be able to identify and ruthlessly eliminate things like metadata, introductions of the players, time information, and any other noise that isn't directly related to the actual analysis of the position or the move.  I need to distill each comment down to its essence – the "why" and "how" of each move – leaving a clear and concise explanation for each position and move.  It's about removing the context and fluff to reach the actual, insightful comments.
