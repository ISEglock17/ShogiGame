**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations. My goal here is to streamline them – get rid of all the noise, the fluff that doesn't actually help with the analysis. Things like who's playing, how long it took, all that meta information, it's just cluttering up the important stuff.

What I *really* want is the core of the annotations: the explanations of the board positions and the reasoning behind each move. I'm imagining a cleaned-up version, where the commentary flows naturally, focusing just on the game's strategic nuances. That's what I need for my analysis, a direct and concise look at the *why* behind the moves. Let's see how I can achieve this transformation.
