**Cleaning Up My Notes for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I've got this trove of game data from Shogi matches, right? And it's got these comments attached to each move. The problem is, it's a bit of a mess. There's all sorts of fluff in there – metadata I don't need, introductions to the players, time stamps – you know the drill. It's distracting and frankly, a bit of a hindrance when I'm trying to really dig into the strategic nuances.

My goal here is pretty straightforward: I want to surgically remove all that extraneous stuff and just leave the pure gold – the board state descriptions and the actual analysis of each move. I'm after a lean, mean, comment stream that reads like a focused, insightful discussion of the game. Thankfully, it seems like the process is well-defined, and the rules are clear-cut. This should be a fairly systematic cleaning operation.
