**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Game Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so the goal here is to refine the comments found in shogi game records. I need to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta stuff, the player intros, the time stamps – and just leave behind the pure, distilled essence: the explanations of the board state and the moves themselves. It's about getting to the heart of the analysis, the tactical and strategic insights, without getting bogged down in the peripheral details.

I'm thinking I'll need to work through the commentary move by move, applying some clearly defined rules.  The focus has to be on identifying and removing the irrelevant bits.  I've already got a list of what needs to go:

*   **Rule Out Player Distractions:** Anything that's not directly about the move itself needs to go. That includes other people's names, ranks, or details about their training – it's not relevant to understanding the gameplay.
*   **Keep it Current:**  I have to get rid of anything that's referencing prior games or historical context. The analysis needs to be focused on the current move and its implications, not a history lesson.
*   **Time Is Irrelevant:** I can't keep any time information.  It's useful for watching a game, but it just clutters the analysis, I need a clear presentation without these elements.

The goal is to produce a streamlined, concise version of the commentary that allows for focused and efficient analysis. I need to remove all of the junk!
