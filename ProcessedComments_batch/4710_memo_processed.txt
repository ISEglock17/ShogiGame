**Focusing My Efforts: Refining Game Commentary for Sharper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tackling a project that's right up my alley. I'm being asked to clean up and refine the comments found within shogi game data. The goal is clear: I need to extract the truly valuable information for analysis and ditch the noise. Think of it like a meticulous sort through a mountain of data, separating the gold from the gravel.

My primary objective is to hone in on the core of the game commentary. I want to keep only the insightful explanations of board positions and move evaluations. Everything else – the meta-commentary, biographical tidbits about the players, the time stamps... all the peripherals that aren't critical for understanding the flow of the game – needs to be trimmed. It's about data minimalism and maximum clarity.

The process is straightforward in principle: first, I'll apply a series of rules to identify and remove all the irrelevant portions. Once the extraneous material is gone, the remaining text will need a linguistic facelift. I'll focus on ensuring that the commentary reads fluidly, like a well-structured argument. So, it is about data cleaning and linguistic refinement to make the data more useful for me and other expert researchers.
