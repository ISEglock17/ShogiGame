**Processing Shogi Game Commentary: A Streamlined Approach**

Okay, so I've got this task of cleaning up these Shogi game commentaries, right? The user wants me to essentially distill the commentary down to its pure essence: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves, eliminating all the fluff. Think of it like taking the core logic of a proof and throwing away the introduction, the biographical notes on the players, and all that superfluous stuff.

My goal here is to transform these messy comments into something that's elegant and useful for, say, a deep dive into the strategic elements.

My process will be straightforward and rule-based. I'll need to first understand the specified rules, and then systematically go through each comment.

1.  **Filtering:** I'll meticulously scan each comment for elements that are marked for removal based on the rules. This includes, metadata, player intros, and time stamps. Essentially, anything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board's situation or the reasoning behind a specific move is out.
2.  **Refinement:** After that, it's about crafting the remainder into cohesive, sensible sentences. I'll remove any superfluous phrases, and ensure the resulting text flows naturally.
