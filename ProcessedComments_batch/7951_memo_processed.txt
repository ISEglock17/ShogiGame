**Processing and Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game commentary entries. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, you name it – and leave behind only the essential, insightful commentary about the board state and the moves themselves. Essentially, I'm aiming to transform these raw comments into concise, flowing explanations that are ideal for deep analysis.

My approach is straightforward: I'll go through each comment, move by move. I'll meticulously apply the defined rules to identify the unwanted information and remove it. It's a bit like surgical precision, removing extraneous elements to reveal the core truth about the game's evolution. I'm focusing on preserving the logical flow of the game's narrative, ensuring that the remaining text clearly articulates the strategic thinking behind each move and its impact on the board.
