**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these Shogi game comments. The goal is clear: strip away all the fluff – the metadata, player intros, time stamps, you know, the stuff that's not directly related to the actual moves and board state. What I'm after is the pure, distilled analysis, the kind of insights that would actually be useful for a deep dive into the game itself.

My process will be pretty straightforward: First, I'll meticulously go through each comment and use the specified rules to identify and ruthlessly eliminate any irrelevant information. This means I'll be looking for specific patterns to identify what needs to go and what needs to stay. Once that's done, I'll then smooth out what's left, making sure the remaining commentary reads naturally and makes sense in context. Finally, I'll format the output to match the required specifications – keep it consistent and easy to parse, because that's what makes for a good dataset.
