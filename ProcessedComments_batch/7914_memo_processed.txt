Essentially, I'm going through each comment, analyzing it based on the defined rules, and meticulously removing the irrelevant parts. I'm focusing on preserving the essence of the game's unfolding story in the most concise and coherent way possible. It's about data refinement, stripping away the unnecessary layers to reveal the core analytical value within the comments.
