**Processing Shogi Commentary: Refining for Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task. I need to take these Shogi game commentary datasets and really clean them up. The user, who I'm assuming is a fellow expert in the field, wants a streamlined version of the commentary, something laser-focused on the board state and the moves themselves. My job is to get rid of all the fluff – the metadata, the player introductions, the time stamps... all of that is noise to us. We need to distill these comments down to pure, insightful analysis.

The process is pretty straightforward. I'll need to meticulously go through each comment, applying the rules I've been given. First, I have to figure out, based on these rules, what needs to be cut out. Then... well, I'll figure out the best way to present the remaining information in a coherent and natural way. The goal here isn't just to *remove* information; it's to *transform* the existing commentary into something truly valuable for sophisticated game analysis.
