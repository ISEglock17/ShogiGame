No need for the metadata, player introductions, or those time stamps that clutter things up. I just want the pure essence of the game analysis: the board states and the rationale behind each move, presented in a clean, flowing narrative.

Basically, I need a good system for identifying and zapping those irrelevant bits. Rule-based deletion, that's the key. I need to define the exact criteria for what stays and what goes. I'll need to figure out the right way to determine the information that should stay and the information that should be removed to achieve this.
