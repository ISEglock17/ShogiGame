The goal is crystal clear: I want to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta-stuff, introductions, time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, distilled essence of the board and the player's reasoning behind each move.  It's about getting to the heart of the strategic thinking without distractions.  Fortunately, there's a really well-defined process to follow, which should make this relatively straightforward.
