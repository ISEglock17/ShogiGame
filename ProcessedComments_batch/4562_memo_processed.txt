They want the *real* insights, the juicy bits, the specific descriptions of the board state and the reasoning behind each move, presented in a clean, flowing narrative.  Essentially, they're asking me to transform raw, noisy data into a polished, insightful analytical resource.  This is a familiar process for me; I've done this countless times. Now, it's just a matter of applying the right techniques.
