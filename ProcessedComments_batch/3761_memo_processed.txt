**The Challenge of Cleaning Up Japanese Shogi Commentary**

Right, so the user wants me to clean up Shogi game commentary, making it presentable and focused. It sounds straightforward enough, but it requires a bit of finesse.  Essentially, I need to strip away anything irrelevant to the actual game progression and the explanations of moves. Think of it like a distilled essence of the match - just the key moments and the thought process behind them.

**My Approach**
Here's how I envision tackling this:

1.  **Iterate through each move's commentary:** I'll analyze each comment block separately.
2.  **Focus on the Core:** I'll have to identify what's essential – descriptions of board states, analyses of specific moves, and maybe some context around the strategies employed.
3.  **Eliminate the Noise:** Anything that's extraneous - general remarks, acknowledgements, side conversations, or anything not directly related to the game's development gets excised.
4.  **Rewrite for Fluency:** The challenge will be to take the remnants and rewrite them to read smoothly, almost like a narrated game analysis. Think of it as crafting a narrative that guides the expert reader through the critical points of the match.
