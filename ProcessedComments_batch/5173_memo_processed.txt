**Focusing the Comments for Sharper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm being asked to clean up the comments attached to each move in a Shogi game record. The goal is to strip away all the extraneous stuff – you know, the meta-commentary, biographical details, timing information – anything that's not directly relevant to the board state or the move itself.  I need to distill each comment down to its core: a natural, concise explanation of the position and the reasoning behind the move.  It's all about getting to the meat of the analysis, eliminating the fluff, and leaving only the insightful nuggets that contribute to a deeper understanding of the game.  And thankfully, there's a clear, well-defined set of steps and rules to guide me through this process.  I can work with this!
