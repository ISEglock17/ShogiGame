**Cleaning Up Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

My focus is laser-sharp. I want to extract the pure essence: the *why* behind each move, the strategic reasoning, the unfolding narrative of the game as it plays out on the board. This involves a surgical removal of anything that isn't directly relevant to the board state or the player's intentions. I want the remaining commentary to flow smoothly, presenting a clear and concise explanation of the tactics and strategy in play. This streamlined version will be much more valuable for detailed Shogi analysis and understanding of game dynamics.
