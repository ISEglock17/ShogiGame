**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

I want to strip out all the extraneous stuff – the metadata, player introductions, time information – all the clutter that gets in the way of a clear, concise analysis. Basically, I want to leave only the valuable parts: the actual explanations of the board position and the rationale behind each move. Think of it like a distillation process, getting to the pure essence of the strategic thought behind each play. The process is pretty straightforward, I think, as it's just a matter of applying a set of rules to identify what's irrelevant and removing it.
