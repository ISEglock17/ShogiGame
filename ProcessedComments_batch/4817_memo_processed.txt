**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tackling this task of cleaning up some game commentary for a user. It seems they've got this trove of commentary data, presumably related to Shogi games. The goal is pretty straightforward: whip the commentary into shape. We're looking to streamline it, to distill the core value.

My first thought is to strip out the fluff. All that metadata, character introductions, time stamps... that's noise. It gets in the way of what *really* matters for analysis - the actual explanations of the board state and the moves being made. I need to focus on distilling those explanations into concise, flowing sentences that a player, or an analysis program, could actually use. It's about preserving the *insight*, not the extra noise. I'll need a good process to filter that irrelevant stuff.
