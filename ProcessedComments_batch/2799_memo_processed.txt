What I'm left with should be a clean, concise, human-readable commentary specifically about the board position and the moves being played. I will carefully analyze each comment based on the defined rules and produce the reformatted comment, and of course, I'll document exactly *why* I removed what I did. This should provide excellent data for deeper analysis later on.
