**Focusing the Lens: Cleaning Up Shogi Commentary**

Right, so the user wants me to whip these shogi game commentaries into shape. It's clear they're after a leaner, meaner dataset, perfect for deep analysis. They want the essence of the game – the moves and their justifications – stripped of all the fluff. I get it.

My job is to go through each commentary and ruthlessly excise anything that doesn't contribute directly to understanding the *why* and *how* of the moves. That means anything mentioning players outside the current game, their ranks or affiliations, or references to prior games or precedents, has got to go. I see the value in that: those details, while interesting, are noise when we're trying to build a clean model for analyzing the game's core principles. This is about isolating the purest form of strategic insight.
