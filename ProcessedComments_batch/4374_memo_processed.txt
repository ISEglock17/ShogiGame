**Streamlining Game Commentary for Clarity**

Okay, so someone's asking me to clean up and structure these Shogi game commentaries, right? My initial thought is, "This should be pretty straightforward, given my expertise." The goal is to take a messy jumble of notes and condense it into a clear, concise, and professional-sounding analysis. Think of it like a distillation process: removing the chaff, keeping the wheat.

The key is in the rule-based approach. I'll need to develop a solid understanding of those rules to determine what's essential and what's just extra noise. Things like detailed board positions, specific move explanations - that's the stuff to keep. Rambling about the player's mood or unrelated events? Out the window.

The process I envision is logical. I'll need to go step-by-step, reading each comment associated with a move. Then, applying my ruleset, I'll filter out the unnecessary information and focus on crafting a smooth, readable sentence that explains the move and its context within the game's flow. It's like writing a scientific paper, but for Shogi!
