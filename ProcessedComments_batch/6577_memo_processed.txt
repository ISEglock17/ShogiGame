I want the *pure* commentary, the insightful explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each specific move, presented as if it were a natural flowing narrative. It's about distilling the essence of the analysis, the raw intellectual energy driving the game.

Now, I need to figure out how to process each individual comment, following the given rules to make sure I don't lose that vital information while simultaneously pruning the irrelevant bits.
