**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up the comments within Shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to strip away all the extraneous noise – the things that aren't directly related to the board position or the player's moves, and instead retain only those insightful explanations and analyses, presented in a fluid, readable style.  Basically, I need to make them helpful for a deep dive into the game, not just a casual read.

I'll be tackling each move's commentary individually. It's a structured process: first, I'll apply the given rules to assess and discard anything deemed irrelevant.  After that, whatever remains will need to be rephrased for naturalness – taking any fragmented notes and weaving them into coherent sentences that flow logically from one idea to the next. That way, the resulting commentary becomes a valuable tool, not just a jumble of data. I'm keen to make the resulting cleaned-up comments are truly valuable for further analysis.
