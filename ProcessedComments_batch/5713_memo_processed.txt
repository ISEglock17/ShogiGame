**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm dealing with a lot of commentary from Shogi games, right? My goal here is to refine this data. I need to take these often lengthy and unstructured comments attached to each move and boil them down to their essence. I want to isolate the pure, insightful analysis of the board state and the move itself.

Think of it like this: I'm pulling out the meat of the argument, the core strategic thinking, and discarding all the fluff. That means removing all the extraneous stuff - biographical details about the players, the timestamps, or the general "scene-setting" that's not directly related to the actual game's progress. I want a clean, focused, and ultimately useful analysis for each move. It's about distilling the commentary into its purest form.
