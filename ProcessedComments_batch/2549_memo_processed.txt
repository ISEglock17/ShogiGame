**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Deeper Analysis**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task, a familiar one: a dataset of shogi game commentary, and the goal is to refine it.  The aim here is crystal clear. I need to strip away all the extraneous information clogging up the good stuff.  Think of it like sifting through a mountain of ore to get to the pure gold.  That means removing the metadata, the player introductions, time stamps, and anything else that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the board state or the reasoning behind each move.  I want to be left with the core of the commentary – the actual insights into the game, framed in a way that's easy to digest and analyze.  This will let me focus on the tactical and strategic nuances, which is exactly what I'm interested in to get the analysis I need. The next step is to define how to do that, so I can start working on it.
