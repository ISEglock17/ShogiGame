This means a meticulous process. I have to be incredibly discerning. I need to sift through the comments, identifying and systematically removing all the extraneous fluff: the introductions of the players, the time stamps that don't contribute to understanding the game's flow, any meta-commentary that steps outside the immediate tactical or strategic considerations.

It's a process of distillation, really.  I'm taking what's potentially a narrative of a match and transforming it into a precise, focused record of thought. I'm focusing in on what *actually* contributes to insightful analysis of the position.
