**Refining Chess Game Commentary for Expert Analysis**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these chess commentary logs. The user, who's likely a seasoned player themselves, wants me to refine the comments associated with each move in a game. The goal is clear: strip away all the extraneous noise, the fluff, and get down to the pure, unadulterated chess analysis.  I need to keep only the insightful explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each move. Think of it like a distillation process - removing the impurities to reveal the concentrated essence of the game's unfolding strategy.

Essentially, I see my process in three key steps. First, I'll meticulously go through each comment, applying a set of rules – my expertise here will be crucial – to identify and remove all the irrelevant bits. This is where my understanding of what constitutes meaningful commentary in professional chess analysis will come into play. Second, I'll then take the remaining fragments and reshape them into coherent, readable sentences. The goal isn't just to *remove* information, but to *preserve* the valuable insights and make them accessible. Finally, my output will need to be structured in a way that’s logical for a detailed examination: including the move number, the cleaned-up comment itself, and a brief note on why certain elements were removed.  This structured output will allow for a much more direct understanding of the insights that are being retained and those that have been dismissed.
