**Cleaning Up the Commentary for Analysis**

Okay, so I've been asked to clean up some commentary attached to shogi game data. It's that typical situation: the raw data is filled with all sorts of things that aren't *really* relevant to the actual game analysis I want to do. We're talking metadata, player introductions, time stamps... you know, the fluff. 

The goal here is pretty straightforward: get rid of the noise and leave behind the pure gold - the actual explanations of the board state and the moves made. I want to end up with a smooth, natural-sounding narrative of the game's key moments, just the bits that are crucial for my deeper analysis of strategy and tactics.  It's about data quality, essentially, so that my models don't get sidetracked by irrelevant details.
