The goal is to isolate the *useful* bits – the explanations of the board state and the moves themselves – and present them in a coherent, natural-sounding way.  Essentially, I need to strip away all the extraneous noise that's cluttering up the analysis.  This means I need to start by understanding the rules of what *should* be removed. Then, once I have those rules nailed down, I can automate the process and transform the comments. This will make the data much more digestible and, hopefully, more insightful for further analysis.
