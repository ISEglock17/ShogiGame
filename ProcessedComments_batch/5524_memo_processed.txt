**Structuring Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a Shogi game's commentary and tasked with cleaning it up. The goal here is to get rid of all the fluff – any extraneous information that isn't directly related to the board position or the moves themselves. My expert eye needs to focus solely on the strategic heart of the game.

Here's how I'll approach this:

First, I'm going to carefully read through each comment associated with every move. This means I'll be meticulously examining each sentence or phrase connected to each turn.

Next, I'll apply the deletion rules I have in my mind, or perhaps those I am given explicitly, to identify the parts that should be removed. This includes any meta-commentary, off-topic discussions, or general observations that don't contribute to the understanding of the board.

Finally, I'll take what remains, the core of the commentary regarding the game's evolution, and reformat it for clarity. This means making sure the sentences flow, the ideas are articulated logically, and the essence of each move and its impact on the position are properly conveyed. Essentially, I'm curating the analysis, ensuring the user gets the pure, strategic meat of the game without unnecessary noise.
