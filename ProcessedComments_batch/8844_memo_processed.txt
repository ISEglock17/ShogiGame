**Cleaning Up Game Analysis Commentary**

Okay, so I'm looking at a bunch of comments that come with these Shogi game records. My goal here is to make this commentary useful for analyzing the games themselves. I need to strip out all the fluff – the metadata, introductions, maybe even the time stamps – you know, the stuff that doesn't actually help me understand the board or the moves that were made. I want to be left with the really insightful explanations of what's happening on the board and why each move was played, written in a clear and natural way. Basically, I want to convert those scattered notes into a readable game narrative.
