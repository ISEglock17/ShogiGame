I'm essentially a filter, meticulously going through each comment for every move. My objective is crystal clear: to isolate the meaningful explanations of the board state and the reasoning behind each player's decisions. I've got a set of rules – a checklist, really – to guide me in this process. These rules are crucial because they dictate exactly what to toss out and what to keep.

Basically, I'm a digital editor, but instead of words, I'm refining the flow of strategic understanding. No room for biographical details about the players, past rivalries, clock-watching, or travel information. I'm solely interested in the moves themselves and the expert perspectives on them.
