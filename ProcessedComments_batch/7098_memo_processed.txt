**Cleaning Up Those Game Comments**

Okay, so I'm looking at this data, these comments attached to shogi game records. My goal is clear: I need to clean them up. I want to strip out all the noise – the meta stuff, the introductions to the players, the time stamps... all of that. It's clutter, and it's getting in the way. What I *need* is the pure essence of the analysis, the insightful commentary on the board positions and the moves themselves, all presented in flowing, natural language. That's the key to making this data really useful. I want to get to the heart of the game commentary, without getting bogged down in the extraneous.
