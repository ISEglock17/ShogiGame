Okay, so the user wants me to clean up comments from shogi game records. Sounds straightforward enough, but the goal is crucial. They need me to strip away all the extraneous noise – you know, the meta stuff, the intros, the time stamps – and leave behind only the pure, distilled essence: the board state explanations and the actual move analysis, all rendered in grammatically sound sentences. They want *clean* data, the kind that lets you focus on the chess and not the chatter around it. I get it. This is about making the data ready for serious analysis, separating signal from noise, making it easier to see the patterns and insights. It’s like preparing a gourmet meal; you need to separate the herbs and spices for each course, just so you can actually taste them.
