**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

Okay, so I'm tasked with cleaning up these comments within Shogi game datasets. The goal here is pretty clear: I need to transform raw commentary into something useful for analyzing the games. I'm focusing on the *essence* of the analysis. So, any noise – the metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that stuff – it has to go. It's distracting and doesn't contribute to my understanding of the board states or the moves themselves.

My process will involve two primary steps. First, I have to *interpret* the comments and *identify* the information to remove according to the rules. I'll need a clear strategy to determine what constitutes "useless" information.
