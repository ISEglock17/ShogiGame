**My Approach to Refining Shogi Game Commentary**

The goal is clear: I need to strip away all the fluff and extraneous details – the introductions, the player biographies, the time stamps – and leave behind only the genuinely insightful analysis of the board state and the moves made. I need to make sure that the remaining text reads smoothly and naturally.

Basically, I will be filtering the comments based on a specific set of rules. First, I have to figure out what to get rid of, based on those rules, and delete it. Second, whatever's left, I'll turn it into proper sentences.
