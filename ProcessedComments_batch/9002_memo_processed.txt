**Refining Commentary in Shogi Data**

Okay, so I'm tackling this Shogi data cleaning task. The user wants me to go through the commentary associated with each move and clean it up. The goal is clear: strip out all the fluff that's not directly related to explaining the board position and the move itself. We're talking about removing extraneous stuff like biographical information about commentators, time stamps, and any general introductions that aren't strictly analysis. Basically, I need to isolate the "meat" of the explanation.

My approach will be straightforward:

1.  **Rule-based filtering:** I'll apply a set of rules to identify and remove the unwanted information. These rules are key to automating the process.
2.  **Natural Language Polishing:** After filtering, I'll work on smoothing out what remains into more coherent sentences.
3.  **Organized Output:** Finally, the result will be presented in a standardized format. Each move's commentary will start with `手数<N>: <整形済みのコメント>`, followed by a detailed breakdown of the removed items with their corresponding rule numbers using the `削除理由:` prefix.

The first rule, which is a big one, dictates that I remove any mention of names, ranks, or affiliations of individuals who aren't directly involved in the game or its analysis.
