**My Approach to Refining Shogi Commentary**

Okay, so I'm presented with a task: someone wants me to clean up and structure shogi game commentary, removing all the fluff and just keeping the valuable analytical stuff about the board and moves.  Essentially, they want a concise, digestible commentary, perfect for expert analysis.

Here's how I'll tackle it:

First, I'm going to take each comment individually.  Then, I'll meticulously scan each one, checking for any text that falls under the "delete" rules – anything that's not directly related to the current board state or the immediate consequences of a move.  That's key. Anything extraneous has to go.

Next, I'll apply the rules precisely to remove those identified sections. After that, I'll work on making what remains sound natural, like a good English commentary, while respecting the original intent.

Of course, the player names need to be standardized to "Sente" and "Gote" for clarity, and I have to present the information in their specific output format: Move number, the cleaned-up comment, and then a brief explanation for why certain parts were deleted.  That way, they can see my rationale.
