That means stripping out anything that's not directly related to the board position or the moves themselves. Think of it like a meticulous editor cutting away the fluff.

My plan is broken down into a few clear steps. First, I'll analyze each comment against the given rules – essentially, identifying and flagging anything that's meta-information, player introductions, time stamps, and so on. That stuff has to go. Second, once I've got a bare-bones version, I'll work on making the remaining comments flow more naturally, structuring them into cohesive, readable sentences. Finally, I'll ensure the cleaned-up output conforms to the specified formatting guidelines. It’s about taking raw data and turning it into something usable and insightful for analysis.
