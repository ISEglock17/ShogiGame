**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Right now, there's a lot of noise in there – metadata, player introductions, time stamps, all that jazz. It's distracting and doesn't help me understand the actual strategic thinking behind the moves.

What I need is a streamlined version. I want to isolate the core commentary: the explanations of board positions, the insights into the thinking behind each move. I want this to read smoothly, as a natural explanation of the game's progression. It's about distilling the essence of the analysis, removing the irrelevant fluff, and leaving behind the pure, distilled strategic insights. So basically, I'm aiming for clean, concise, and analytically rich text.
