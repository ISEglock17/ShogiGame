**Cleaning Up Game Annotations**

Okay, so I'm looking at this dataset of Shogi game annotations.  The goal here is pretty clear: I need to whip these comments into shape.  Right now, they're cluttered with all sorts of irrelevant stuff – metadata about the game, introductions to the players, timestamps, you name it.  My job is to strip all that out. I want to keep only the good stuff, the meat of the analysis: the actual explanations of the board state and the moves being played, presented in a way that flows naturally. Basically, I want to make them useful for further analysis and, dare I say, maybe even feeding into a model.  It's about distilling the essence of the commentary, leaving just the core strategic insights intact.
