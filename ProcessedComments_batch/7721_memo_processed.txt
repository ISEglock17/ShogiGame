**Cleaning Up These Awkward Comments**

Okay, so I've been tasked with cleaning up these comments attached to shogi game data. The goal, as I understand it, is to take these often rambling, stream-of-consciousness comments and distill them down to their core – the *actual* explanation of what's happening on the board.  I need to strip out all the noise: the metadata, the personal introductions of the players (which I can probably get from the game data anyway), and especially those time stamps that are utterly useless for any meaningful analysis.

Essentially, I want to create a clean, natural-sounding narrative from each comment, focusing *only* on the position and the implications of the move. That way, I can use these cleaned comments for deeper analytical work down the line.  It's about getting to the heart of the matter and removing all the fluff.
