**Focusing the Analysis: Streamlining Game Commentaries**

Okay, so I'm presented with a pretty common task in my work with shogi game data: cleaning up the commentaries. The user wants me to go through these game records and surgically remove all the noise. They want a clean, focused narrative that gets straight to the point of *the game itself*. No fluff, no extraneous details about the players' backgrounds, no time stamps, no extraneous metadata. The goal is to isolate and preserve the insightful commentary specifically related to the board state and the moves being played. I need to leave only the natural, flowing explanations of the strategic decisions and their consequences, and present them in a way that remains logically sound. Effectively, I'm transforming these records into pure analytical gold.
