**Focusing the Commentary: Streamlining Shogi Analysis**

Okay, so the user wants me to clean up these Shogi game commentaries, right? My task is to filter out all the fluff that's bogging down the really crucial information for analysis. Think of it like a distilled version of the game's narrative – all the insightful commentary on the board state and the moves, minus the distractions. We're cutting out everything that doesn't directly contribute to understanding the *tactical* and *strategic* nuances of each position.

Let's break down the rules I need to follow. The goal is a lean, mean, analysis machine.

*   **First up:** Bye-bye to anything related to people *outside* the game itself. No introductions, no titles, and definitely no reminiscing about past encounters or students. Just the game.
*   **Time's up!** Time information is a huge distraction. We need to focus on what's happening on the board, not how long it took.
*   **Irrelevant backstory:** Player profiles, their history, their records, that doesn't matter for the analysis.
*   **No meta data:** Things like the setting, weather, hotel info, etc., has no bearing on the current state of the game.
*   **No extraneous information:** Trends on play style or the tendencies of the fighters.

Basically, I'm aiming for a highly focused, direct line of thought from the commentator to the board itself. Let's get to work!
