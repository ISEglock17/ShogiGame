Then, with the core commentary laid bare, I'll work on smoothing out the remaining text, ensuring the flow makes sense and the explanations are natural. It's about preserving the *meaning* of the commentary, not just the words.  The aim is to create a resource that's directly usable for further analysis, freed from distractions.
