I want to strip out all the extraneous stuff - you know, the metadata, the introductions of the players, time stamps, all that noise. My aim is to be left with only the actual insightful analysis of the board position and the specific move itself. 

It's a two-step process, really. First, I have to apply my knowledge and the rules I've defined to identify and remove all the information that doesn't contribute to understanding the game's flow. Then, with the essential pieces remaining, I need to smoothly reconstruct the raw analysis into a coherent, natural-sounding explanation of what's happening on the board.
